#  1. Do you know how to cook? (If yes, how did you learn it; if no, why?)
> Words may be used:
> teach
> effort
> difficult
> lazy
> dangerous
> 
I do **know a little about cooking**, though I’m far from being an expert. I learned it mainly from my mom—she taught me the basics like boiling eggs, frying vegetables, and making simple soups when I was a teenager. At first, it felt difficult: I burned the rice, over-salted the dishes, and even once almost cut my finger while chopping onions. It took a lot of effort to get the hang of controlling heat and seasoning.  
我确实对烹饪有所了解，尽管我远非专家。我主要是从我妈妈那里学到的——在我十几岁的时候，她教我煮鸡蛋、炒蔬菜和做简单的汤等基础知识。起初，我觉得很难：我把米饭烧了，把盘子撒了太多盐，甚至有一次在切洋葱的时候差点割破手指。要掌握控制热量和调味的诀窍需要付出很多努力。  

I used to be **a bit lazy**, avoiding cooking by ordering takeout, but as I grew older, I realized relying on others wasn’t a long-term solution. Also, some cooking steps do feel dangerous, like deep-frying or handling hot oil, but with practice, I learned to be careful. Now, I can make a few decent meals, thanks to my mom’s patience and my own small efforts to improve.
我过去有点懒，不点外卖做饭，但随着年龄的增长，我意识到依靠别人并不是一个长期的解决方案。此外，有些烹饪步骤确实很危险，比如油炸或处理热油，但通过练习，我学会了小心。现在，我可以做几顿像样的饭了，这要归功于我妈妈的耐心和我自己的小小努力。

#  2. Do you prefer to eat at home or eat out?
> Words may be used:
> healthy
> cheap/expensive
> convenient
> tasty
> time-saving

I prefer eating at home, and there are several reasons for that. First, home-cooked meals are usually healthier—you can control the amount of oil, salt, and sugar, which is hard to do when eating out where dishes often have excessive seasoning.  
我更喜欢在家吃饭，这有几个原因。首先，家常菜通常更健康——你可以控制油、盐和糖的量，这在外出就餐时很难做到，因为外出就餐的菜肴往往有过多的调味料。  
 

Second, it’s much cheaper. Eating out regularly, especially at restaurants, can be expensive, while cooking at home with ingredients from the market saves a lot of money over time.   
其次，它便宜得多。经常外出就餐，尤其是在餐馆，可能会很贵，而在家里用市场上的食材烹饪会随着时间的推移节省很多钱。  

Admittedly, eating out is more convenient and time-saving—you don’t have to shop for ingredients or clean up. And some restaurants do serve incredibly tasty dishes that are hard to replicate at home. But for me, the benefits of healthy, affordable meals at home outweigh the convenience of eating out.
诚然，外出就餐更方便、更省时——你不必购买食材或打扫卫生。有些餐馆确实提供令人难以置信的美味菜肴，在家里很难复制。但对我来说，在家吃健康、负担得起的饭菜的好处超过了外出就餐的便利性。

#  3. Do you eat breakfast every day? Why or why not?
> Words may be used:
> necessary
> importance
> provide
> rush
> get up
Yes, I eat breakfast every day because I deeply understand its importance. It’s widely said that breakfast is the most necessary meal of the day, and I couldn’t agree more.  
是的，我每天吃早餐，因为我深刻理解它的重要性。人们普遍认为早餐是一天中最必要的一餐，我完全同意。  

A good breakfast can provide enough energy to kick-start my day, helping me stay focused and productive in the morning. I used to skip it occasionally when I got up late and was in a rush to go to work or school. But I soon realized that without breakfast, I’d feel sluggish by mid-morning, unable to concentrate on tasks.  

一顿丰盛的早餐可以提供足够的能量来开始我的一天，帮助我在早上保持专注和高效。过去，当我起床晚了，急着去上班或上学时，我偶尔会跳过它。但我很快意识到，如果不吃早餐，到上午三点，我会感到迟钝，无法集中精力完成任务。  

Now, I make it a habit to get up 10 minutes earlier to prepare a simple breakfast—like oatmeal, a boiled egg, or a sandwich. It doesn’t take much time, but it ensures I start the day on the right foot. Skipping it just isn’t worth the drop in energy and focus.
现在，我养成了早起10分钟准备简单早餐的习惯——比如燕麦片、煮鸡蛋或三明治。这不需要太多时间，但它确保我以正确的方式开始新的一天。跳过它不值得精力和注意力的下降。

#  4. Why do some people like to cook?
> Words may be used:
> enjoyable
> process
> show love
> praise
> calm
Some people enjoy cooking for various reasons, and many find the process itself enjoyable. From selecting fresh ingredients to chopping, stirring, and seasoning, each step allows them to focus on the task at hand, which can be surprisingly calm—like a form of meditation that eases stress.  
有些人出于各种原因喜欢烹饪，许多人发现这个过程本身很有趣。从选择新鲜食材到切碎、搅拌和调味，每一步都让他们专注于手头的任务，这可能会令人惊讶地平静——就像一种缓解压力的冥想。  

Cooking also gives people a chance to show love. Preparing a meal for family or friends is a heartfelt way to care for others, and seeing their smiles when they taste the food brings great satisfaction. Additionally, receiving praise for a well-cooked dish boosts their confidence, making the effort feel worthwhile.  

烹饪也给了人们表达爱意的机会。为家人或朋友准备一顿饭是一种发自内心的关心他人的方式，看到他们品尝食物时的笑容会带来极大的满足感。此外，对一道精心烹制的菜肴的赞扬会增强他们的信心，让他们觉得努力是值得的。  

For these reasons, cooking isn’t just about making food—it’s a blend of creativity, emotion, and fulfillment that draws people in.

出于这些原因，烹饪不仅仅是制作食物——它是创造力、情感和满足感的融合，吸引着人们。

#  5. Do you think it is important to know how to cook well?
> Words may be used:
> basic
> skill
> independent
> take care of
> rely on
> 
Yes, I think knowing how to cook well is an important basic skill, and its value goes beyond just preparing food.  
是的，我认为知道如何烹饪是一项重要的基本技能，它的价值不仅仅是准备食物。  

First, it’s closely tied to being independent. When you can cook, you don’t have to rely on takeout, restaurants, or others to feed yourself—you can handle meals on your own, which is crucial for daily life, whether living alone or managing a busy schedule.  

首先，这与独立密切相关。当你会做饭时，你不必依靠外卖、餐馆或其他人来养活自己——你可以自己做饭，这对日常生活至关重要，无论是独自生活还是管理繁忙的日程。  

Second, cooking well allows you to better take care of yourself and the people around you. You can control the ingredients, adjust flavors to suit dietary needs, and ensure meals are healthy and nutritious. For example, you can avoid excessive oil or salt, which is hard to do when depending on outside food.  

其次，烹饪得当可以让你更好地照顾自己和周围的人。你可以控制食材，调整口味以满足饮食需求，并确保饭菜健康营养。例如，你可以避免摄入过多的油或盐，这在依赖外界食物时很难做到。  

In short, cooking is more than a practical skill; it’s a way to gain independence and show care, making it truly important to master.
简而言之，烹饪不仅仅是一种实用技能；这是一种获得独立和关心的方式，使掌握它变得真正重要。
