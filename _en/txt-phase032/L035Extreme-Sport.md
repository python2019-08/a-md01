# 1. Why do people enjoy extreme sports?
> Words may be used:
> boring
> different
> exciting
> experience
> challenge
> 
People are drawn to extreme sports for a mix of emotional, psychological, and experiential reasons, often rooted in a desire to break from the ordinary.  
  人们被极限运动吸引是出于情感、心理和体验的原因，往往植根于打破平凡的欲望。
One key driver is the thrill of doing something **exciting**. Unlike routine activities that might feel **boring**, extreme sports———whether it’s skydiving, rock climbing, or big-wave surfing----deliver intense adrenaline[英/əˈdrenəlɪn/美/əˈdrenəlɪn/n.肾上腺素] rushes. The high-stakes nature of these sports, where split-second decisions matter, creates a sense of excitement that’s hard to replicate in daily life.  
  一个关键的驱动力是做一些令人兴奋的事情的快感。与可能感觉**无聊**的常规活动不同，极限运动——无论是跳伞、攀岩还是大浪冲浪——都会带来强烈的肾上腺素激增。这些运动的高风险性质，即瞬间的决定很重要，创造了一种在日常生活中难以复制的兴奋感。  
Many also seek the **challenge** they present. Pushing physical and mental limits—mastering a difficult trick, scaling a steep cliff, or navigating rough terrain—brings a profound sense of accomplishment. Overcoming these challenges builds resilience[英/rɪˈzɪliəns/美/rɪˈzɪliəns/] and confidence, making the effort feel deeply rewarding.  
  许多人还寻求他们提出的**挑战**。突破身心极限——掌握一个困难的技巧、攀登陡峭的悬崖或在崎岖的地形上航行——会带来深刻的成就感。克服这些挑战可以建立韧性和信心，使努力感到非常有回报。 
Extreme sports also offer a chance to have a **different** kind of **experience**. They often take place in dramatic, unconventional settings—remote mountains, vast oceans, or urban rooftops—allowing participants to connect with nature or explore environments they’d never encounter otherwise. This novelty makes the activity feel unique and memorable.  
  极限运动也提供了一个获得**不同**体验**的机会。它们通常发生在戏剧性的、非传统的环境中——偏远的山脉、广阔的海洋或城市屋顶——让参与者与自然联系或探索他们从未遇到过的环境。这种新颖性让活动感觉独特而难忘。 
Ultimately, for many, extreme sports are a way to escape monotony, test themselves, and feel truly alive through excitement, challenge, and one-of-a-kind experiences.
  最终，对许多人来说，极限运动是一种摆脱单调、考验自己、通过兴奋、挑战和独一无二的体验感受真正活力的方式。

# 2. What do you think about extreme sports? 
> Words may be used:
> dangerous
> prepare
> learn
> valuable
> life
> 
Extreme sports are a complex blend of thrill and risk, and my perspective on them is nuanced—they can be both rewarding and perilous, depending on how they’re approached.  
极限运动是刺激和风险的复杂结合，我对它们的看法是微妙的——它们既有回报也有危险，这取决于人们如何对待它们。  
First, it’s undeniable that extreme sports are **dangerous**. Activities like base jumping, white-water rafting in Class V rapids, or free solo climbing involve inherent risks of injury or even loss of **life** if things go wrong. This danger is part of what draws some people, but it also demands respect and caution.  
  首先，不可否认的是，极限运动是危险的。跳台、V级急流中的白水漂流或自由单人攀岩等活动都存在固有的受伤风险，如果出了问题，甚至可能导致**人死亡**。这种危险是吸引一些人的部分原因，但也需要尊重和谨慎。  
However, when approached with proper **preparedness**, they can offer **valuable** lessons and growth. Those who engage in extreme sports often spend months or years learning skills: studying terrain, practicing techniques, and understanding safety protocols. This process of **learning** builds discipline, focus, and problem-solving abilities—traits that extend beyond the sport itself and enrich daily life.  
  然而，当他们以适当的**准备**接近时，他们可以提供**宝贵的**经验教训和成长。从事极限运动的人通常会花几个月或几年的时间学习技能：研究地形、练习技术和理解安全协议。这种学习过程建立了纪律、专注力和解决问题的能力，这些特质不仅限于运动本身，还丰富了日常生活。
Moreover, the sense of achievement from mastering a challenging extreme sport can be deeply fulfilling, teaching participants about their own limits and resilience. It’s a reminder that growth often comes from stepping outside comfort zones, as long as the risks are managed responsibly.  
  此外，掌握一项具有挑战性的极限运动的成就感可以让人深感满足，让参与者了解自己的局限性和韧性。这提醒我们，只要风险得到负责任的管理，增长往往来自走出舒适区。  
In short, extreme sports are not inherently good or bad. Their value lies in the balance between the thrill of the challenge, the commitment to learning and preparation, and the awareness of the risks to life and well-being.
  简而言之，极限运动本身并没有好坏之分。它们的价值在于挑战的刺激、对学习和准备的承诺以及对生命和幸福风险的认识之间的平衡。

# 3. Should young people be allowed to do extreme sports?
> Words may be used:
> lost interest
> traditional sports
> risk
> injure
> mature
Whether young people should be allowed to engage in extreme sports is a nuanced issue, balancing their desire for adventure with concerns about safety and maturity.  
  是否应该允许年轻人参加极限运动是一个微妙的问题，在他们对冒险的渴望与对安全和成熟的担忧之间取得平衡。 
First, **risk** is a central consideration. Extreme sports carry a high potential for serious injury or worse, and young people—whose brains and decision-making abilities are still developing—may underestimate these dangers. Their tendency to act impulsively or overestimate their skills could increase the likelihood of being **injured**, making unrestricted participation risky.  
  首先，**风险**是一个核心考虑因素。极限运动极有可能造成严重伤害或更严重的伤害，而大脑和决策能力仍在发展的年轻人可能会低估这些危险。他们冲动行事或高估自己技能的倾向可能会增加受伤的可能性，使无限制的参与变得有风险。 
However, outright prohibition may not be the answer. Many young people are drawn to extreme sports as a break from **traditional sports**, which they might find repetitive or unexciting. Banning them could lead to frustration or even secret, unsupervised participation----posing greater risks. Instead, guided involvement, with proper training and adult supervision, can help mitigate dangers while allowing them to explore their interests.  
  然而，彻底禁止可能不是答案。许多年轻人被极限运动所吸引，作为对**传统运动**的突破，他们可能会发现这些运动重复或乏味。禁止他们可能会导致沮丧，甚至是秘密、无监督的参与，从而带来更大的风险。相反，在适当的培训和成人监督下，有指导的参与可以帮助减轻危险，同时让他们探索自己的兴趣。  
**Mature** judgment is key here. Younger teens, for example, may lack the emotional regulation to handle high-pressure situations, while older youth might be better equipped to assess risks. It’s also important to ensure they don’t **lost interest** in other activities; extreme sports should complement, not replace, a balanced lifestyle that includes physical, academic, and social growth.  
  **成熟的**判断是关键。例如，年轻的青少年可能缺乏应对高压情况的情绪调节，而年长的青少年可能更有能力评估风险。确保他们不会对其他活动失去兴趣也很重要；极限运动应该补充而不是取代一种平衡的生活方式，包括身体、学业和社会成长。  
In summary, young people can engage in extreme sports, but with safeguards: age-appropriate assessments, rigorous training, and supervision. This approach respects their desire for challenge while prioritizing their safety and development. 
  总之，年轻人可以参加极限运动，但要有保障：适龄评估、严格训练和监督。这种方法尊重他们对挑战的渴望，同时优先考虑他们的安全和发展。

# 4. Should extreme sports be banned?
> Words may be used:
> injury/death
> freedom
> choose
> encourage
> decrease
The question of whether extreme sports should be banned is a complex one, as it balances concerns about safety with principles of personal freedom. Here’s a breakdown of key considerations:  
    极限运动是否应该被禁止的问题是一个复杂的问题，因为它平衡了对安全的担忧和个人自由的原则。以下是关键考虑因素的细分：
First, the risks of **injury** or even **death** are undeniable. Extreme sports inherently involve high-stakes scenarios—from unforeseen weather changes during mountain climbing to equipment failures in skydiving—that can lead to tragic outcomes. This reality raises valid concerns about public safety and the potential burden on emergency services.  
    首先，受伤甚至死亡的风险是不可否认的。极限运动本质上涉及高风险的情况——从登山过程中不可预见的天气变化到跳伞中的设备故障——这可能会导致悲剧性的结果。这一现实引发了人们对公共安全和紧急服务潜在负担的合理担忧。 
However, banning extreme sports would infringe on individuals’ **freedom** to **choose** how they pursue fulfillment. Many participants engage in these sports voluntarily, after rigorous training and with a clear understanding of the risks. For them, extreme sports are not reckless but a way to test limits, build resilience, or connect with nature—experiences that hold deep personal meaning.  
    然而，禁止极限运动会侵犯个人**选择**如何追求成就感的**自由**。许多参与者在经过严格训练并清楚地了解风险后，自愿参加这些运动。对他们来说，极限运动不是鲁莽的，而是一种测试极限、建立韧性或与自然联系的方式——这些经历具有深刻的个人意义。 
Instead of an outright ban, a more balanced approach might focus on regulation to **decrease** harm: enforcing safety standards, requiring proper training and certification, and ensuring access to emergency protocols. This way, we respect people’s autonomy while mitigating unnecessary risks.  
    与其彻底禁止，不如采取更平衡的方法，将重点放在监管上，以减少伤害：执行安全标准，要求适当的培训和认证，并确保获得应急协议。这样，我们尊重人们的自主权，同时减少不必要的风险。  
Moreover, banning extreme sports could stifle a culture of adventure that often **encourages** discipline, courage, and innovation—qualities that extend beyond the sports themselves.  
    此外，禁止极限运动可能会扼杀一种冒险文化，这种文化往往鼓励纪律、勇气和创新——这些品质超越了运动本身。 
In short, extreme sports should not be banned. The better path is to protect personal freedom while implementing measures to reduce preventable injury and death, allowing individuals to choose their own adventures responsibly. 
    简而言之，极限运动不应该被禁止。更好的途径是保护个人自由，同时采取措施减少可预防的伤亡，让个人负责任地选择自己的冒险。

# 5. What motivates athletes to participate in extreme sports?
> Words may be used:
> money
> fame
> remember
> prove
> ability

Athletes are motivated to participate in extreme sports by a mix of personal, emotional, and sometimes external drivers, each tied to the unique intensity of these activities.  
    运动员参加极限运动的动力来自个人、情感，有时还有外部因素，每种因素都与这些活动的独特强度有关。
One practical motivator is **money** and **fame**. Top extreme sports athletes often secure sponsorships, prize money, or media deals—especially in high-profile events like the X Games. The visibility of these sports, with their dramatic stunts and viral moments, can catapult athletes to public attention, making fame a tangible goal.  
    一个实际的激励因素是**金钱**和**名声**。顶尖极限运动运动员通常会获得赞助、奖金或媒体交易，尤其是在X Games等备受瞩目的赛事中。这些运动的知名度，以及它们戏剧性的特技和病毒式的时刻，可以让运动员引起公众的关注，使成名成为一个切实的目标。  
Yet, deeper personal motivations often play a larger role. Many athletes seek to **prove** something—whether to themselves, peers, or critics. This might involve showing they can overcome fear, master a skill once thought impossible, or push past perceived limits. For example, a free climber scaling a sheer cliff without ropes may aim to prove their mental toughness and technical precision.  
    然而，更深层次的个人动机往往起着更大的作用。许多运动员试图证明一些东西——无论是对自己、同龄人还是批评者。这可能涉及展示他们可以克服恐惧，掌握一项曾经被认为不可能的技能，或者突破感知的极限。例如，一个没有绳索攀爬陡峭悬崖的自由登山者可能会试图证明他们的心理韧性和技术精度。  
Athletes also thrive on testing and showcasing their **ability**. Extreme sports demand exceptional physical control, quick decision-making, and specialized skills, and excelling in them is a powerful validation of one’s talent and dedication. Perfecting a tricky surf maneuver or nailing a base jump landing becomes a way to celebrate their hard-earned expertise.  
    运动员也在测试和展示他们的**能力**中茁壮成长。极限运动需要卓越的身体控制、快速决策和专业技能，而在这些方面表现出色是对一个人天赋和奉献精神的有力证明。完善一个棘手的冲浪动作或固定一个基础跳跃着陆成为庆祝他们来之不易的专业知识的一种方式。  
Finally, there’s the desire to create moments worth **remembering**. The adrenaline, the challenge, and the uniqueness of extreme sports often lead to unforgettable experiences—moments that feel more vivid and meaningful than everyday life. For many athletes, these memories, and the sense of living fully, are motivation enough.  
    最后，还有创造值得**记住**的时刻的愿望。极限运动的肾上腺素、挑战和独特性往往会带来难忘的经历——比日常生活更生动、更有意义的时刻。对于许多运动员来说，这些记忆和充实的生活感足以激励他们。  
In short, while money and fame can fuel participation, the core motivations often lie in proving oneself, celebrating ability, and creating lasting, impactful memories.
    简而言之，虽然金钱和名誉可以促进参与，但核心动机往往在于证明自己、庆祝能力和创造持久、有影响力的记忆。
