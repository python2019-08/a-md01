I have a mixed view on video games—whether I like them depends on how they’re played.  
我对电子游戏的看法不一——我是否喜欢它们取决于它们是如何玩的。  

On the positive side, some games can be a great way to relax after a busy day. Immersing myself in a virtual world, solving puzzles, or exploring landscapes helps me unwind. They’re also good for passing the time, especially during long waits or lazy weekends. Additionally, many games are challenging, requiring strategy or quick reflexes, which can feel rewarding when you overcome a tough level—it’s almost like exercising your brain, and in that sense, they can be helpful.  

从积极的一面来看，一些游戏可以成为忙碌一天后放松的好方法。沉浸在虚拟世界中，解决谜题或探索风景可以帮助我放松。它们也有助于打发时间，尤其是在漫长的等待或懒散的周末。此外，许多游戏都具有挑战性，需要策略或快速反应，当你克服一个艰难的水平时，这会让你感到有收获——这几乎就像锻炼你的大脑，从这个意义上说，它们可能会有所帮助。  

However, I’m aware that excessive gaming can be a waste of time. Spending hours mindlessly grinding or losing track of responsibilities because of games defeats their purpose. So, I enjoy video games in moderation—they’re fun as a hobby, but I make sure they don’t take over other important parts of life.

然而，我意识到过度游戏可能是浪费时间。因为游戏而花费数小时无意识地磨蹭或忘记责任，会破坏他们的目的。所以，我适度地喜欢电子游戏——它们作为一种爱好很有趣，但我确保它们不会占据生活的其他重要部分。

#  2. What are the causes of video game addiction (上瘾) 
> Words may be used:
> designed
> make a profit
> reward 
>  satisfaction 
> reallife
> 
Video game addiction can stem from several interconnected factors, many of which are tied to how games are crafted and the emotional needs they temporarily fulfill.  
电子游戏成瘾可能源于几个相互关联的因素，其中许多因素与游戏的制作方式以及它们暂时满足的情感需求有关。  

First, modern video games are often carefully designed to keep players engaged. Developers use techniques like frequent in-game rewards—such as leveling up, unlocking new items, or completing challenges—to create a cycle of anticipation and satisfaction. This makes players eager to keep playing, as each small achievement triggers a sense of accomplishment that’s hard to resist.  

首先，现代电子游戏通常经过精心设计，以保持玩家的参与度。开发人员使用频繁的游戏奖励等技术，如升级、解锁新物品或完成挑战，来创造一个期待和满足的循环。这让玩家渴望继续玩下去，因为每一个小成就都会引发一种难以抗拒的成就感。  

Second, the profit motive drives many game companies to maximize playtime. They may introduce features like daily missions, limited-time events, or pay-to-win mechanics, which encourage players to log in regularly or even spend money, indirectly fostering compulsive behavior.  

其次，利润动机驱使许多游戏公司最大化游戏时间。他们可能会引入日常任务、限时活动或付费获胜机制等功能，鼓励玩家定期登录甚至花钱，间接培养强迫行为。  

Another key cause is the contrast with real life. For some people, real-life challenges—like stress, loneliness, or a lack of achievement—make virtual worlds more appealing. In games, they can easily gain recognition, build social connections, or escape from daily pressures, leading them to rely excessively on games to feel satisfied, eventually developing an addiction.  

另一个关键原因是与现实生活的对比。对一些人来说，现实生活中的挑战——比如压力、孤独或缺乏成就——使虚拟世界更具吸引力。在游戏中，他们可以很容易地获得认可，建立社交关系，或逃避日常压力，导致他们过度依赖游戏来获得满足感，最终形成成瘾。  

In short, the intentional design of games, profit-driven strategies, and the emotional gaps in real life together contribute to video game addiction.

简而言之，游戏的有意设计、利润驱动的策略和现实生活中的情感差距共同导致了电子游戏成瘾。

# 3. Do violent video games make people violent?
> Words may be used:
> stress
> allow
> control oneself 
> hurt 
> copy 
Whether violent video games make people violent is not a simple yes-or-no question—it depends on various factors, including individual traits and context.  
暴力电子游戏是否会让人变得暴力并不是一个简单的是非问题——它取决于各种因素，包括个人特征和背景。  

Some argue that violent games might influence certain people, especially those who struggle to control themselves, to copy aggressive behaviors. For these individuals, repeated exposure to virtual violence (where hurting others is often part of the gameplay) could blur the line between fantasy and reality, making them more likely to mimic what they see in real life.  

一些人认为，暴力游戏可能会影响某些人，尤其是那些难以控制自己的人，模仿攻击性行为。对于这些人来说，反复接触虚拟暴力（伤害他人通常是游戏的一部分）可能会模糊幻想和现实之间的界限，使他们更有可能模仿他们在现实生活中看到的东西。  

However, many experts emphasize that most people can distinguish between virtual and real-world actions. Violent games often allow players to release stress in a controlled, fictional environment—shouting or acting aggressively in the game can ease tension, which may *reduce* the urge to act violently in real life. What truly matters is a person’s ability to control oneself: those with strong self-regulation understand that virtual violence is not a license to harm others.  

然而，许多专家强调，大多数人都能区分虚拟和现实世界的行为。暴力游戏通常允许玩家在受控的虚构环境中释放压力——在游戏中大喊大叫或表现得咄咄逼人可以缓解紧张情绪，这可能会减少在现实生活中采取暴力行为的冲动。真正重要的是一个人控制自己的能力：那些自我调节能力很强的人明白，虚拟暴力不是伤害他人的许可证。  

In short, violent video games alone do not make people violent. The key lies in whether individuals can separate fantasy from reality and maintain self-control.

简而言之，暴力电子游戏本身并不能让人变得暴力。关键在于个人能否将幻想与现实分开，保持自我控制。

#  4. How does playing video games affect sleep?
> Words may be used:
> excited 
>  fall asleep 
> reduce
> quality 
> awake
>  
Playing video games can have notable effects on sleep, often disrupting both its quantity and quality.  
玩电子游戏会对睡眠产生显著影响，通常会扰乱睡眠的数量和质量。  

First, many games are designed to be engaging and stimulating, which can leave players feeling excited long after they stop playing. This heightened state of arousal makes it harder to calm down, delaying the ability to fall asleep. For example, intense gameplay—like competitive matches or cliffhanger story moments—triggers the release of adrenaline, keeping the mind active and making it difficult to transition into a restful state.  
首先，许多游戏的设计都是为了吸引人和刺激人，这可能会让玩家在停止玩游戏后很长一段时间都感到兴奋。这种高度的觉醒状态使人更难冷静下来，推迟了入睡的能力。例如，激烈的游戏玩法——比如竞技比赛或扣人心弦的故事时刻——会引发肾上腺素的释放，使大脑保持活跃，并使其难以过渡到宁静的状态。  

Second, excessive gaming can reduce total sleep time. Players may lose track of time, staying up late to finish a level or continue a session, cutting into hours that should be spent sleeping. Even if they manage to fall asleep, the overstimulation from the game can lower sleep quality: the brain may remain partially awake, leading to lighter, more fragmented sleep that leaves them feeling unrested the next day.  
其次，过度游戏会减少总睡眠时间。玩家可能会忘记时间，熬夜完成一个关卡或继续一个会话，从而减少应该用来睡觉的时间。即使他们设法入睡，游戏的过度刺激也会降低睡眠质量：大脑可能保持部分清醒，导致更轻、更零碎的睡眠，让他们第二天感觉没有休息。  

In short, while gaming can be fun, its stimulating nature often interferes with falling asleep, reduces sleep duration, and harms overall sleep quality.
简而言之，虽然游戏可以很有趣，但它的刺激性往往会干扰入睡，缩短睡眠时间，并损害整体睡眠质量。

#  5. What are the benefits of playing video games for children?
> Words may be used:
> follow the rules 
> solve
> think 
> practice 
> social activity     
Playing video games can offer several benefits for children when enjoyed in moderation, contributing to their cognitive and social development.  
适度玩电子游戏可以为孩子们带来多种好处，有助于他们的认知和社交发展。  

First, many games require children to follow the rules strictly to progress. Whether it’s adhering to gameplay mechanics, completing quests within set parameters, or cooperating with teammates, this helps them understand the importance of structure and discipline—skills that can translate to real-life situations like following classroom rules.  
首先，许多游戏要求孩子们严格遵守规则才能进步。无论是遵循游戏机制、在设定参数内完成任务，还是与队友合作，这都有助于他们理解结构和纪律的重要性——这些技能可以转化为现实生活中的情况，比如遵守课堂规则。  

Second, gaming often encourages children to solve problems. From figuring out puzzles in adventure games to strategizing in multiplayer battles, they learn to analyze challenges, think critically, and experiment with different solutions. This process sharpens their problem-solving abilities and fosters flexible thinking.  
其次，游戏往往鼓励孩子们解决问题。从在冒险游戏中解决谜题到在多人战斗中制定战略，他们学会了分析挑战、批判性思考和尝试不同的解决方案。这个过程提高了他们解决问题的能力，培养了灵活的思维。  

Additionally, certain games serve as a social activity, especially multiplayer ones. Children practice communicating with peers, collaborating toward a common goal, or resolving in-game conflicts, which helps build teamwork and social skills.  
此外，某些游戏是一种社交活动，尤其是多人游戏。孩子们练习与同龄人沟通、为共同目标合作或解决游戏中的冲突，这有助于培养团队合作和社交技能。  

In these ways, well-chosen video games can be more than just entertainment—they can support children in developing important cognitive and social competencies.
通过这些方式，精心选择的电子游戏不仅可以娱乐，还可以支持孩子们发展重要的认知和社交能力。
