# 1. How do you define beauty?
> Words may be used:
> appearance
> outside/inside
> selfish/greedy/dishonest
> confident/optimistic/kind/caring
> heart
> 
Beauty is a multifaceted concept that transcends surface-level impressions, weaving together elements of appearance, character, and essence.  
美是一个多方面的概念，它超越了表面的印象，将外观、性格和本质的元素编织在一起。  

On one hand, **appearance** plays a role—symmetry, grace, or unique features can catch the eye, creating an initial sense of allure. But true beauty runs deeper, rooted in the balance between what lies **outside** and **inside**. A person with striking looks may lose their luster if their actions reveal selfishness, greed, or dishonesty; these traits corrupt any external charm, leaving a hollow impression.  
一方面，**外表**起着重要作用——对称、优雅或独特的特征可以吸引眼球，创造出最初的吸引力。但真正的美是更深层次的，植根于**外在**和**内在**之间的平衡。一个长相出众的人，如果他们的行为表现出自私、贪婪或不诚实，可能会失去光彩；这些特质会破坏任何外在的魅力，留下空洞的印象。  


Conversely, qualities like being **confident** (without arrogance), **optimistic** (even in hardship), **kind**, and **caring** illuminate beauty from within. A kind word to a stranger, empathy for others’ struggles, or the courage to stay true to one’s values—these actions reflect a **heart** that is rich and generous. Such inner qualities radiate outward, making even the simplest gestures feel beautiful.  
相反，像**自信**（没有傲慢）、**乐观**（即使在困难中）、**善良**和**关心**这样的品质会从内心照亮美丽。对陌生人说一句好话，同情他人的挣扎，或者有勇气忠于自己的价值观——这些行为反映了一颗丰富而慷慨的**心。这种内在品质向外辐射，即使是最简单的手势也会让人感觉很美。  

In the end, beauty is not about perfection, but about authenticity: a harmony where outer presence aligns with an inner life that chooses goodness over greed, honesty over deceit, and compassion over self-interest. It is the glow of a heart that strives to lift others, making both the person and the world around them more radiant.

最后，美不是关于完美，而是关于真实性：一种和谐，在这种和谐中，外在的存在与内在的生活相一致，内在的生活选择善良而不是贪婪，诚实而不是欺骗，同情而不是自私。这是一颗努力提升他人的心的光芒，使他们周围的人和世界更加容光焕发。

# 2. What are the advantages of being good-looking?
> Words may be used:
> likely 
> trust/believe
> free
> job 
> popular
> 
Being good-looking can bring several advantages in various aspects of life, though these benefits are often tied to social perceptions rather than inherent abilities.  
长相好看可以在生活的各个方面带来几个好处，尽管这些好处往往与社会观念有关，而不是与内在能力有关。  

First, good-looking people are more likely to be perceived positively in social interactions. Others may trust or believe them more easily, assuming they are friendly, competent, or reliable based solely on appearance—a phenomenon often called the "halo effect." This can make forming relationships smoother and help them feel more confident in social settings.  
首先，长相好看的人在社交互动中更有可能被正面看待。其他人可能更容易信任或相信他们，仅仅根据外表就假设他们是友好的、有能力的或可靠的——这种现象通常被称为“光环效应”。这可以使建立关系更顺利，并帮助他们在社交环境中更有信心。  

Second, they may have an edge in certain job markets, especially roles that involve frequent face-to-face interactions, such as customer service, sales, or entertainment. Employers might unconsciously favor attractive candidates, assuming they’ll better engage clients or represent the company well, increasing their chances of landing such positions.  
其次，他们可能在某些就业市场上具有优势，尤其是涉及频繁面对面互动的角色，如客户服务、销售或娱乐。雇主可能会无意识地青睐有吸引力的候选人，假设他们会更好地吸引客户或很好地代表公司，从而增加他们获得这些职位的机会。  
 
Additionally, good-looking individuals often tend to be more popular. They may receive more attention, invitations to social events, or opportunities to connect with others, which can enhance their social networks and sense of belonging.  
此外，长相好看的人往往更受欢迎。他们可能会得到更多的关注、社交活动的邀请或与他人联系的机会，这可以增强他们的社交网络和归属感。  

In some cases, they might even get small "perks," like free upgrades, discounts, or leniency in minor situations, as others may be more inclined to go out of their way to assist them.  
在某些情况下，他们甚至可能获得一些小的“福利”，比如免费升级、折扣或在轻微情况下的宽大处理，因为其他人可能更倾向于不遗余力地帮助他们。  

Of course, these advantages are not universal, and true success still depends on skills and character. But physical attractiveness can open doors that might be harder to access for others.
当然，这些优势并不是普遍的，真正的成功仍然取决于技能和性格。但外表的吸引力可以打开其他人可能更难进入的大门。

# 3. Why do women wear makeup (化妆) ?
> Words may be used:
> polite 
> confidence
> fun 
> younger 
> attractive
Women wear makeup for a variety of reasons, often tied to personal, social, or emotional needs.  
女性化妆的原因多种多样，通常与个人、社会或情感需求有关。  

One key reason is to boost confidence. Applying makeup can help women feel more put-together, allowing them to face daily interactions with greater self-assurance—whether in professional settings or social gatherings.  
一个关键原因是增强信心。化妆可以帮助女性感到更加团结，让她们在面对日常互动时更加自信——无论是在专业场合还是社交聚会中。  

Many also use makeup to enhance their attractiveness. Subtle touches like mascara or lipstick can highlight features, while more elaborate looks might help them feel more appealing, which can positively influence how they perceive themselves and how others respond to them.  
许多人还使用化妆品来增强自己的吸引力。睫毛膏或口红等微妙的触感可以突出特征，而更精致的外表可能会帮助他们感觉更具吸引力，这可以积极影响他们对自己的看法以及他人对他们的反应。  

In some contexts, wearing makeup is seen as a polite gesture, especially in formal situations or workplaces where a polished appearance is expected. It signals care for one’s presentation, showing respect for the occasion or the people around them.  
在某些情况下，化妆被视为一种礼貌的姿态，尤其是在正式场合或需要优雅外表的工作场所。它表示关心自己的演讲，尊重场合或周围的人。  

Additionally, makeup can be a fun form of self-expression. Experimenting with different colors, styles, or trends allows women to play with their appearance, turning routine into a creative activity.  
此外，化妆可以成为一种有趣的自我表达方式。尝试不同的颜色、风格或趋势可以让女性玩弄自己的外表，将日常生活变成一种创造性的活动。  

For others, makeup may help create the illusion of looking younger, which can align with personal preferences or societal ideals of beauty, further contributing to their sense of well-being.  
对于其他人来说，化妆可能有助于创造看起来更年轻的错觉，这可以与个人偏好或社会对美的理想相一致，进一步有助于他们的幸福感。  

Ultimately, the reasons are deeply personal, blending confidence, social norms, creativity, and individual preferences.
归根结底，原因是个人的，混合了自信、社会规范、创造力和个人偏好。

# 4. What do you think about plastic surgery (整形手术)
> Words may be used:
> unnecessary 
> expensive
> natural 
> choice 
> judge
My views on plastic surgery are balanced—while it’s not for everyone, it ultimately comes down to personal choice that deserves respect.  
我对整形手术的看法是平衡的——虽然它不适合每个人，但最终归结为值得尊重的个人选择。  


First, plastic surgery is often expensive, requiring significant financial investment that not everyone can afford. For many, the cost alone makes it an impractical option, and focusing on self-acceptance might be more meaningful than pursuing physical changes through costly procedures.  
首先，整形手术通常很昂贵，需要大量的财务投资，并非每个人都能负担得起。对许多人来说，仅凭成本就使其成为一种不切实际的选择，专注于自我接受可能比通过昂贵的程序追求物理变化更有意义。  


Second, some argue that altering one’s appearance goes against the value of natural beauty, suggesting that our original features are part of what makes us unique. In this sense, plastic surgery can feel unnecessary, as true confidence shouldn’t rely on modifying one’s looks.  
其次，一些人认为改变一个人的外表违背了自然美的价值，这表明我们的原始特征是我们独特的一部分。从这个意义上说，整形手术可能会让人觉得没有必要，因为真正的自信不应该依赖于改变自己的外表。  


However, it’s crucial to recognize that everyone has the right to make choices about their own body. Whether someone seeks plastic surgery to correct a feature they’ve long felt insecure about or to boost their self-esteem, it’s their decision, and others shouldn’t judge them harshly for it.  
然而，至关重要的是要认识到，每个人都有权对自己的身体做出选择。是否有人寻求整形手术来纠正他们长期以来感到不安全的特征，或者提高他们的自尊，这是他们的决定，其他人不应该因此而严厉地评判他们。


In short, plastic surgery is a personal choice—expensive and potentially unnecessary for some, but a valid option for others, and no one should be quick to judge those who choose it.
简而言之，整形手术是一种个人选择——对一些人来说昂贵且可能没有必要，但对其他人来说是一种有效的选择，任何人都不应该很快判断选择它的人。

# 5. How do you decide whether a person is attractive or not in your country?
> Words may be used:
> skin
> eyes/nose/mouth/lip
> face/hair 
> height/weight
> shape
> 
Perceptions of attractiveness in a country are often shaped by cultural norms, personal preferences, and evolving trends, but there are some common factors that tend to influence such judgments.  
对一个国家吸引力的看法往往受到文化规范、个人偏好和不断变化的趋势的影响，但也有一些共同因素往往会影响这种判断。  

First, **facial features** play a significant role. Many people pay attention to the symmetry and proportion of the face—for example, well-defined eyes, a balanced nose, and full lips are often considered appealing. Smooth, clear skin is also highly valued, as it’s associated with health and vitality, and is often seen as a key marker of attractiveness across genders.  

首先，**面部特征**起着重要作用。许多人注意面部的对称性和比例——例如，轮廓分明的眼睛、平衡的鼻子和丰满的嘴唇通常被认为很有吸引力。光滑、透明的皮肤也很受重视，因为它与健康和活力有关，通常被视为跨性别吸引力的关键标志。  

**Hair** is another noticeable factor. Shiny, well-groomed hair—whether long, short, curly, or straight—can enhance one’s overall appearance. Trends in hairstyles may shift, but neatness and healthiness are consistently admired.  

**头发**是另一个值得注意的因素。闪亮、整洁的头发——无论是长发、短发、卷发还是直发——都能提升一个人的整体形象。发型的趋势可能会发生变化，但整洁和健康一直受到人们的钦佩。  

Beyond facial features, **body-related traits** like height, weight, and shape matter too. While ideals vary, a proportionate body shape (e.g., a balanced height relative to weight) is often favored. For instance, a slender yet toned figure might be seen as attractive in some contexts, though there’s growing appreciation for diverse body types.  

除了面部特征，**身高、体重和体型等与身体相关的特征**也很重要。虽然理想情况各不相同，但通常更喜欢相称的体型（例如，相对于体重的平衡身高）。例如，在某些情况下，苗条而匀称的身材可能会被视为有吸引力，尽管人们越来越欣赏不同的体型。  

It’s important to note that these standards are not fixed—they evolve with time and are influenced by media, fashion, and changing societal values. What one person finds attractive might differ from another, but these elements (skin, facial features, hair, height, weight, and shape) are among the most commonly cited when people assess attractiveness.

值得注意的是，这些标准不是固定的——它们会随着时间的推移而演变，并受到媒体、时尚和不断变化的社会价值观的影响。一个人觉得有吸引力的东西可能与另一个人不同，但这些元素（皮肤、面部特征、头发、身高、体重和形状）是人们评估吸引力时最常引用的。
