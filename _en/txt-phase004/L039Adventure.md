Free Talk with Topic Ⅰ– Lesson 39 Adventure
===========================================================
1. Do you like adventure stories? Why?
Words may be used:
exciting
interesting
boring
imagination
experience
===========================================================
2. Why do people go on adventures?
Words may be used:
day-to-day life
tired of
seek
alive
attractive

===========================================================
3. Is danger a necessary part of adventure?
Words may be used:
so long as
event/activity/journey
strange
feeling
unusual

===========================================================
4. Do you consider yourself an adventurous person? Why?
Words may be used:
willing
afraid
ability
satisfied with
risk

===========================================================
5. Would you consider picking up strangers on the road?
Words may be used:
depend on
situation
most of the time
in danger
trust


===========================================================
Selective Questions:
(*If all the five questions above have already been finished, you can choose to discuss the following ones.)
6. What’s the most exciting thing you’ve ever done? 
7. If you could travel through time and space, what time and place would you choose?
8. Talk about one of your favorite adventure movies or books.
9. What adventures would you like to go on in the future?
10. Which country would you like to live in other than your own?
