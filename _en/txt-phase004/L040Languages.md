# 1. How can we improve our foreign language skills?
> Words may be used:
> native speakers
> TV shows/movies
> music
> radio
> practice

We can improve our foreign language skills through a mix of **practice** and immersive learning, using tools that make the process engaging.  
我们可以通过**练习**和沉浸式学习的结合来提高我们的外语技能，使用使过程引人入胜的工具。  

First, interacting with **native speakers** is one of the most effective ways. Even short conversations—whether through language exchange apps, local meetups, or online chats—help us learn natural pronunciation, slang, and how to respond in real time. This kind of practice fixes mistakes we might not notice alone and builds confidence in speaking.  
首先，与**母语人士**互动是最有效的方式之一。即使是简短的对话——无论是通过语言交换应用程序、本地聚会还是在线聊天——也能帮助我们学习自然发音、俚语以及如何实时回应。这种练习可以修复我们可能不会单独注意到的错误，并建立说话的信心。  

Second, using **TV shows/movies** and **music** turns learning into a fun activity. Watching shows with subtitles (in the target language first, then no subtitles) trains our listening and helps us pick up everyday phrases; singing along to songs improves pronunciation and memorization of vocabulary, since music makes words easier to recall. For example, a short 15-minute daily session of watching a sitcom or listening to a song can reinforce what we’ve learned.  
其次，使用**电视节目/电影**和**音乐**将学习变成一项有趣的活动。观看有字幕的节目（先用目标语言，然后没有字幕）可以训练我们的听力，帮助我们掌握日常短语；跟着歌曲唱歌可以提高发音和词汇记忆，因为音乐使单词更容易回忆。例如，每天看一部情景喜剧或听一首歌的短短15分钟可以强化我们所学到的知识。  

Listening to **radio** programs in the foreign language also works well, especially for improving focus and understanding fast speech. News, talk shows, or podcasts tailored to language learners expose us to different accents and topics, making our listening skills more flexible.  
听外语广播节目也很有效，特别是对于提高注意力和理解快速语音。为语言学习者量身定制的新闻、脱口秀或播客让我们接触到不同的口音和话题，使我们的听力技能更加灵活。  

Most importantly, consistency in **practice** ties it all together. Whether it’s writing a short journal entry, repeating lines from a movie, or chatting with a native speaker once a week, small, regular efforts help turn new skills into long-term habits. Combining these methods keeps learning varied and effective, making progress feel natural.
最重要的是，**实践**的一致性将这一切联系在一起。无论是写一篇简短的日记，重复电影中的台词，还是每周与母语人士聊天一次，小而有规律的努力都有助于将新技能转化为长期习惯。将这些方法结合起来，可以保持学习的多样性和有效性，让进步感觉自然。

Yes, it’s extremely important for young people to learn English—and this importance stems from how it opens doors and builds key foundations for their future.  
是的，学习英语对年轻人来说非常重要，这种重要性源于它如何为他们的未来打开大门并奠定关键基础。  

First, English is often a basic **requirement** for opportunities in **international companies** (and even many local top firms). In a globalized job market, cross-border teamwork, client communications, and access to global projects usually rely on English; young people who master it stand out when applying for roles, whether it’s a multinational internship or a full-time position that involves working with overseas teams. Without English, they might miss out on these competitive chances.  
首先，英语通常是**国际公司**（甚至许多当地顶级公司）获得机会的基本要求。在全球化的就业市场中，跨境团队合作、客户沟通和参与全球项目通常依赖英语；掌握这一技能的年轻人在申请职位时脱颖而出，无论是跨国实习还是涉及与海外团队合作的全职职位。没有英语，他们可能会错过这些竞争机会。  

Second, English boosts their **ability** to access global resources. Most academic papers, advanced online courses, and cutting-edge industry information (like tech trends or business insights) are first published in English. Learning it lets young people stay updated with the latest knowledge in their field, rather than waiting for translations—which is crucial for growing professionally or academically.  
其次，英语提高了他们获取全球资源的**能力**。大多数学术论文、高级在线课程和前沿行业信息（如技术趋势或商业见解）首先以英文发表。学习它可以让年轻人了解他们所在领域的最新知识，而不是等待翻译——这对专业或学术成长至关重要。  

Finally, learning English helps **prepare** them for a connected world. Whether it’s studying abroad, traveling to communicate with people from different cultures, or even collaborating on international volunteer projects, English acts as a common language. It lets them step beyond their local environment, build global networks, and adapt to diverse situations—skills that become more valuable as the world grows more interconnected.  
最后，学习英语有助于**为**连接世界做好准备。无论是出国留学、旅行与来自不同文化的人交流，甚至是在国际志愿者项目上合作，英语都是一种共同语言。它让他们超越当地环境，建立全球网络，适应不同的情况——随着世界变得更加互联，这些技能变得更加有价值。  

In short, English isn’t just a language; it’s a tool that expands opportunities, strengthens capabilities, and readies young people for future challenges.
简而言之，英语不仅仅是一种语言；它是一种扩大机会、增强能力、让年轻人为未来的挑战做好准备的工具。

# 3. What do you think about international student exchange programs?
> Words may be used:
> chance
> experience
> independent
> communicate
> improve

International student exchange programs are incredibly valuable—they’re far more than just a “study trip,” but a life-enriching journey that shapes personal and academic growth.  
国际学生交换项目非常有价值——它们远不止是一次“学习之旅”，而是一次塑造个人和学术成长的丰富人生的旅程。  

First, they offer a unique **chance** to step out of one’s comfort zone. Living in a foreign country means adapting to new daily habits (like local food or transportation), navigating unfamiliar systems (such as school protocols or public services), and solving small challenges on your own—all of which builds **independent** thinking and problem-solving skills. For example, figuring out how to grocery shop in a language you’re still learning or managing a tight budget abroad teaches self-reliance in a way classroom learning never could.  
首先，他们提供了一个独特的**机会**走出自己的舒适区。生活在国外意味着要适应新的日常习惯（如当地的食物或交通），驾驭不熟悉的系统（如学校协议或公共服务），并自己解决小挑战——所有这些都能培养**独立**的思维和解决问题的能力。例如，弄清楚如何用你仍在学习的语言购物，或者在国外管理紧张的预算，以一种课堂学习永远无法实现的方式教会你自力更生。  

Second, they provide an immersive **experience** that deepens cultural understanding. Unlike short travels, exchange programs let students live with local families (in many cases) or interact closely with peers from different backgrounds. This means not just “seeing” a culture, but “living” it—whether it’s celebrating local festivals, learning traditional crafts, or debating global topics with classmates who have different perspectives. Such experiences break down stereotypes and foster a more open, global mindset.  
其次，它们提供了一种身临其境的**体验**，加深了对文化的理解。与短途旅行不同，交换项目让学生与当地家庭住在一起（在许多情况下）或与来自不同背景的同龄人密切互动。这意味着不仅要“看到”一种文化，还要“生活”它——无论是庆祝当地节日、学习传统手工艺，还是与有不同观点的同学讨论全球话题。这样的经历打破了刻板印象，培养了一种更开放、更全球化的心态。  

Moreover, exchange programs force students to **communicate** actively in a foreign language (often daily), which drastically helps **improve** their language skills. Conversing with native speakers about schoolwork, hobbies, or even trivial things (like asking for directions) makes language learning practical and memorable—far more effective than memorizing vocabulary lists alone.  
此外，交换项目迫使学生**积极地用外语交流**（通常是每天），这大大有助于**提高**他们的语言技能。与母语人士谈论功课、爱好，甚至琐碎的事情（比如问路），都会使语言学习变得实用和难忘——比单独记忆词汇表更有效。  

In short, international student exchange programs are a bridge between cultures, a catalyst for personal growth, and a powerful tool for learning. They turn students into more adaptable, empathetic, and capable individuals, preparing them for a connected world.
简而言之，国际学生交换项目是文化之间的桥梁，是个人成长的催化剂，也是学习的有力工具。他们将学生培养成更具适应性、同理心和能力的人，为他们进入一个互联的世界做好准备。

# 4. Should we save dying languages?
> Words may be used:
> unique
> culture
> diversity
> source
> knowledge
Yes, we absolutely should save dying languages—they are irreplaceable treasures that hold far more value than just words.  
是的，我们绝对应该拯救垂死的语言——它们是不可替代的宝藏，比文字更有价值。  

First, dying languages carry **unique** cultural meanings that can’t be fully translated or replicated in other languages. Many Indigenous or regional languages are tied to specific traditions, stories, and ways of seeing the world: for example, a language might have 10 distinct words for “snow” (reflecting a community’s deep connection to cold environments) or special terms for traditional rituals passed down for generations. Losing these languages means losing the subtle, irreplaceable parts of a **culture** that define its identity.  
首先，垂死的语言具有**独特的**文化意义，无法完全翻译或复制到其他语言中。许多土著或地区语言都与特定的传统、故事和看待世界的方式有关：例如，一种语言可能有10个不同的单词来表示“雪”（反映了一个社区与寒冷环境的深厚联系），或者是代代相传的传统仪式的特殊术语。失去这些语言意味着失去**文化**中定义其身份的微妙、不可替代的部分。  

Second, preserving dying languages protects global **diversity**. Just as we safeguard different species to keep ecosystems healthy, we need to protect diverse languages to keep human culture rich and balanced. If only a handful of “dominant” languages survive, we risk a homogenized world where unique perspectives—on nature, community, or life itself—disappear. Each language is a thread in the tapestry of human culture, and cutting any thread weakens the whole.  
其次，保护垂死的语言可以保护全球的多样性。正如我们保护不同的物种以保持生态系统的健康一样，我们也需要保护不同的语言以保持人类文化的丰富和平衡。如果只有少数“主导”语言幸存下来，我们就有可能面临一个同质化的世界，在这个世界里，对自然、社区或生命本身的独特观点都会消失。每种语言都是人类文化挂毯上的一根线，剪断任何一根线都会削弱整体。  

Finally, dying languages are a vital **source** of **knowledge**—especially about the environment, medicine, and history. Many Indigenous languages, for instance, hold ancient wisdom about local plants (which ones are medicinal, how to sustainably harvest them) or oral histories of events that were never written down. Losing these languages means losing this accumulated knowledge—knowledge that could even help solve modern problems, like climate change or finding new natural remedies. 
最后，垂死的语言是知识的重要来源，尤其是关于环境、医学和历史的知识。例如，许多土著语言都有关于当地植物的古老智慧（哪些是药用的，如何可持续地收获它们）或从未被记录下来的事件的口述历史。失去这些语言意味着失去这些积累的知识——这些知识甚至可以帮助解决现代问题，如气候变化或寻找新的自然疗法。  

In short, saving dying languages isn’t just about “preserving words”—it’s about protecting culture, diversity, and priceless human knowledge for future generations.
简而言之，拯救垂死的语言不仅仅是“保存文字”，而是为子孙后代保护文化、多样性和无价的人类知识。

# 5. Do children learn languages faster than adults?
> Words may be used:
> imitate
> fear
> mistake
> expose
> time
> 
Yes, children often learn languages faster than adults, and this difference stems from key differences in their learning patterns and mindsets.  
是的，孩子们学习语言的速度通常比成年人快，这种差异源于他们学习模式和思维方式的关键差异。  


First, children are natural at **imitate**ing sounds and rhythms—their brains are more flexible (a trait called neuroplasticity), letting them pick up native-like pronunciation and intonation more easily. Unlike adults, who may overthink grammar rules or struggle to unlearn accents from their first language, kids absorb new speech patterns through simple repetition, whether it’s copying a parent’s words or mimicking characters in a show. This innate ability to mimic helps them master the “sound layer” of a language quickly, a hurdle many adults find hard to overcome.  
首先，孩子们天生擅长模仿声音和节奏——他们的大脑更灵活（一种称为神经可塑性的特征），让他们更容易学会母语般的发音和语调。与成年人不同，成年人可能会过度思考语法规则或难以从第一语言中忘记口音，孩子们通过简单的重复来吸收新的说话模式，无论是模仿父母的话还是模仿节目中的角色。这种天生的模仿能力帮助他们快速掌握语言的“声音层”，这是许多成年人很难克服的障碍。  


Second, children have less **fear** of making **mistake**s. They don’t worry about being judged for mixing up tenses or mispronouncing words—instead, they treat errors as part of learning. Adults, by contrast, often hold back from speaking to avoid embarrassment, which slows down practice. Kids’ willingness to “try and fail” lets them get more real-time feedback, a key part of building language skills fast.  
其次，孩子们对犯错误的恐惧较小。他们不担心因时态混淆或单词发音错误而受到评判——相反，他们将错误视为学习的一部分。相比之下，成年人通常会忍住不说话，以避免尴尬，这会减缓练习的速度。孩子们“尝试失败”的意愿让他们得到了更多的实时反馈，这是快速培养语言技能的关键部分。  


Additionally, children are constantly **expose**d to language in low-pressure, daily contexts: they learn words while playing, eating, or listening to stories, turning learning into a natural part of life. Adults, on the other hand, usually have to carve out extra **time** for language study amid work, chores, and other responsibilities—this fragmented, intentional learning is often less efficient than children’s immersive, constant exposure.  
此外，孩子们经常在低压的日常环境中接触语言：他们在玩耍、吃饭或听故事的同时学习单词，将学习变成生活中自然的一部分。另一方面，成年人通常不得不在工作、家务和其他责任中腾出额外的**时间**进行语言学习——这种零碎的、有意识的学习往往不如儿童沉浸式的、持续的学习有效。  


That said, adults can still learn languages well—they just tend to take longer, especially with pronunciation and fluency. But for picking up a language quickly and naturally, children have clear advantages.
也就是说，成年人仍然可以很好地学习语言——他们只是往往需要更长的时间，尤其是在发音和流利度方面。但对于快速自然地掌握一门语言，孩子们有明显的优势。

# 7. Do you think watching foreign TV shows or movies is a good way to improve our language skills? Please provide a brief answer.
Yes, watching foreign TV shows or movies is absolutely a good way to improve language skills. It lets you **expose** yourself to natural pronunciation, everyday slang, and real-life conversation rhythms—things textbooks often don’t cover. For example, a sitcom can teach you casual phrases used among friends, while a drama might show more formal or emotional language. You can also **imitate** the characters’ tones and words, which helps with speaking fluency. Even if you start with subtitles, gradually switching to the target language’s subtitles (or no subtitles) trains your listening. Though it won’t replace structured learning, it makes practice fun and memorable, turning passive viewing into an active way to build vocabulary and understand how the language is actually used.
是的，看外国电视节目或电影绝对是提高语言技能的好方法。它让你**接触**自然发音、日常俚语和现实生活中的对话节奏——这些都是教科书通常没有涵盖的。例如，情景喜剧可以教你朋友之间使用的随意短语，而戏剧可能会展示更正式或更感性的语言。您还可以**模仿**角色的语调和单词，这有助于提高口语流利度。即使你从字幕开始，逐渐切换到目标语言的字幕（或没有字幕）也能训练你的听力。虽然它不会取代结构化学习，但它使练习变得有趣和难忘，将被动观看转变为建立词汇和理解语言实际使用方式的主动方式。

# 10. How important is grammar in language learning? Please provide a brief answer.

Grammar is quite important in language learning—it’s like the “framework” that helps make communication clear and understandable. Without basic grammar, even if you know lots of words, your sentences might be confusing (e.g., mixing up tenses could make others misunderstand when something happened). It also helps you **improve** your ability to express complex ideas: for example, using correct sentence structure lets you share stories or opinions coherently, whether you’re writing a message or talking to **native speakers**. That said, grammar doesn’t need to be mastered perfectly at the start—you can learn it gradually through **practice** (like correcting mistakes in conversations or writing). But having a solid grasp of grammar ensures your language use is accurate, so others can easily follow and engage with what you’re trying to say.
语法在语言学习中非常重要——它就像一个“框架”，有助于使交流清晰易懂。如果没有基本的语法，即使你知道很多单词，你的句子也可能会令人困惑（例如，混淆时态可能会让别人在发生事情时产生误解）。它还可以帮助你**提高**表达复杂想法的能力：例如，使用正确的句子结构可以让你连贯地分享故事或观点，无论你是在写信息还是与**母语人士交谈**。也就是说，语法不需要一开始就完全掌握——你可以通过**练习**（比如纠正对话或写作中的错误）逐渐学习。但是，扎实掌握语法可以确保你的语言使用是准确的，这样其他人就可以很容易地理解和参与你想说的话。
