5. Why do people change their jobs?
‌人们换工作的主要原因包括以下几个方面‌：
‌1)经济因素‌：经济因素是人们换工作的主要原因之一。如果当前的薪资低于平均水平，或者无法满足生活需求，人们可能会选择换工作以获得更高的收入 。
‌2)职业发展机会‌：职业发展机会也是重要的考虑因素。如果当前的工作缺乏晋升机会或者职业发展前景不佳，员工可能会寻找新的工作以获得更好的职业前景和更多的发展机会‌ 。
‌3)工作环境和人际关系‌：工作环境和人际关系问题也可能导致人们换工作。例如，遇到不友好的上司或者工作环境不佳，员工可能会选择离开‌ 。此外，工作压力、缺乏成就感和职业倦怠也是常见的换工作原因 。
‌4)技术和行业变化‌：经济和技术变化也会影响人们的职业选择。行业衰退、自动化和新兴行业的发展可能导致原有工作的不稳定，促使人们寻找新的工作机会 。

8. What is the difference between a job and a career?
A job and a career differ in several key aspects, including duration, satisfaction, and personal growth.‌

‌Duration‌: A job is typically short-term work that provides income, while a career is a longer-term pursuit that involves multiple jobs over time. A job can last for a few weeks or a few years, whereas a career spans over an extended period and involves multiple positions within the same field or related fields‌ .

‌Satisfaction and Personal Growth‌: A job is often seen as a means to an end, providing financial stability but lacking in personal fulfillment. A career, on the other hand, is more satisfying because it allows individuals to follow their interests and passions, providing a sense of purpose and fulfillment‌ .

‌Professional Development‌: A job focuses mainly on the task at hand, while a career involves continuous learning and development. A career path involves setting goals, acquiring new skills, and advancing within the profession‌ .

‌Financial Stability‌: While both jobs and careers provide income, a career offers more financial stability over time as it involves multiple positions within the same field, often leading to higher pay and better job prospects‌
