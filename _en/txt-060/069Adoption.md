# 1. What do you think about adoption?
> Words may be used:
> family
> unable
> provide
> chance
> love
I think adoption is a warm and meaningful thing.
我认为收养是一件温暖而有意义的事情。

Many couples are **unable** to have kids on their own, and adoption lets them build a complete **family**. It can also give homeless or abandoned children a precious **chance** to start a new life. Adoptive parents can **provide** stable living conditions, care and endless **love** for these children.
许多夫妇**无法**独自生孩子，收养让他们建立了一个完整的**家庭**。它还可以为无家可归或被遗弃的儿童提供一个宝贵的机会，开始新的生活。养父母可以为这些孩子提供稳定的生活条件、关怀和无尽的爱。

In short, adoption connects lonely kids and loving families, and it is full of kindness and warmth.
简而言之，收养将孤独的孩子和充满爱的家庭联系在一起，充满了善良和温暖。

# 2. Should adopted children find their birth parents? 
> Words may be used:
> right/deserve to
> not necessarily
> expect
> imagination
> regret
> 
I think adopted children have the **right** to know their origins, but finding birth parents is **not necessarily** a must for everyone.
我认为被收养的孩子有**权利**知道他们的出身，但找到亲生父母并不一定是每个人都必须的。

Every adopted child **deserve to** make their own choice. Some may hold beautiful **imagination** about their birth families and eagerly look for them, hoping to get answers. However, birth parents may not live up to their **expect**. The truth could be painful, and it may bring conflict, sadness or lifelong **regret**.
每个被收养的孩子**都应该**做出自己的选择。有些人可能对他们的出生家庭有着美好的**想象**，并热切地寻找他们，希望得到答案。然而，亲生父母可能无法达到他们的期望。真相可能是痛苦的，它可能会带来冲突、悲伤或终身的**遗憾**。

The most important thing is the stable love from their adoptive family. They should decide whether to search by themselves, with no pressure from others.
最重要的是来自收养家庭的稳定的爱。他们应该自己决定是否进行搜索，不受他人的压力。

# 3. Should parents tell their adopted children about their adoption?
> Words may be used:
> find out
> trust
> heartbroken
> loving family
> different
I believe parents should gently tell their adopted children about their adoption.
我认为父母应该温和地告诉他们的养子他们被收养了。

First, children may **find out** the truth by accident one day, which will make them feel confused and **heartbroken**. Being honest helps build true **trust** between parents and kids. Although their life experience is **different**, they still belong to a warm and **loving family**.
首先，孩子们可能有一天会偶然发现真相，这会让他们感到困惑和心碎。诚实有助于在父母和孩子之间建立真正的**信任**。虽然他们的生活经历不同，但他们仍然属于一个温暖而充满爱的家庭。

Telling the truth properly lets children understand they are loved deeply, instead of feeling cheated. It also helps them accept their special story in a positive way.
正确地说出真相可以让孩子们明白他们被深深地爱着，而不是感到被欺骗。这也有助于他们以积极的方式接受自己的特殊故事。

# 4. What kind of parents should be allowed to adopt a child?
> Words may be used:
> stable income
> house/apartment
> enough time
> personality
> caring/patient/humorous
In my opinion, qualified adoptive parents should meet several important requirements.
在我看来，合格的养父母应该满足几个重要要求。



First, they need a **stable income** and a proper **house or apartment** to provide basic living support for the child. Second, they must have **enough time** to stay with and take care of the kid. Besides, a healthy **personality** is essential.
首先，他们需要**稳定的收入**和合适的**房子或公寓**，为孩子提供基本的生活保障。其次，他们必须有足够的时间陪伴和照顾孩子。此外，健康的**性格**是必不可少的。


They should be **caring** and **patient** to comfort the child’s feelings, and also be gentle and a little **humorous** to create a happy family atmosphere. Only responsible and warm parents can give adopted children a safe and loving home.
他们应该**关心**和**耐心**来安慰孩子的感受，也要温柔和一点**幽默**来营造一个幸福的家庭氛围。只有负责任和热情的父母才能给被收养的孩子一个安全和充满爱的家。

# 5. Which do you think is more important for a child, birth parents or foster parents （养父母）?
> Words may be used:
> born
> raise
> character
> twins
> influence
I believe foster parents are much more important for a child than birth parents.
我认为养父母对孩子来说比亲生父母重要得多。

A child is **born** with birth parents, but it is foster parents who **raise** them day by day. They spend the longest time together and shape the child’s daily life. Moreover, foster parents have the biggest **influence** on a child’s **character** and growth. Even **twins** with the same birth background can grow into totally different people under different parenting.
一个孩子出生时有亲生父母，但抚养他们的是养父母。他们在一起的时间最长，塑造了孩子的日常生活。此外，养父母对孩子的**性格**和成长有最大的**影响**。即使是出生背景相同的双胞胎，在不同的养育方式下也会成长为完全不同的人。

Birth only gives life, but long-term care and company from foster parents decide who a child truly becomes.
出生只能赋予生命，但养父母的长期照顾和陪伴决定了孩子真正成为什么样的人。

# 7. What does it take to raise a child?
Raising a child requires lots of effort, resources and sincere love.
养育孩子需要付出很多努力、资源和真诚的爱。

First, it needs a **stable income** and basic living conditions to cover daily food, education and medical costs. Second, parents must spare **enough time** to accompany, guide and care for the kid as they grow up.
首先，它需要**稳定的收入**和基本的生活条件来支付日常食品、教育和医疗费用。其次，父母必须留出足够的时间陪伴、指导和照顾孩子长大。


Besides, a good **personality** is necessary. Parents need to be **caring** and **patient** to listen to the child’s needs. They also play a key role in shaping the child’s **character** and bringing positive life **influence**. Raising a child is never easy; it needs long-term responsibility, constant company and gentle guidance all the time.
此外，良好的**性格**是必要的。父母需要**关心**和**耐心**，才能倾听孩子的需求。他们在塑造孩子的**性格**和带来积极的生活**影响**方面也发挥着关键作用。养育孩子从来都不容易；它需要长期的责任感、不断的陪伴和始终如一的温柔指导。

# 8. Should there be an age restriction for the adoptive parents?
I think there should be a proper age restriction for adoptive parents, and it is reasonable and necessary.
我认为养父母应该有适当的年龄限制，这是合理和必要的。

Firstly, age is closely related to physical condition and energy. Younger and middle-aged parents have better health and **enough time** to look after kids and accompany their growth. If adoptive parents are too old, they may lack energy to raise a child and face more health risks.
首先，年龄与身体状况和精力密切相关。中青年父母身体健康，**有足够的时间**照顾孩子并陪伴他们成长。如果养父母年纪太大，他们可能缺乏抚养孩子的精力，面临更多的健康风险。


Secondly, a suitable age helps parents keep a mature **personality** and long-term parenting ability, offering steady care and positive **influence** on children’s **character** development.
其次，合适的年龄有助于父母保持成熟的**个性**和长期的育儿能力，为孩子的**性格**发展提供稳定的照顾和积极的**影响**。


However, the restriction should not be too strict. As long as the applicants have a **stable income**, a qualified house, and are **caring** and patient, they can be considered properly. A fair age rule can better protect the interests of adopted children.
然而，限制不应过于严格。只要申请人有**稳定的收入**、合格的房子，并且**有爱心**和耐心，他们就可以得到适当的考虑。公平的年龄规则可以更好地保护被收养儿童的利益。

# 9. How would you feel if you found out that you were adopted?
If I found out I was adopted, I would feel shocked and confused at first, even a little **heartbroken**. I might feel **different** from others and wonder about my birth story.
如果我发现自己被收养了，一开始我会感到震惊和困惑，甚至有点心碎。我可能会觉得自己与其他人不同，并对自己的出生故事感到好奇。

But after calming down, I would be grateful to my foster parents. They have chosen to **raise** me, given me endless love, and built a warm **loving family** for me. I would still fully trust them and value our deep bond.
但冷静下来后，我会感激我的养父母。他们选择抚养我，给我无尽的爱，为我建立了一个温暖的爱的家庭。我仍然完全信任他们，珍视我们之间深厚的纽带。

Meanwhile, I might be curious about my birth parents, but I would not let that change my life. I would accept my story peacefully and cherish the family that has always cared for me.
与此同时，我可能对我的亲生父母很好奇，但我不会让这改变我的生活。我会平静地接受我的故事，珍惜一直关心我的家人。

# 10. Should parents adopt a child of a different race?
I think parents can adopt a child of a different race, and it is a beautiful and inclusive choice.
我认为父母可以收养不同种族的孩子，这是一个美丽而包容的选择。

First, every child deserves a warm family regardless of race. Race should never be a barrier to receiving love and care. Loving families can always provide comfort and a safe growing environment, no matter the skin color or cultural background.
首先，每个孩子都应该有一个温暖的家庭，不分种族。种族永远不应该成为接受爱和关怀的障碍。无论肤色或文化背景如何，爱的家庭总能提供舒适和安全的成长环境。

Meanwhile, cross-race adoption helps children embrace cultural diversity and build an open mind. It also teaches families to respect differences and spread kindness. As long as parents are **caring**, patient, and willing to understand the child’s native culture, they can raise the child healthily. The most important thing in a family is sincere love, not race.
同时，跨种族收养有助于孩子们拥抱文化多样性，建立开放的心态。它还教导家庭尊重差异，传播善意。只要父母**关心**、耐心，并愿意了解孩子的本土文化，他们就可以健康地抚养孩子。家庭中最重要的是真诚的爱，而不是种族。
