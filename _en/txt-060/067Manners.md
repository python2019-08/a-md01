# 1. What are some good and bad manners? Why do you think they are good or bad?
> Words may be used:
> positive language
> handshake/bow/greeting
> polite/gentle
> rude/annoying
> stare at people/interrupt/shout
 
There are many common good and bad manners in daily life.

For **good manners**, people use **positive language** and keep a **polite and gentle** attitude. Simple actions like a warm **greeting**, a polite **handshake** or a slight **bow** show respect to others. These manners help build friendly relationships and make communication smooth and comfortable.

As for **bad manners**, it is **rude and annoying** to **stare at people**, **interrupt** others when they are talking, or **shout** loudly in public. These behaviors make people feel disrespected and upset, and they will hurt interpersonal relationships and leave a bad impression on others.

So we should always keep good manners and avoid these rude behaviors in our daily life.

# 2. Are you often overtaken by other cars when driving? Why do some people have road rage(暴怒)?
> Words may be used:
> traffic jam
> angry/impatient/tightening feeling
> competition/aggressive driving
> on the highway/expressway
> change lanes

Sometimes I get overtaken by other cars when driving, especially during **traffic jam** or **on the highway**. Many drivers like to suddenly **change lanes** and overtake others blindly.
有时我开车时会被其他车超车，尤其是在**交通堵塞**或**高速公路**上。许多司机喜欢突然**变道**并盲目超车。


There are several reasons why people have road rage. First, long waiting in a **traffic jam** makes people **impatient** and creates a **tightening feeling** inside. 
人们有路怒症的原因有几个。首先，在**交通堵塞**中长时间等待会让人**不耐烦**，并在内心产生**紧绷感**。
Second, some drivers treat driving as a kind of **competition** and like **aggressive driving**. They get easily **angry** when others overtake them or cut in line. Besides, on the **expressway**, unsafe lane changes and reckless overtakes often trigger bad tempers and lead to road rage.
其次，一些司机将驾驶视为一种**竞争**，喜欢**激进的驾驶**。当别人追上他们或插队时，他们很容易生气。此外，在**高速公路**上，不安全的变道和鲁莽的超车往往会引发脾气暴躁，导致路怒。

# 3. Do people have better manners now or in earlier times?人们现在还是以前更有礼貌？
> Words may be used:
> decency/politeness/respect
> no comparison
> styles/social background/family
> in the modern era/nowadays
> gentleman/lady

I think people’s manners are hard to simply say are better or worse, there is **no comparison** between now and earlier times.
我认为人们的举止很难简单地说是好是坏，现在和以前没有可比性。

In **earlier times**, people valued **decency**, **politeness** and **respect** very much. People tried to act like a **gentleman** or **lady**, and paid strict attention to daily etiquette/ˈetɪkət/.
在**早期**，人们非常重视**得体**、**礼貌**和**尊重**。人们试图表现得像个**绅士**或**女士**，并严格遵守日常礼仪。


But **in the modern era** and **nowadays**, social rules have changed. Different **social background** and **family** education shape different behavioral **styles**. Some people still keep good manners and respect others, while others become more casual and ignore basic politeness.
但是**在现代**和**现在**，社会规则已经发生了变化。不同的**社会背景**和**家庭**教育塑造了不同的行为**风格**。有些人仍然保持礼貌，尊重他人，而另一些人则变得更加随意，忽视了基本的礼貌。


Overall, the standard of manners changes with time. It depends on personal education and self-cultivation more than the era we live in.
总的来说，礼仪标准会随着时间而变化。这比我们生活的时代更取决于个人教育和自我修养。

# 4. What's the best way to teach manners to children?
> Words may be used:
> practice
> learn from a good example 
> family dinner
> details
> well-behaved

The best way to teach children manners is through daily influence and constant **practice**.

First, parents should **learn from a good example** themselves. Kids easily copy adults’ words and actions, so parents need to behave politely all the time. Second, seize small daily **details** to guide kids, such as speaking gently and waiting in line quietly. Having rules during **family dinner** is also a great way to teach table manners and respect for others.

With long-term guidance and repetition, children will gradually become **well-behaved** and form good manners naturally.

# 5. What are the good manners in different cultures? Try to give more details.
> Words may be used:
> make a bow
> take off one's hat
> ladies first
> respect/high regard
> being modest

Different cultures have their own good manners, and they all show **respect** and **high regard** for others.

In many Asian cultures, people **make a bow** to greet elders or guests, which is a traditional way to show politeness and sincere respect. In Western culture, men often **take off one's hat** when meeting others or entering a room, and they follow the rule of **ladies first** when opening doors or walking into places.

Besides, many cultures value **being modest** in communication. People avoid showing off and speak gently to keep humble manners. All these polite customs help people get along well and express kindness across different cultures.

# 6. What advices would you give to a foreign visitor who would like to show good manners?

I’d give several practical tips to a foreign visitor to show good manners in a new country.

First, learn and follow basic local polite gestures, such as how to greet others properly, whether to **make a bow** or simply smile and nod. Second, always keep **being modest** in conversations and avoid being too loud or arrogant.

Third, respect local traditions and show **respect** and **high regard** to elders and locals. Remember simple rules like **ladies first** in public places. Also, learn to dress properly on formal occasions and **take off one's hat** when entering indoor spaces.

Most importantly, be patient, polite and willing to observe local customs. This is the easiest way to leave a good impression and show nice manners as a foreign visitor.

# 7. Can manners affect your success in life? Why?

Yes, I absolutely believe manners can greatly affect your success in life.

First, good manners show **respect** and leave a friendly impression on others. People are more willing to trust, cooperate and make friends with someone who is polite, gentle and **being modest**.

Second, proper manners help you build good interpersonal relationships in school, work and social situations. When you communicate nicely and behave properly, you get more chances for career development and support from others.

On the contrary, bad manners make people feel uncomfortable and unrespected. It may close doors to opportunities and ruin your personal image. In short, good manners are like a hidden advantage that supports long-term life success.

# 10. Do you know some good table manners? Give some examples.
Yes, I know many important good table manners.

First, wait for everyone to sit down and start eating before you pick up your chopsticks or spoon. Don’t **interrupt** others while they are talking at the table, and keep your voice low instead of choosing to **shout**. Second, never stare at others’ food or reach across the table to grab dishes.

Also, sit up straight, don’t play with utensils, and chew food with your mouth closed. During **family dinner**, being **polite and gentle** to elders shows basic **respect**. All these small details are necessary manners to behave as a **well-behaved** person.
