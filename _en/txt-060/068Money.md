# 1. Do you sometimes buy things that you don't need? Why?
> Words may be used:
> a shopping habit/just-for-fun shopping 
> a greater degree of security
> depressed/unhappy
> exposed to advertising
> keep up with your friends/family

Yes, I sometimes buy things that I don’t really need.
是的，我有时会买一些我并不真正需要的东西。

First, I have a small **just-for-fun shopping** habit. When I feel **depressed or unhappy**, shopping helps me relax and brings me joy. Besides, we are easily **exposed to advertising** online and in daily life. Those attractive ads make me want unnecessary products.
首先，我有一个小小的**只是为了好玩的购物**习惯。当我感到**沮丧或不开心**时，购物可以帮助我放松，给我带来快乐。此外，我们很容易在网上和日常生活中接触到广告。那些吸引人的广告让我想要不必要的产品。

Also, I sometimes want to **keep up with my friends**, so I buy similar items they have. Unnecessary shopping also gives me **a greater degree of security** and makes me feel satisfied for a short time. Even though I know these things are not essential, I still cannot control this little shopping habit easily.
此外，我有时想**跟上我的朋友**，所以我买了他们有类似的东西。不必要的购物也给了我**更大的安全感**，让我在短时间内感到满意。尽管我知道这些东西不是必需的，但我仍然无法轻易控制这个小小的购物习惯。

# 2. Are you good at saving money? Try to give some examples.
> Words may be used:
> investing/trading
> no unnecessary shopping
> free online budget tools
> prepare easy meals
> get books/DVDs from the library

Yes, I think I am pretty good at saving money.
是的，我觉得我很擅长省钱。


First, I stick to **no unnecessary shopping** and avoid blind consumption. 
首先，我坚持**不进行不必要的购物**，避免盲目消费。 

Second, I use **free online budget tools** to record my daily costs and control my spending. 
其次，我使用**免费的在线预算工具**来记录我的日常成本并控制我的支出。 

Third, I choose to **prepare easy meals** at home instead of eating out, which saves a lot of money. 
第三，我选择在家准备简单的饭菜，而不是在外面吃饭，这节省了很多钱。 

Besides, I **get books and DVDs from the library** rather than buying new ones. 
此外，我**从图书馆**买书和DVD，而不是买新的。 

I also learn simple **investing** knowledge to manage my money wisely instead of wasteful trading. 
我还学习了简单的**投资**知识，以明智地管理我的资金，而不是浪费交易。 

These small ways help me save money step by step.
这些小方法帮助我一步一步地省钱。

# 3. Will you give money to a beggar? Why or why not?
> Words may be used:
> homeless people
> generosity/kindness
> a burden/a trouble
> how the beggar spend the money 
> liars/crime

I will sometimes give money to beggars, but not all of them.
我有时会给乞丐钱，但不是全部。

On one hand, showing **generosity** and **kindness** can help poor **homeless people** who are truly in need and reduce their life pressure.
一方面，表现出**慷慨**和**善良**可以帮助真正需要帮助的贫困**无家可归者**，减轻他们的生活压力。

On the other hand, I also worry about **liars** and hidden **crime**. I cannot know clearly **how the beggars spend the money**. Some may waste it on bad things instead of daily needs. What’s more, blindly giving money may become **a burden** to society and encourage lazy begging.
另一方面，我也担心**骗子**和隐藏的**犯罪**。我不清楚乞丐是怎么花钱的。有些人可能会把它浪费在坏事上，而不是日常需求上。此外，盲目捐钱可能会成为社会的负担，并鼓励懒惰乞讨。

So I will give help to those who are disabled or really helpless, but refuse to support fake beggars.
所以我会帮助那些残疾人或真正无助的人，但拒绝支持假乞丐。

# 4. If someone gave you a million dollars, what would you do with it? 
> Words may be used:
> give it to charity 
> use it for vacation
> buy a nice house/car
> set up a business 
> pay off everything

If I received a million dollars, I would make a careful plan to use it wisely.
如果我收到一百万美元，我会制定一个谨慎的计划，明智地使用它。


First, I would **pay off everything** to clear all possible debts and reduce life pressure. 
首先，我会还清所有债务，减轻生活压力。
Next, I would **buy a nice house** for my family to live comfortably, instead of wasting money on a fancy car right away. 
接下来，我会**买一栋漂亮的房子**，让我的家人过上舒适的生活，而不是马上把钱浪费在一辆豪华轿车上。
Then, I would **give it to charity** to help poor and homeless people and share my luck with others. 
然后，我会把它捐给慈善机构，帮助穷人和无家可归的人，并与他人分享我的运气。 


After that, I would use a small part of the money for a relaxing **vacation** to enjoy life. In the long run, I plan to **set up a business** to make the money grow steadily. In this way, the money can bring long-term benefits to me, my family and society.
在那之后，我会用一小部分钱度过一个轻松的**假期**来享受生活。从长远来看，我计划**创办一家企业**，使资金稳步增长。通过这种方式，这笔钱可以为我、我的家庭和社会带来长期利益。

此外，如果我有多余的空间，我可以**租一个房间**或选择**合租公寓**以获得定期租金。同时，我可以学习安全的**投资**，避免高风险的**交易**，慢慢增加收入。所有这些方法都可以帮助我适当地赚外快。

# 6. Do you buy lottery?

不，我几乎不买彩票。
I think winning the lottery is just blind luck and high risk. I prefer to gain money through steady ways such as working hard or simple **investing**, instead of relying on chance. Buying lottery is a waste of money for me, and I want to keep reasonable spending and good saving habits.
我认为中彩票只是盲目的运气和高风险。我更喜欢通过稳定的方式赚钱，比如努力工作或简单的**投资**，而不是依靠机会。买彩票对我来说是浪费钱，我想保持合理的消费和良好的储蓄习惯。

# 8. Do you ever buy second-hand things ("used things")?
Yes, I sometimes buy second-hand things, and I think it is a smart choice.
是的，我有时会买二手货，我认为这是一个明智的选择。

First, second‑hand goods are much cheaper, which helps me cut down daily costs and form a good saving habit. I often buy used books, daily supplies and some daily items. They are still in good condition and totally useful.
首先，二手商品要便宜得多，这有助于我降低日常成本，养成良好的储蓄习惯。我经常买旧书、日常用品和一些日常用品。它们仍然状况良好，完全有用。

Besides, buying used things is eco-friendly and avoids waste. Compared with expensive new products, proper second‑hand shopping is reasonable and economical. I will keep this habit and avoid unnecessary expensive new items.
此外，购买二手物品既环保又避免浪费。与昂贵的新产品相比，适当的二手购物是合理和经济的。我会保持这个习惯，避免不必要的昂贵新物品。

# 9. Do you bargain when you shop?
Yes, I usually bargain when I shop, especially in local markets and small shops.
是的，我购物时通常会讨价还价，尤其是在当地市场和小商店。


Bargaining helps me cut down my daily costs and avoid overspending. Sellers often set a higher price at first, so talking politely can get a fair and reasonable price. I will not bargain too fiercely, but I try my best to save money on daily goods. It is also a simple way to develop a good saving habit and stop **unnecessary shopping**.
讨价还价帮助我降低日常成本，避免超支。卖家通常一开始会设定更高的价格，所以礼貌地交谈可以得到公平合理的价格。我不会讨价还价太激烈，但我尽力在日常用品上省钱。这也是培养良好储蓄习惯和停止**不必要购物**的简单方法。
