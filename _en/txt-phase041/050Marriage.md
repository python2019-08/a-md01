# 1. what can be done to keep a marriage healthy?
> Words may be used:
> respect
> honest
> support
> trust
> express
Maintaining a healthy marriage requires consistent effort, intentional communication, and nurturing core emotional bonds—here’s a structured guide using the required words:  
维持健康的婚姻需要持续的努力、有意的沟通和培养核心的情感纽带——以下是一个使用所需单词的结构化指南：

1. **Prioritize mutual respect**: Respect is the foundation of a lasting marriage. This means valuing your partner’s opinions, boundaries, and individuality—even when you disagree. Avoid dismissive language, eye-rolling, or belittling their feelings; instead, listen actively and treat them with the same courtesy you’d offer a loved one. Respecting each other’s time, space, and personal goals (e.g., career aspirations, hobbies) fosters a sense of dignity and appreciation that keeps the relationship balanced. 
1.**优先考虑相互尊重**：尊重是持久婚姻的基础。这意味着要重视伴侣的意见、界限和个性——即使你不同意。避免轻蔑的语言、翻白眼或贬低他们的感受；相反，要积极倾听，像对待亲人一样礼貌地对待他们。尊重彼此的时间、空间和个人目标（如职业抱负、爱好）可以培养一种尊严和欣赏感，使关系保持平衡。  
 

2. **Be honest and transparent**: Honesty builds trust and avoids resentment. Communicate openly about your thoughts, feelings, and concerns—even when the conversation is difficult. Avoid lying, hiding mistakes, or keeping secrets (big or small), as these erode trust over time. Being honest doesn’t mean being harsh; frame your words with kindness (e.g., “I felt hurt when we canceled our plans last minute” instead of “You always let me down”) to ensure your partner feels safe to be vulnerable too.  
2.**诚实透明**：诚实建立信任，避免怨恨。坦诚地交流你的想法、感受和担忧，即使谈话很困难。避免撒谎、隐瞒错误或保守秘密（无论大小），因为这些会随着时间的推移侵蚀信任。诚实并不意味着严厉；用善意来表达你的话（例如，“当我们最后一刻取消计划时，我感到很受伤”，而不是“你总是让我失望”），以确保你的伴侣也感到安全。  


3. **Offer unwavering support**: Marriage is a partnership, and supporting each other through life’s ups and downs is crucial. Celebrate their successes (e.g., a promotion, a personal milestone) with genuine enthusiasm, and stand by them during challenges (e.g., work stress, health issues). Offer practical help (e.g., taking over household chores when they’re overwhelmed) or emotional support (e.g., listening without judgment when they’re upset). Knowing your partner has your back creates a sense of security and teamwork. 
3.**提供坚定的支持**：婚姻是一种伙伴关系，在人生的起伏中相互支持至关重要。以真正的热情庆祝他们的成功（例如晋升、个人里程碑），并在面临挑战（例如工作压力、健康问题）时支持他们。提供实际帮助（例如，当他们不堪重负时接管家务）或情感支持（例如，在他们沮丧时不加评判地倾听）。知道你的伴侣支持你，会产生一种安全感和团队合作感。
 

4. **Cultivate and protect trust**: Trust is fragile—once broken, it’s hard to rebuild. Nurture it by being reliable (keeping promises, showing up when you say you will) and consistent in your actions. If you make a mistake, apologize sincerely and take responsibility instead of making excuses. Avoid behaviors that undermine trust, such as dishonesty, infidelity, or keeping important information from your partner. Trust allows couples to feel safe being their true selves and reduces anxiety about the relationship.  
4.**培养和保护信任**：信任是脆弱的——一旦破碎，就很难重建。通过可靠（信守承诺，在你说你会的时候出现）和行动的一致性来培养它。如果你犯了错误，真诚道歉，承担责任，而不是找借口。避免破坏信任的行为，如不诚实、不忠或对伴侣隐瞒重要信息。信任让情侣们感到做真实的自己是安全的，并减少了对这段关系的焦虑。  


5. **Express your feelings openly and regularly**: Don’t assume your partner knows how you feel—express gratitude, love, and even frustration in a constructive way. Regularly say “I love you,” acknowledge their efforts (e.g., “Thank you for cooking dinner tonight”), and share your emotions (e.g., “I miss spending quality time with you”). When conflicts arise, express your needs calmly instead of bottling them up (e.g., “I need us to talk about this instead of avoiding it”)—suppressed feelings often lead to resentment.  
5.**公开、定期地表达你的感受**：不要以为你的伴侣知道你的感受——以建设性的方式表达感激、爱，甚至沮丧。经常说“我爱你”，承认他们的努力（例如，“谢谢你今晚做晚饭”），并分享你的情绪（例如，”我想念和你共度美好时光“）。当冲突发生时，冷静地表达你的需求，而不是把它们憋在心里（例如，“我需要我们谈谈这件事，而不是回避它”）——压抑的感情往往会导致怨恨。  


These practices work together to create a marriage rooted in emotional safety, mutual understanding, and shared growth. A healthy marriage isn’t about being perfect; it’s about choosing to show up for each other, day after day, with respect, honesty, support, trust, and open expression.  
这些做法共同创造了一种植根于情感安全、相互理解和共同成长的婚姻。健康的婚姻不是完美的；这是关于选择日复一日地以尊重、诚实、支持、信任和开放的表达方式出现在彼此面前。

# 2. what are the things that people might consider when they are getting married?
> Words may be used:
> get along with each other.
> parents ：龙生龙，凤生凤。 Dragons born dragons, phoenixes born phoenixes.
> finance
> health
> children
When people decide to get married, they often weigh several core factors to ensure a stable and fulfilling lifelong partnership, with the following key considerations:  
当人们决定结婚时，他们通常会权衡几个核心因素，以确保稳定和充实的终身伴侣关系，并考虑以下关键因素：

1. **Whether they truly get along with each other**: This is the foundation of a lasting marriage. Couples reflect on their compatibility in values, communication styles, and daily habits—can they resolve conflicts calmly, support each other’s goals, and enjoy spending time together beyond the initial romance? Getting along with each other means accepting each other’s flaws, sharing common interests, and feeling emotionally secure in the relationship.  
1.**他们是否真的相处融洽**：这是持久婚姻的基础。情侣们反思他们在价值观、沟通方式和日常习惯上的兼容性——他们能冷静地解决冲突，支持彼此的目标，并享受在最初的浪漫之后共度的时光吗？相处意味着接受彼此的缺点，分享共同的兴趣，并在关系中感到情感安全。  

2. **Views on family and parents**: They consider how to balance their new family with their respective original families. This includes whether they can get along with each other’s parents (e.g., respecting parental opinions while setting healthy boundaries), how involved parents will be in their married life, and aligning on family traditions or caregiving responsibilities for aging parents.
2.**对家庭和父母的看法**：他们考虑如何平衡新家庭与各自原来的家庭。这包括他们是否能与彼此的父母相处（例如，在设定健康界限的同时尊重父母的意见），父母在婚姻生活中的参与程度，以及在家庭传统或照顾年迈父母的责任上保持一致。  
  

3. **Financial stability and plans**: Finances are a critical practical factor. Couples discuss their current financial situation (e.g., savings, debts, income stability), shared financial goals (e.g., buying a house, saving for retirement), and how to manage money together (e.g., joint bank accounts, budgeting, handling expenses). Clear financial communication helps avoid conflicts rooted in monetary disagreements.  
3.**财务稳定和计划**：财务是一个关键的实际因素。夫妻讨论他们目前的财务状况（如储蓄、债务、收入稳定）、共同的财务目标（如买房、退休储蓄）以及如何共同管理资金（如联合银行账户、预算、处理费用）。清晰的财务沟通有助于避免因货币分歧而产生的冲突。  

4. **Health conditions**: Both physical and mental health are important considerations. People may discuss chronic illnesses, genetic factors, or mental health histories—ensuring they are prepared to support each other through health challenges. This also includes aligning on lifestyle choices (e.g., exercise, diet) that impact long-term health and well-being as a couple.
4.**健康状况**：身心健康都是重要的考虑因素。人们可能会讨论慢性疾病、遗传因素或心理健康史，以确保他们准备在健康挑战中相互支持。这也包括调整影响夫妻长期健康和幸福的生活方式选择（如锻炼、饮食）。  

5. **Attitudes toward children**: Couples clarify whether they want to have children, when to have them, and how to raise them. This involves discussions about parenting styles, work-life balance (e.g., who will take on more childcare responsibilities), and how children will fit into their career and life plans. Aligning on views about children prevents future rifts and ensures both partners are committed to the same family vision.
5.**对孩子的态度**：夫妻明确他们是否想要孩子，何时要孩子，以及如何抚养孩子。这涉及到关于育儿方式、工作与生活平衡（例如，谁将承担更多的育儿责任）以及孩子将如何融入他们的职业和生活计划的讨论。对孩子的看法保持一致可以防止未来的分歧，并确保双方都致力于实现相同的家庭愿景。  

These considerations cover emotional, practical, and long-term aspects of marriage, helping couples build a solid foundation for their life together.  
这些考虑因素涵盖了婚姻的情感、实际和长期方面，帮助夫妻为共同生活奠定了坚实的基础。

# 3. Do you think marriage is the death of romance?
> Words may be used:
> to some extent
> for some people
> change
> family
> romantic
I don’t believe marriage is inherently the death of romance—instead, it reshapes romance in meaningful ways, with outcomes varying by individual circumstances. Here’s a structured perspective using the required words:  
我不认为婚姻天生就是浪漫的死亡——相反，它以有意义的方式重塑了浪漫，结果因个人情况而异。以下是使用所需单词的结构化透视图：

Marriage does not kill romance, but it does **change** its form—from the passionate, grand gestures of courtship to quieter, more enduring moments of connection. **To some extent**, the initial thrill of new love may fade as couples navigate the practicalities of shared life (e.g., finances, household chores, raising a **family**), but this shift does not equate to the end of romance. Instead, romance evolves into small, consistent acts of care: a thoughtful note left on the fridge, staying up late to listen to a partner’s worries, or prioritizing quality time amid busy schedules—these are all deeply **romantic** in their own right, rooted in commitment rather than fleeting excitement.  
婚姻不会扼杀浪漫，但它确实会改变浪漫的形式——从充满激情的求爱姿态到更安静、更持久的联系时刻。**在某种程度上**，随着夫妻共同生活的实际情况（例如，财务、家务、养育一个**家庭**），新爱情的最初刺激可能会消退，但这种转变并不等于浪漫的结束。相反，浪漫演变成小而持续的关怀行为：在冰箱上留下一张深思熟虑的便条，熬夜听伴侣的担忧，或者在繁忙的日程中优先安排优质的时间——这些都是深深的**浪漫**，植根于承诺而不是短暂的兴奋。  


For some people, however, marriage may feel like the "death of romance" if they cling to the idea that romance must be loud or spontaneous. When daily responsibilities take center stage, it’s easy to neglect intentional gestures, and over time, familiarity can breed complacency. But this is a choice, not an inevitability. Couples who actively nurture their bond—whether through regular date nights, shared hobbies, or simply expressing gratitude—prove that marriage can deepen romance by adding layers of trust, intimacy, and shared history that casual relationships often lack.  
然而，对于一些人来说，如果他们坚持认为浪漫必须是大声的或自发的，婚姻可能会感觉像是“浪漫的死亡”。当日常责任成为焦点时，人们很容易忽视有意的姿态，随着时间的推移，熟悉会滋生自满情绪。但这是一种选择，而不是必然。积极培养关系的夫妻——无论是通过定期的约会之夜、共同的爱好，还是仅仅表达感激之情——都证明了婚姻可以通过增加信任、亲密关系和共同的历史来加深浪漫，而这些是随意关系往往缺乏的。  

Moreover, **family** life can actually enhance romance in unexpected ways. Raising children together, overcoming challenges as a team, or celebrating milestones (like anniversaries or career wins) creates shared memories that strengthen emotional connection. The romance in marriage lies in choosing each other, day after day, even when life feels mundane—and finding joy in the ordinary moments that only come with long-term commitment.  
此外，**家庭**生活实际上可以以意想不到的方式增强浪漫。一起抚养孩子，作为一个团队克服挑战，或庆祝里程碑（如周年纪念日或职业胜利），都会创造共同的记忆，加强情感联系。婚姻中的浪漫在于日复一日地选择对方，即使生活感觉平凡——并在只有长期承诺才能带来的平凡时刻找到快乐。  

In short, marriage is not the death of romance; it’s a transition to a more mature, sustainable form of it. **To some extent**, it requires effort to adapt to the changing dynamics, but for those willing to invest in their relationship, marriage can be a space where romance grows richer, deeper, and more fulfilling over time.  
简而言之，婚姻不是浪漫的死亡；这是向更成熟、更可持续的婚姻形式的过渡。**在某种程度上**，这需要努力适应不断变化的动态，但对于那些愿意投资于他们关系的人来说，婚姻可以是一个浪漫随着时间的推移变得更加丰富、深入和充实的空间。

# 4. Is it important to get along with your in-laws (姻亲) ?
> Words may be used:
> concern
> family
> live together
> avoid
> relationship
Yes, getting along with your in-laws is undoubtedly important, as it deeply impacts your **family** harmony, marital happiness, and overall emotional well-being. Here’s a structured analysis using the required words:  
是的，与姻亲相处无疑很重要，因为它会深刻影响你的家庭和谐、婚姻幸福和整体情绪健康。以下是使用所需单词的结构化分析：

First, in-laws are an integral part of your spouse’s **family**, and their attitudes can directly influence your marital **relationship**. When you maintain a positive bond with them, you alleviate your spouse’s stress of mediating between two closest groups—your spouse won’t feel torn between their parents and you, which strengthens trust and intimacy in your marriage. Conversely, constant conflict may breed resentment or misunderstanding, putting unnecessary strain on your partnership.  
首先，姻亲是配偶**家庭不可或缺的一部分，他们的态度会直接影响你的婚姻**关系**。当你与他们保持积极的联系时，你可以减轻配偶在两个最亲密的群体之间进行调解的压力——你的配偶不会在父母和你之间感到撕裂，这增强了你婚姻中的信任和亲密感。相反，持续的冲突可能会滋生怨恨或误解，给你们的伙伴关系带来不必要的压力。  

Second, if you **live together** with in-laws (a common scenario in many cultures), getting along becomes even more crucial. Shared living spaces require mutual respect and tolerance; small frictions can escalate into major disputes if not handled well. By fostering goodwill, you create a peaceful home environment where everyone feels valued, avoiding the anxiety of daily tension. Even if you don’t cohabit, regular interactions during holidays or family gatherings will be far more enjoyable when there’s mutual affection, rather than awkwardness or hostility.  
其次，如果你和姻亲住在一起（这在许多文化中都是常见的情况），那么相处就变得更加重要。共享生活空间需要相互尊重和宽容；如果处理不当，小摩擦可能会升级为重大争端。通过培养善意，你创造了一个和平的家庭环境，让每个人都感到被重视，避免了日常紧张的焦虑。即使你们不同居，当有相互的感情，而不是尴尬或敌意时，假期或家庭聚会期间的定期互动也会更加愉快。  

Third, getting along with in-laws helps you **avoid** unnecessary conflicts rooted in unmet expectations or miscommunication. In-laws often **concern** themselves with their child’s happiness and well-being, and their intentions are usually not malicious—though their ways may differ from yours. By listening to their perspectives, showing appreciation for their care, and communicating your own needs gently, you can bridge gaps and turn potential friction into mutual support. For example, a kind word about their cooking or a willingness to involve them in family decisions can ease their worries and build trust.  
第三，与姻亲相处可以帮助你避免因期望未得到满足或沟通不畅而产生的不必要的冲突。姻亲们经常关心孩子的幸福和福祉，他们的意图通常不是恶意的——尽管他们的方式可能与你的不同。通过倾听他们的观点，对他们的关心表示感谢，并温柔地传达自己的需求，你可以弥合差距，将潜在的摩擦转化为相互支持。例如，对他们的烹饪说一句好话，或者愿意让他们参与家庭决策，可以缓解他们的担忧，建立信任。  

Finally, a harmonious relationship with in-laws enriches your extended family network. They can become reliable sources of emotional support, practical help (such as childcare), or life advice. When you treat them with respect and kindness, you cultivate a circle of love that benefits everyone—including future generations, who will grow up in a warm, connected family atmosphere. 
最后，与姻亲的和谐关系丰富了你的大家庭网络。他们可以成为情感支持、实际帮助（如育儿）或生活建议的可靠来源。当你以尊重和善良的态度对待他们时，你培养了一个对每个人都有益的爱的圈子，包括后代，他们将在温暖、互联的家庭氛围中成长。  

In short, getting along with in-laws isn’t about being perfect or forcing closeness; it’s about prioritizing **family** unity, respecting boundaries, and nurturing a **relationship** built on understanding. By doing so, you not only avoid unnecessary strife but also strengthen your marriage and create a more fulfilling family life.  
简而言之，与姻亲相处不是为了完美或强迫亲密；这是关于优先考虑家庭团结，尊重界限，培养建立在理解基础上的关系。通过这样做，你不仅可以避免不必要的冲突，还可以加强你的婚姻，创造更充实的家庭生活。

# 5. what influence does a marriage have on a person?
> Words may be used:
> responsible
> mature
> take care of
> consider
> point of view
Marriage exerts a profound and multifaceted influence on a person, shaping their character, behaviors, and outlook on life:  
婚姻对一个人产生了深远而多方面的影响，塑造了他们的性格、行为和人生观：

First, it fosters a stronger sense of **responsible**—whether it’s managing shared finances, fulfilling family commitments, or supporting a partner through life’s ups and downs, marriage encourages individuals to step beyond self-interest and prioritize collective well-being. This shift often leads to greater **maturity**, as people learn to navigate conflicts calmly, compromise, and take accountability for their actions instead of acting impulsively.  
首先，它培养了更强的**责任感**——无论是管理共同财务、履行家庭承诺，还是在生活的起伏中支持伴侣，婚姻都鼓励个人超越自身利益，优先考虑集体福祉。这种转变往往会带来更大的成熟度，因为人们学会了冷静地应对冲突、妥协，并对自己的行为负责，而不是冲动行事。  

Second, marriage teaches individuals to actively **take care of** others: from small daily gestures (preparing a meal, listening to a partner’s worries) to long-term care during illness or hardship. This practice of empathy and selflessness deepens one’s capacity for love and connection, making them more attuned to the needs of those around them.  
其次，婚姻教会个人积极照顾他人：从日常的小动作（做饭、倾听伴侣的担忧）到生病或困难期间的长期护理。这种同理心和无私的做法加深了一个人的爱和联系能力，使他们更加适应周围人的需求。  

Additionally, marriage pushes people to **consider** perspectives beyond their own. Living closely with a partner means encountering different values, habits, and ways of thinking—whether it’s making decisions about careers, family planning, or daily life. This requires open-mindedness and a willingness to adapt, expanding one’s **point of view** and challenging narrow-mindedness. Over time, individuals often become more flexible, patient, and skilled at seeing the world through another’s eyes.  
此外，婚姻促使人们**考虑**自己以外的观点。与伴侣生活在一起意味着遇到不同的价值观、习惯和思维方式——无论是在职业、计划生育还是日常生活方面做出决定。这需要开放的心态和适应的意愿，扩展自己的观点，挑战狭隘的思维。随着时间的推移，人们往往变得更加灵活、耐心，并且善于通过他人的眼睛看待世界。  

In essence, marriage is a transformative journey that nurtures responsibility, maturity, and empathy, while broadening one’s perspective through the act of caring for and compromising with a partner.  
从本质上讲，婚姻是一场变革之旅，培养责任感、成熟度和同理心，同时通过关心和妥协伴侣的行为拓宽自己的视野。
