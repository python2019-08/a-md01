Acadsoc Free Talk: Basic====Art
========================================================  
1. Do you like art?
Words may be used:
imagination
explore
beneath
reality
convey
  

========================================================
2. Do you think art classes are necessary? why?
Words may be used:
young
beneficial
development
appreciate
encourage
  

========================================================
3. How often do you visit art galleries?
Words may be used:
rarely/seldom
online resources
trouble
expensive
boring
  

========================================================
4. Is it easy to learn how to draw?
Words may be used:
challenge
patience
practice
skill
concentrate
  

========================================================
5. what makes a good painting?
Words may be used:
opinion
in my eyes
meaning/concept
beautiful
enjoy
