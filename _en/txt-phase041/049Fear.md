Fear
========================================================  
1. How can we overcome fear?
Words may be used:
understand

========================================================
2. What are the benefits of fear?
Words may be used:
notice
protect
develop confidence
push
focus
  

========================================================
3. What are the disadvantages of fear?
Words may be used:
stop/prevent
anxious/upset
health
less confident
hold back
  
========================================================

danger
ghost/monster
underneath
hide/cover/conceal
