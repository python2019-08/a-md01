# 1.Why do some people prefer to live near the city center?
> Words may be used:
> convenient
> safe
> facilities
> traffic
> friend
Many people prefer living near the city center for its practical advantages that fit daily life.  
许多人更喜欢住在市中心附近，因为它有适合日常生活的实用优势。  

First, it’s extremely **convenient**—key **facilities** like supermarkets, hospitals, and schools are within easy reach, saving time on daily errands.  
首先，它非常方便——超市、医院和学校等关键设施触手可及，节省了日常跑腿的时间。  

Second, **traffic** is well-developed, with buses, subways, and taxis readily available, making commuting or meeting **friend**s hassle-free. 
其次，**交通发达，公共汽车、地铁和出租车随处可见，使通勤或与**朋友会面变得轻而易举。  


Additionally, city centers often have better public security measures, feeling more **safe** than remote areas.  
此外，城市中心通常有更好的公共安全措施，比偏远地区更安全。 

These factors together make urban core living efficient and connected.  
这些因素共同使城市核心生活高效互联。

# 2. why are neighbors important?
> Words may be used:
> safety
> peaceful
> living environment
> help
> support
Neighbors are important because they shape a better daily life and provide vital backup in life.  
邻居很重要，因为他们塑造了更好的日常生活，并为生活提供了重要的支持。  

First, they enhance **safety**—neighbors can watch your home when you’re away, and alert you to dangers like gas leaks.  
首先，它们增强了**安全**——当你不在的时候，邻居可以监视你的家，并提醒你注意煤气泄漏等危险。  


Second, they offer timely **help** and **support**—whether borrowing daily supplies, caring for kids temporarily, or helping in emergencies, neighbors are the closest helpers.  
其次，他们提供及时的**帮助**和**支持**——无论是借用日常用品、临时照顾孩子，还是在紧急情况下提供帮助，邻居都是最亲密的帮手。  


Moreover, good neighbors create a **peaceful living environment**—friendly interactions and mutual respect make the community warm and harmonious, making daily life more comfortable.  
此外，好邻居创造了一个**和平的生活环境**——友好的互动和相互尊重使社区温暖和谐，使日常生活更加舒适。

# 3. Why do some people choose to live in the countryside?
> Words may be used:
> quiet
> larger house
> nature
> activity
> stress
Some people choose to live in the countryside for a slower, more relaxed lifestyle.  
有些人选择住在农村，过着更慢、更放松的生活。  

 
First, it’s much **quiet** than cities, helping reduce daily **stress** from noise and crowds.  
首先，这里比城市安静得多，有助于减轻噪音和人群带来的日常压力。  

Second, countryside homes often mean a **larger house** with more space, plus direct access to **nature**—fresh air, green fields, and scenic views.  
其次，乡村住宅通常意味着更大的房子，有更多的空间，而且可以直接进入**自然**——新鲜空气、绿地和风景。  

Additionally, they can enjoy outdoor **activity** like hiking, gardening, or fishing easily, which aligns with their love for a natural, low-key life.   
此外，他们可以轻松享受户外**活动**，如徒步旅行、园艺或钓鱼，这与他们对自然、低调生活的热爱相一致。

# 4. when someone moves into a neighborhood, should they introduce themselves to their new neighbors? why?
> Words may be used:
> depend
> afraid of
> stranger
> knock
> come across
It’s not mandatory but recommended—whether to do it can **depend** on personal comfort, but the benefits outweigh hesitation.  
这不是强制性的，但建议这样做——是否这样做取决于个人舒适度，但好处大于犹豫。  

Introducing yourself helps break the "**stranger**" barrier. If you just **knock** gently and say a quick hello, neighbors will recognize you later when you **come across** each other, avoiding awkwardness.  
自我介绍有助于打破“**陌生人**”的障碍。如果你只是轻轻地敲门，快速地打个招呼，当你**遇到**时，邻居们会认出你，避免尴尬。  

It also builds basic trust—you won’t feel **afraid of** interacting in daily life, and neighbors might offer help (like sharing local tips) when you need it.  
它还建立了基本的信任——你在日常生活中不会感到**害怕**互动，邻居可能会在你需要的时候提供帮助（比如分享当地技巧）。

Simple introductions make the community warmer without pressure.  
简单的介绍让社区更温暖，没有压力。

# 5. Some people say moving is one of the most stressful experiences a person can have. Do you agree?
> Words may be used:
> time and energy
> money
> throw away
> lose
> on the way
I agree—moving is indeed highly stressful for most people.  
我同意——搬家对大多数人来说确实压力很大。  

First, it consumes a lot of **time and energy**—sorting belongings, packing, moving, and unpacking all take endless effort.  
首先，它消耗了大量的时间和精力——整理物品、打包、移动和拆包都需要无休止的努力。  

Second, it costs extra **money** for moving services, packing supplies, or even home renovations.  
其次，搬家服务、包装用品甚至房屋装修都需要额外的**钱**。  

You also face tough choices: deciding what to keep or **throw away**, and worrying about items getting damaged or **lose** **on the way**.  
你还面临着艰难的选择：决定保留什么或扔掉什么，担心物品在途中损坏或丢失。  


These combined pressures make moving a mentally and physically draining experience.  
这些综合压力使搬家成为一种身心俱疲的体验。

# 6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of your neighborhood?
My neighborhood has clear pros and cons that shape daily life.  
我的社区有明显的利弊，影响着日常生活。  

###  Advantages
- **Convenient facilities**: Supermarkets, pharmacies, and a community clinic are within a 5-minute walk, making daily errands effortless.  
- **Friendly atmosphere**: Neighbors often greet each other, and there’s a small park where kids play and adults chat, fostering a warm community vibe.  
- **Smooth traffic**: Bus stops are nearby, and it’s not too crowded during rush hour, easing commutes.  

###优势
-**便利的设施**：超市、药店和社区诊所距离酒店有不到5分钟的步行路程，让您轻松完成日常杂务。  
-**友好的氛围**：邻居经常互相问候，还有一个小公园，孩子们在那里玩耍，大人在那里聊天，营造出一种温暖的社区氛围。  
-**交通顺畅**：公交车站就在附近，高峰时段也不太拥挤，方便通勤。  
  
---

###  Disadvantages
- **Noise at night**: A busy street nearby means occasional traffic noise after 10 PM, which can disturb light sleepers.  
- **Limited parking**: Parking spaces are scarce, so guests often struggle to find a spot.  
- **Small green spaces**: The community park is compact, lacking enough room for outdoor activities like jogging.  

###缺点
-**夜间噪音**：附近繁忙的街道意味着晚上10点后偶尔会有交通噪音，这会打扰浅睡者。  
-**停车位有限**：停车位很少，所以客人经常很难找到停车位。  
-**小绿地**：社区公园紧凑，缺乏足够的空间进行慢跑等户外活动。

# 8. How are house prices changing in your area? Are they rising or falling? Why?
The housing market in my area, Fengtai District of Beijing, shows a clear divided trend in 2025: new housing prices have slightly increased recently, while second-hand housing prices have been continuously falling. The specific changes and their reasons are as follows:
我所在的北京丰台区的住房市场在2025年呈现出明显的分化趋势：最近新房价格略有上涨，而二手房价格一直在持续下跌。具体变化及其原因如下：

1.  **Price changes**1.**价格变动**
    - **New houses**: The average price of new houses in Fengtai District in November 2025 is 81,021 yuan per square meter, with a month-on-month increase of 0.11%. According to another data, the average price of new houses even reached 86,845 yuan per square meter in November, a month-on-month rise of 1.67%. Some high-end new housing projects with large space and good supporting facilities have been launched one after another, which has driven the overall average price of new houses up.
    -**新房**：2025年11月丰台区新房平均价格为每平方米81021元，环比上涨0.11%。另一项数据显示，11月份新房平均价格甚至达到每平方米86845元，环比上涨1.67%。一些空间大、配套设施好的高端新房项目陆续推出，带动新房整体平均价格上涨。
    - **Second-hand houses**: The second-hand housing market presents a continuous downward trend. Its listing average price in November is 45,661 yuan per square meter, a month-on-month decrease of 0.96%. Looking back at the whole year of 2025, this downward trend has been maintained. For example, the average price in January was 54,583 yuan per square meter, and it has dropped steadily month by month since then, falling to around 46,000 yuan per square meter in October. Some non-core area second-hand houses even have a price drop of nearly 20%, and the listing cycle is prolonged significantly.
    -**二手房**：二手房市场呈持续下降趋势。其11月份的挂牌均价为每平方米45661元，环比下跌0.96%。回顾2025年全年，这一下降趋势一直保持着。例如，1月份的平均价格为每平方米54583元，此后逐月稳步下降，10月份降至每平方米4.6万元左右。一些非核心区二手房甚至出现了近20%的价格下跌，上市周期明显延长。
    
2.  **Reasons for the price changes** **价格变动的原因**
    - **For the slight rise of new houses**: On one hand, Fengtai District has obvious advantages in transportation and supporting facilities. With convenient subway lines such as Line 19 and the upcoming Lijin Line, and complete commercial and medical facilities like Fengke Wanda and Tiantan Hospital, new housing projects here are quite attractive. On the other hand, many high-quality high-end new housing projects have been launched recently. These projects not only have high floor area ratios and high - end exterior designs such as stone and aluminum plates, but also have well - designed house types. In addition, the government has increased the supply of core plots and emphasized the construction of "high-quality houses", which has promoted the quality and price of new houses.
    -**新房略有增加**：一方面，丰台区在交通和配套设施方面具有明显优势。这里有19号线和即将开通的利津线等便捷的地铁线路，以及丰科万达、天坛医院等完善的商业和医疗设施，这里的新住房项目非常有吸引力。另一方面，最近又推出了许多高质量的高端新房项目。这些项目不仅具有较高的建筑面积比和高端的外部设计，如石材和铝板，而且具有设计良好的房屋类型。此外，政府增加了核心地块的供应，强调建设“优质住宅”，促进了新房的质量和价格。
    - **For the continuous fall of second-hand houses**: Firstly, the supply and demand relationship is unbalanced. The new housing inventory in Fengtai District is large, and a large number of new projects have been put on the market continuously. These cost-effective new houses have diverted many buyers, making the competition in the second-hand housing market more intense. Secondly, the confidence of second-hand housing buyers has not been fully restored. Most buyers tend to choose cost-effective low-price houses. High-priced second-hand houses are hard to sell, so owners have to reduce prices to promote transactions. Finally, there are differences in the quality of second-hand houses. Most of the second-hand houses with a long building age have problems such as inconvenient elevator installation and unreasonable house type design. Compared with newly-built houses with various advantages, they are less competitive, which also leads to the continuous decline of their prices.
    -**二手房持续下跌**：一是供需关系不平衡。丰台区新房存量大，大量新项目不断投放市场。这些性价比高的新房吸引了众多买家，使二手房市场的竞争更加激烈。其次，二手房买家的信心尚未完全恢复。大多数买家倾向于选择成本效益高的低价房。高价二手房很难出售，因此业主不得不降价以促进交易。最后，二手房的质量也存在差异。大多数建筑年龄较长的二手房都存在电梯安装不便、户型设计不合理等问题。与各种优势的新建房屋相比，它们的竞争力较弱，这也导致了它们的价格持续下降。

Yes, I think my neighborhood is a safe place to live in—its security measures and community atmosphere give me peace of mind.  
是的，我认为我的社区是一个安全的居住地——它的安全措施和社区氛围让我感到安心。

我很少听说这里发生盗窃或安全事故。即使在晚上走路也感觉很安全，因为路灯维护良好，社区安静而没有混乱。

# 10. Would you move to somewhere you hate in exchange for a good school for your child?
I wouldn’t—though a good school matters, living in a place I hate would hurt the whole family’s well-being.  
我不会——尽管一所好学校很重要，但住在我讨厌的地方会损害全家的幸福。  

A stressful, unpleasant living environment (like noise, poor facilities, or a lack of comfort) would affect my mood and energy, which in turn impacts how I care for my child. Kids thrive not just on good education, but also on a stable, happy home life—if the neighborhood feels hostile or unlivable, it could overshadow the school’s benefits.  
压力大、不愉快的生活环境（如噪音、设施差或缺乏舒适感）会影响我的情绪和精力，进而影响我照顾孩子的方式。孩子们不仅在良好的教育中茁壮成长，而且在稳定、幸福的家庭生活中茁壮成长——如果社区感到敌对或不适合居住，这可能会掩盖学校的好处。  

I’d rather find a balance: prioritize a school that’s strong enough, but pair it with a neighborhood where both my child and I feel at ease.  
我宁愿找到一个平衡点：优先考虑一所足够强大的学校，但要与一个我和孩子都感到自在的社区配对。
