  

Acadsoc Free Talk: Basic
Neighborhood
  
========================================================
1.Why do some people prefer to live near the city center?
Words may be used:
convenient
safe
facilities
traffic
friend
  
========================================================
2. why are neighbors  important?
Words may be used:
safety
peaceful
living environment
help
support
  
========================================================
3. Why do some people choose to live in the countryside?
Words may be used:
quiet
larger house
nature
activity
stress
  
========================================================
4. when someone moves into a neighborhood, should they introduce themselves to their new neighbors? why?
Words may be used:
depend
afraid of
stranger
knock
come across

  
========================================================
5. Some people say moving is one of the most stressful
experiences a person can have. Do you agree?
Words may be used:
time and energy
money
throw away
lose
on the way
  
========================================================
Selective Questions:
(*If all the five questions above have already been finished, you can choose to discuss the following ones.)
========================================================
6. What are the advantages and disadvantages of your neighborhood?

========================================================
7. Does your neighborhood have all the facilities you need? What would you like to add?

========================================================
8. How are house prices changing in your area? Are they rising or falling? Why?

========================================================
9. Do you think your neighborhood is a safe place to live in?

========================================================
10. Would you move to somewhere you hate in exchange for a good school for your child?
