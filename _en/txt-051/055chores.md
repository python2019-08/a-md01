  

# 1. Can you name some chores that need to be done every day?
Words may be used:
collect trash
wash the dishes
do the laundry
water the plants
make the bed
clean the kitchen
  

# 2. Do you pay your children (or get paid from your parents) for doing chores? How does your family decide who does various chores around the house?
Words may be used:
from one to ten yuan
weekly/monthly chore chart
boys/girls
young/old
tall/short
strong/thin
  

# 3. if you could buy only one of these two things, would you choose a dishwasher or a washing machine? why?
Words may be used:
dry
convenient
useful
necessary
can' t live without
  

# 4. Do you agree that "getting kids involved in chores helps the family work better'?
Words may be used:
share
responsible
motivated
tired
interests
  

# 5. How can we get family members to help us with chores?
Words may be used:
standard
realize
teach
pay
needs
