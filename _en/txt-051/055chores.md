Every day, there are many common chores we need to do.

Besides, **water the plants** is a daily task too. Sometimes we also need to **clean the kitchen** every day, and **do the laundry** regularly as part of daily housework.

# 2. Do you pay your children (or get paid from your parents) for doing chores? How does your family decide who does various chores around the house?
> Words may be used:
> from one to ten yuan
> weekly/monthly chore chart
> boys/girls
> young/old
> tall/short
> strong/thin

No, I don’t get paid by my parents for doing chores. Even if some families give pocket money **from one to ten yuan** for housework, my family thinks doing chores is our responsibility.

My family uses a **weekly chore chart** to divide the work fairly. We don’t split chores by **boys or girls**, nor by being **tall or short**. Instead, we consider who is **young or old** and **strong or thin**. People who are stronger take heavier work, while younger and thinner family members do lighter simple chores.

# 4. Do you agree that  "getting kids involved in chores helps the family work better"?
> Words may be used:
> share
> responsible
> motivated
> tired
> interests
Yes, I agree that getting kids involved in chores helps the family work better.

When children do chores, they learn to **share** housework with parents. This makes the whole family less **tired** and more relaxed. Taking on small tasks helps kids become more **responsible** and grow up independently. When they finish chores well, they feel **motivated** and proud. Also, doing chores according to their **interests** makes them more willing to take part. In this way, the family becomes more united and runs much more smoothly.

当孩子做家务时，他们学会了与父母**分担**家务。这能让全家都少些**疲惫**，更轻松自在。承担琐碎任务有助于孩子变得**负责**，独立成长。完成家务后，他们会感到**动力**满满并充满自豪。此外，根据他们的**兴趣**安排家务能让他们更乐于参与。如此一来，家庭关系更加融洽，运转也顺畅许多。

# 5. How can we get family members to help us with chores?
> Words may be used:
> standard
> realize
> teach
> pay
> needs 

We can get family members to help with chores in several ways. 
First, we should **teach** them how to do chores properly and set a clear **standard** for housework. When they **realize** the importance of sharing chores, they will be more willing to help. 
We can also **pay** attention to everyone’s different **needs** and divide chores fairly. In this way, all family members will take turns to support each other.
我们可以通过多种方式让家人帮忙做家务。首先，我们应该教他们如何正确地做家务，并为家务设定一个明确的标准。当他们意识到分担家务的重要性时，他们会更愿意帮忙。我们还可以**关注每个人不同的**需求**，公平地分配家务。这样，所有家庭成员都会轮流互相支持。

# 6. What chores do you do at home?
At home, I do many simple chores regularly.
I **make the bed** every morning after getting up. After dinner, I help **wash the dishes** and **clean the kitchen**. I also **water the plants** every day and help **collect trash** to take outside. Sometimes I even help my family **do the laundry** when I have free time.

# 7. What kind of products does your family buy on the Internet?

My family buys all kinds of products online.
We usually buy daily necessities, snacks and clothes on the Internet. Sometimes we also purchase books, small home appliances and gardening supplies for watering plants. Shopping online is convenient, so we choose most of our daily items this way.

# 8. How long do you think you could live without a maid or someone to help you in the house?

I think I could live quite a long time without a maid or house helper.
I can finish daily chores by myself, like making the bed, washing the dishes and watering the plants. I am independent and able to keep my room tidy and clean. As long as I keep a regular routine, I can live comfortably on my own for a very long time.

# 9. What chores do you dislike to do?

Among all the house chores, I dislike **cleaning the kitchen** and **doing the laundry** the most.
Cleaning the kitchen is tiring and takes a lot of time to wash greasy dishes. Doing the laundry is also boring because I need to sort clothes, wash them and wait for them to dry. I prefer easy and light chores like making the bed and watering plants instead.

# 10. Can you think of some dangerous chores that you can't do?
  
There are some dangerous chores that I am too young to do safely.
For example, repairing high places on the wall, moving heavy furniture, and dealing with broken electrical appliances are all risky chores. They may cause falling down, getting hurt or electric shock. So I can only do simple and safe housework, and leave these dangerous chores to adults.
