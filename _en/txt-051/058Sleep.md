# 2. What keeps you awake at night? Give more details
> Words may be used:
> uncomfortable bed
> coffee/tea/coke
> stress
> snoring
> noise
  
Several things keep me awake at night. First, I often feel stressed about my work and study, which makes my mind race and unable to relax. Second, if I drink coffee, tea, or coke in the afternoon or evening, the caffeine will definitely keep me awake. Third, my bed is a bit old and uncomfortable, so it’s hard for me to fall asleep quickly. Sometimes there is noise outside my window, like cars or people talking. And when my family member snores loudly, I can’t sleep at all. All these problems together make me stay awake late at night.
 
有好几件事让我晚上睡不着。首先，我经常因为工作和学习感到有压力，脑子停不下来，无法放松。其次，如果我下午或晚上喝了咖啡、茶或可乐，咖啡因一定会让我睡不着。第三，我的床有点旧，睡得不舒服，所以很难快速入睡。有时候窗外有噪音，比如汽车或说话声。而且当家人打鼾很响时，我根本睡不着。这些问题加在一起，让我晚上经常醒着。

# 3. Have you ever used any special methods that help you get to sleep?
> Words may be used:
> music
> earplug
> yoga
> sleeping pills
> counting sheep

Yes, I have tried several special methods to help me fall asleep.
Sometimes I listen to soft **music** to calm my mind down. When it is too noisy, I will wear an **earplug** to block outside sounds. I also try simple **yoga** stretches before going to bed to relax my body. Besides, I sometimes try **counting sheep** when I lie awake. However, I never take **sleeping pills** because I think they are not good for my health.

# 6. Do you believe that it's possible to interpret dreams?
Yes, I partly believe it’s possible to interpret dreams.
Some dreams are connected to our daily thoughts, worries and feelings, so they can be explained in some ways. Dreams can show our hidden emotions and wishes. However, not all dreams have clear meanings, and some are just random thoughts from our mind. So I think we can interpret some dreams, but we shouldn’t take all dream explanations too seriously.

# 7. Do you fall asleep while watching the TV or reading?

Yes, I sometimes fall asleep while watching TV or reading books.
When I feel tired at night, watching a relaxing TV show makes me sleepy easily. Also, reading a quiet and gentle book often makes me feel relaxed and slowly fall asleep. It usually happens when I stay up late and feel exhausted after a long day.

# 8. Do you always have a nap in the day time? Does it help?

Not exactly. I don’t always take a nap every daytime, but I sometimes have a short nap when I feel tired.
Having a nap really helps me refresh my mind, relieve tiredness and make me more energetic for the rest of the day. However, I keep the nap short so it won’t affect my sleep at night.

# 9. Do you ever oversleep?
Yes, I sometimes oversleep, especially on weekends or holidays.
When I stay up late the night before, I find it hard to wake up on time. Once I oversleep, I have to hurry to get ready. I try to set several alarms to avoid oversleeping again.

# 10. Do you need an alarm clock to wake you up?

Yes, I really need an alarm clock to wake me up every day.
I’m not used to waking up naturally, so without an alarm, I would easily oversleep. I usually set several alarms to make sure I can get up on time. It’s a really helpful tool to keep my daily routine regular.
