# 1. Do you know Batman? What does he look like?
> Words may be used:
> strong/tall
> thin/short
> smart/clever
> stupid/foolish
> black/blue/red

Yes, I know Batman, one of the most iconic superheroes in pop culture. 是的，我知道蝙蝠侠，他是流行文化中最具标志性的超级英雄之一。

He is typically depicted as **strong/tall** with a muscular build, reflecting his rigorous training and combat skills. 
他通常被描绘为**强壮/高大**，身材健硕，这反映了他严格的训练和出色的战斗技能。
His signature suit is entirely **black**, often accented with subtle gray details, and paired with a flowing black cape that helps him glide through the night. Beneath the mask, he is Bruce Wayne, a **smart/clever** billionaire who uses his intellect, strategic mind, and advanced technology to fight crime in Gotham City. 
他的标志性套装完全是**黑色**的，通常点缀着微妙的灰色细节，并搭配一件飘逸的黑色斗篷，这有助于他在夜间滑翔。面具之下，他是布鲁斯·韦恩，一个**聪明/机智**的亿万富翁，他利用自己的才智、战略头脑和先进技术打击哥谭市的犯罪活动。
Unlike some superheroes, he has no superpowers—his strength lies in his physical prowess and sharp, tactical thinking.
与一些超级英雄不同，他没有超能力——他的力量在于他的身体力量和敏锐的战术思维。

# 2. In your opinion, what is a hero?
> Words may be used:
> save/protect
> fight
> justice
> mystery
> brave

In my opinion, a hero is someone who acts with courage and purpose to uphold what is right, rather than seeking fame or reward. Here’s a breakdown using the required words:
在我看来，英雄是那些出于勇气和目的，为维护正义而行动，而非追求名利或奖励的人。以下是使用所给词汇的详细解释：

A hero’s core mission is to **save/protect** others, often putting their own safety at risk to defend the vulnerable. They **fight** not just with physical strength, but with unwavering resolve against injustice, oppression, or harm—whether that means standing up to a bully, advocating for marginalized groups, or confronting larger threats to their community.
英雄的核心使命是**拯救/保护**他人，常常为了保护弱势群体而置自身安危于不顾。他们**抗争**的不仅仅是体力上的较量，更是以坚定的决心对抗不公、压迫或伤害——无论是挺身而出反抗恶霸，为边缘群体发声，还是直面社区面临的更大威胁。


At the heart of heroism is a commitment to **justice**. Heroes don’t turn a blind eye to wrongdoing; they use their voice, actions, or influence to right wrongs and ensure fairness for all. This requires being **brave** enough to face fear, uncertainty, or even danger, even when the odds are stacked against them.
英雄主义的核心是对**正义**的坚守。英雄不会对不义之举视而不见；他们会通过自己的声音、行动或影响力来匡正错误，确保人人都能享有公平。这需要足够的**勇气**来面对恐惧、不确定性，甚至是危险，即便形势对他们极为不利。

While some heroes may have an air of **mystery** (like Batman’s secret identity), true heroism isn’t about being enigmatic—it’s about the choices to act with integrity and compassion. A hero can be anyone, from a everyday person stepping up in a crisis to a fictional character fighting for a greater good.
虽然有些英雄可能带有一种**神秘**的气息（比如蝙蝠侠的秘密身份），但真正的英雄主义并不在于神秘莫测——而在于做出正直和富有同情心的选择。英雄可以是任何人，从在危机中挺身而出的普通人到为更崇高的事业而奋斗的虚构人物。

# 3. who is your favorite superhero and why?
> Words may be used:
> Batman/Spiderman/Superman
> handsome/pretty
> smart/talented
> high ideals
> quality
My favorite superhero is **Batman**, and he stands out to me for his unique combination of grit, intellect, and unwavering moral compass, aligning perfectly with the required words:
我最喜欢的超级英雄是**蝙蝠侠**，他因其坚韧、智慧和坚定不移的道德准则的独特结合而脱颖而出，这与以下词语完美契合：

First, what draws me to him is his **smart/talented** nature—unlike Superman’s superpowers or Spiderman’s spider abilities, Batman relies entirely on his sharp mind, strategic genius, and years of rigorous training. He’s a master detective, a skilled fighter, and a tech innovator who turns his resources into tools to protect Gotham, proving that brains can be just as powerful as superhuman strength.
首先，吸引我的是他的**聪明/才华横溢**的天赋——与超人的超能力或蜘蛛侠的蜘蛛能力不同，蝙蝠侠完全依靠他敏锐的头脑、战略天赋和多年的严格训练。他是一位杰出的侦探、熟练的战士和技术创新者，他将资源转化为保护哥谭市的工具，证明智慧与超人的力量一样强大。


Beyond his skills, Batman embodies **high ideals** that drive every action. He fights not for glory, but to uphold justice and protect the innocent, even when it means sacrificing his own happiness. His commitment to never taking a life, no matter how evil his enemies are, reflects a moral integrity that makes his heroism feel deeply human and inspiring.
除了他的技能，蝙蝠侠还体现了**崇高的理想**，这些理想驱使着他采取每一个行动。他战斗不是为了荣耀，而是为了维护正义和保护无辜者，即使这意味着要牺牲自己的幸福。他承诺永不杀人，无论敌人多么邪恶，这体现了一种道德操守，使他的英雄主义充满了人性，令人深受鼓舞。


While he’s often depicted as **handsome** beneath the mask, it’s his inner **quality** that truly defines him—his resilience in the face of trauma, his empathy for the marginalized, and his refusal to give up on Gotham, even when the city has given up on itself. He’s a hero who proves that courage isn’t the absence of fear, but the choice to act despite it.   
虽然他面具下的形象常被描绘得**英俊**，但真正定义他的却是他的内在**品质**——面对创伤时的坚韧不拔，对边缘人群的同情心，以及即使哥谭市已经放弃自己，他仍拒绝放弃这座城市。他是一位英雄，证明勇气并非没有恐惧，而是尽管恐惧仍在，仍选择行动。

# 4. Do you want to be a superhero? Give reasons.
> Words may be used:
> proud/loser
> HERO!
> stronger/confidence
> chance
> dangerous
> challenge
Yes, I would absolutely want to be a superhero, and my reasons tie closely to the required words:
是的，我绝对想成为超级英雄，我的理由与以下关键词密切相关：

First, being a superhero would mean having the **chance** to make a real, tangible difference in the world—to protect the vulnerable, stand up for justice, and turn helplessness into action. That sense of purpose would make me feel deeply **proud**, not for the attention, but for using my abilities to lift others up instead of standing by as a bystander.
首先，成为超级英雄意味着我有**机会**为世界带来真正、切实的改变——保护弱势群体，捍卫正义，将无助转化为行动。这种使命感会让我深感**自豪**，不是因为受到关注，而是因为利用我的能力去帮助他人，而不是袖手旁观。

Second, the role would push me to become **stronger/confidence** in every way—physically, mentally, and emotionally. Superheroes don’t just fight villains; they face their own fears and limitations, turning every **challenge** into a chance to grow. Even the **dangerous** moments would feel meaningful, because they’re in service of something greater than myself.
其次，这个角色会促使我在身体、心理和情感等各方面都变得更加强大/自信。超级英雄不仅仅与恶势力作战；他们还直面自己的恐惧和局限，将每一个**挑战**都转化为成长的机会。即使是**危险**的时刻也会让人觉得有意义，因为它们是为了比自我更伟大的事业而服务的。

Most importantly, being a superhero would mean embodying the idea of **HERO!**—not as a perfect, untouchable figure, but as someone who chooses courage over comfort, and who refuses to let others suffer when they have the power to help. I’d rather take on the risks and challenges than feel like a **loser** who did nothing when the world needed help.   
最重要的是，成为超级英雄意味着要体现**英雄**的概念——不是作为一个完美无瑕、不可触碰的形象，而是作为一个选择勇气而非舒适的人，一个在有能力帮助他人时拒绝让他们受苦的人。我宁愿承担风险和挑战，也不愿在世界需要帮助时，感觉自己像个**失败者**，什么也不做。

# 5. If it is the end of the world, can heroes save the world?
> Words may be used:
> important
> HERO
> loss/damage/hurt/harm
> help/charge
> power/cheer
> team work
  
 In the face of the end of the world, heroes can play a critical role, though saving the world often depends on more than just one individual—it requires collective effort. <!--Here’s how it aligns with the required words: -->
面对世界末日，英雄们可以发挥关键作用，尽管拯救世界往往不仅仅依赖于一个人——它需要集体的努力。以下是如何将其与所需词汇相匹配的：

Heroes are **important** not just for their **power**, but for their ability to **help/charge** others with hope. A single **HERO** might not have the strength to reverse global disaster alone, but their courage can inspire millions to act, turning despair into action. They can lead the way in mitigating **loss/damage/hurt/harm**, whether by rescuing those in danger, sharing critical resources, or devising creative solutions to the crisis.
英雄之所以重要，不仅因为他们的力量，更因为他们能够给予他人希望。单个英雄或许没有足够的力量独自扭转全球灾难，但他们的勇气可以激励数百万人行动起来，将绝望转化为行动。他们可以带头减轻损失/损害/伤害，无论是通过营救处于危险中的人、分享关键资源，还是为危机设计创新的解决方案。

Most importantly, true world-saving relies on **team work**. When heroes collaborate with scientists, first responders, and ordinary people, their combined skills and resolve become far more powerful than any individual’s abilities. Even if they can’t erase all harm, heroes can minimize suffering, protect the vulnerable, and **cheer** people on to keep fighting—turning the "end of the world" into a chance to rebuild and start anew. 
最重要的是，真正的拯救世界依赖于**团队协作**。当英雄与科学家、应急响应人员和普通人携手合作时，他们集体的技能和决心将远胜于任何单个人的能力。即使他们无法消除所有伤害，英雄们也能将痛苦降至最低，保护弱势群体，并**鼓舞**人们继续战斗——将“世界末日”转变为重建和重新开始的机会。

Some fictional heroes live in mysterious mansions, hidden caves or big modern cities, but the true heroes live simply among common people, quietly protecting and caring for others.
