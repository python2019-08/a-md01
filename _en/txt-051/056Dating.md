It depends. In **high school or college**, having a boyfriend or girlfriend does not always lower school performance.
If students have good **self-control**, they can study together and encourage each other.
But if they often **fight**, get into a **bad mood**, and spend too much time dating, it may **result in** worse grades and poor academic performance.
So the key is self-control and balance.

I think online dating websites can be both **helpful** and risky.

> Words may be used:
> on time
> relax
> interesting
> listen
> believe in
To make the first date successful, we can do these simple things.
First, be **on time**—it shows respect.
Second, **relax** and be natural, so both people feel comfortable.
Third, talk about **interesting** topics and be a good person to **listen** to.
Finally, **believe in** yourself and be honest.
These will help the first date go smoothly.

I think dating a coworker can be okay, but we must be careful.
On one hand, we know each other well and can support each other at work.
On the other hand, it may cause trouble if we **fight** or break up.
We need good **self-control** to keep work and feelings separate, so it won’t **result in** a **bad mood** or influence our job.
