Musicians and singing stars have several differences, which are as follows:

    Besides singing, they often play musical instruments, compose music, arrange scores, and conduct musical performances. 
    除了唱歌，他们还经常演奏乐器、作曲、编曲和进行音乐表演。
    They have a deep understanding and proficiency in music theory and techniques. For example, a composer is a type of musician who focuses on creating original music, using various musical elements such as melody, harmony, and rhythm to shape musical works.   他们对音乐理论和技巧有着深刻的理解和熟练的掌握。例如，作曲家是一种专注于创作原创音乐的音乐家，他使用旋律、和声和节奏等各种音乐元素来塑造音乐作品。

    - **Singing stars**: Primarily focus on singing. Their main task is to use their voices to interpret and perform songs. While some singing stars may also be involved in songwriting or playing musical instruments to some extent, singing is their core business. For instance, many pop-singing stars are known for their excellent singing skills and stage performances.     主要专注于唱歌。他们的主要任务是用他们的声音来解释和表演歌曲。虽然一些歌星也可能在一定程度上参与歌曲创作或演奏乐器，但唱歌是他们的核心业务。例如，许多流行歌星以其出色的歌唱技巧和舞台表演而闻名。

It didn't magically solve all my problems, but it gave me the strength to keep going.   这首歌成了我的慰藉。这就像一只温柔的手伸出来治愈我受伤的心。每当我被学业的压力或破碎的友谊的痛苦压垮时，我都会听《修复你》。它并没有神奇地解决我所有的问题，但它给了我继续前进的力量。

Over time, as I slowly mended my relationship with my friend and learned to better manage my school stress, the song still held a special place in my heart.
