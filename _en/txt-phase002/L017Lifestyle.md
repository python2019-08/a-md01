Physical activity such as jogging, cycling, swimming, and the like is indeed a crucial part of a healthy lifestyle. It offers numerous benefits for both physical and mental well-being, as detailed below:

- **Physical health benefits** 
-**身体健康益处**
    - **Strengthening the body**: **1**Regular jogging can enhance cardiovascular function, increasing the heart's ability to pump blood and improving  blood circulation.  **2**Cycling helps build leg strength, while swimming engages multiple muscle groups, improving overall muscle tone and strength. **3** This enhanced physical strength allows people to perform daily activities more easily and reduces the risk of injury.
    -**强身健体**： **1**经常慢跑可以增强心血管功能，提高心脏泵血能力，改善血液循环。 **2**骑自行车有助于增强腿部力量，而游泳可以锻炼多个肌肉群，改善整体肌肉张力和力量。**3**这种增强的体力使人们更容易进行日常活动，并降低了受伤的风险。

Additionally, it can help regulate blood pressure, reducing the risk of hypertension.

-**心理健康福利**

    -**减轻压力和焦虑**：**1**参加慢跑或骑自行车等体育活动可以让身体释放内啡肽。**2**这些是天然的化学物质，可以增强情绪，帮助减轻压力和焦虑感。**3**游泳后，人们通常会感到放松和更自在，因为重复的动作和浸入水中对神经系统有镇静作用。
