1. What did you like to do when you were a child?
> Words may be used:
> climb
> playground
> cartoon
> television
> toy
>
When I was a child, I had a lot of things I liked to do. I was really into climbing. There was a big tree in our backyard, and I would spend hours trying to climb up as high as I could. It made me feel adventurous and excited.
当我还是个孩子的时候，我有很多喜欢做的事情。我真的很喜欢爬山。我们家后院有一棵大树，我会花几个小时尽可能地爬得高。这让我感到冒险和兴奋。

I also loved going to the playground. There were swings, slides, and monkey bars there. I would play on the slides, feeling the wind rush past me as I slid down. The monkey bars were a challenge, but I always tried my best to move from one bar to another. It was great fun to be with other kids at the playground and run around freely.
我也喜欢去操场。那里有秋千、滑梯和单杠。我会在滑梯上玩，当我滑下来时，感觉风从我身边掠过。单杠是一个挑战，但我总是尽力从一个单杠转到另一个。和其他孩子在操场上自由奔跑，真是太有趣了。


Watching cartoons on television was another favorite activity of mine. I would sit in front of the TV for hours, engrossed in colorful and exciting cartoon worlds. My favorite cartoons had funny characters and interesting stories that always made me laugh.
在电视上看动画片是我另一个最喜欢的活动。我会在电视机前坐上几个小时，全神贯注于丰富多彩、激动人心的卡通世界。我最喜欢的漫画有有趣的人物和有趣的故事，总是让我发笑。

Toys were also an important part of my childhood. I had a collection of different toys, from action figures to building blocks. I would spend a long time playing with my action figures, creating my own little adventures for them. Building with blocks was also a lot of fun as I could construct all sorts of things, like houses and towers.
玩具也是我童年的重要组成部分。我收集了各种各样的玩具，从人偶到积木。我会花很长时间玩我的动作人偶，为他们创造我自己的小冒险。用积木建造也很有趣，因为我可以建造各种各样的东西，比如房子和塔楼。
> Play with iron rings, play with whipping top,玩铁环 ，玩陀螺
> Whip the top with a whip 用鞭子抽陀螺

Those were the things I enjoyed doing when I was a child, and they all hold a special place in my heart. 
这些都是我小时候喜欢做的事情，它们在我心中都占有特殊的地位。

2. Do you miss being a child? Why or why not?
> Words may be used:
> pressure/stress
> work/school
> free
> fun
> simple

Sometimes I do miss being a child. There were so many wonderful aspects of that time in my life. When I was a child, life was simple. I didn't have to worry about the pressures and stresses that come with adulthood. There was no need to think about work deadlines, paying bills, or managing complex relationships. School was mainly about learning new things in a relatively carefree environment, and the responsibilities were much lighter compared to what I face now.
有时候，我真的很怀念当孩子的感觉。那段时间在我的生活中有很多美好的方面。当我还是个孩子的时候，生活很简单。我不必担心成年后的压力和压力。没有必要考虑工作截止日期、支付账单或管理复杂的关系。学校主要是在一个相对无忧无虑的环境中学习新事物，与我现在面临的相比，责任要轻得多。

I also had a lot of free time to do the things I loved. I could go to the playground whenever I wanted, play with my toys for hours on end, or watch cartoons without a care in the world. It was all about having fun, and those moments of pure enjoyment were truly precious. There was a sense of freedom in being a child that is hard to replicate as an adult.
我也有很多空闲时间做我喜欢的事情。我可以随时去操场，连续几个小时玩我的玩具，或者无忧无虑地看动画片。这一切都是为了好玩，那些纯粹享受的时刻真的很珍贵。作为一个孩子，有一种很难复制成成年人的自由感。

However, I also realize that while childhood had its charm, there are aspects of being an adult that I wouldn't want to give up. As an adult, I have more independence and the ability to make my own choices. I can pursue my dreams and goals in a more meaningful way, and I've gained valuable experiences and knowledge that have shaped me into the person I am today. The work I do, although it can be challenging at times, gives me a sense of purpose and accomplishment.
然而，我也意识到，虽然童年有它的魅力，但作为一个成年人，有些方面我不想放弃。作为一个成年人，我有更多的独立性和做出自己选择的能力。我可以以更有意义的方式追求我的梦想和目标，我获得了宝贵的经验和知识，这些经验和知识塑造了我今天的样子。我所做的工作，虽然有时很有挑战性，但给了我一种目标感和成就感。


So, in conclusion, I do have a certain longing for the simplicity, freedom, and fun of childhood. But at the same time, I appreciate the growth and opportunities that come with being an adult. It's a balance, and while I cherish the memories of my childhood, I'm also looking forward to what the future holds as an adult. 
所以，总之，我确实对童年的简单、自由和乐趣有一定的渴望。但与此同时，我也很欣赏成年人的成长和机会。这是一种平衡，当我珍惜童年的回忆时，我也期待着成年后的未来。

3. Who was your best childhood friend? Do you still keep in touch with each other?
> Words may be used:
> close
> twins
> hometown
> leave
> lose
> 
My best childhood friend was Lily. We were extremely close and did almost everything together. We lived in the same neighborhood in our hometown, and from a very young age, we would play in each other's yards, go to the playground, and even attend the same school.
我儿时最好的朋友是莉莉。我们非常亲密，几乎所有事情都在一起做。我们住在家乡的同一个街区，从很小的时候起，我们就在彼此的院子里玩耍，去操场，甚至上同一所学校。


Lily was like a sister to me. We had so many shared memories, such as building sandcastles on the beach during summer vacations and having sleepovers where we would stay up late chatting and giggling. We also had a secret hideout in a small wooded area near our houses where we would go to read our favorite books and share our dreams and secrets.
莉莉对我来说就像一个姐姐。我们有很多共同的回忆，比如暑假在海滩上堆沙堡，在那里过夜聊天和咯咯笑。我们还在家附近的一个小树林里有一个秘密藏身之处，在那里我们会去读我们最喜欢的书，分享我们的梦想和秘密。

However, as we grew older, things changed. Her family decided to move to another city for better job opportunities. It was a really difficult time for both of us when she left. We promised each other that we would stay in touch, and at first, we did. We would call each other on the phone and write letters, sharing updates about our new lives.
然而，随着年龄的增长，情况发生了变化。她的家人决定搬到另一个城市去寻找更好的工作机会。她离开时，我们俩都很难过。我们彼此承诺会保持联系，起初，我们做到了。我们会互相打电话、写信，分享我们新生活的最新情况。

But as time went by, our lives got busier. I started focusing more on my studies and making new friends in school, and she was also adjusting to her new environment. Slowly, we lost touch with each other. I often think about Lily and those wonderful times we had together. Although we are no longer in touch, I will always cherish the memories we created as childhood friends. I sometimes wonder what she is doing now and if she ever thinks about me too. Maybe one day, if I ever go back to my hometown or happen to visit the city where she moved, I might try to find her and see if we can rekindle our friendship. 
但随着时间的推移，我们的生活变得更加繁忙。我开始更加专注于学业，在学校结交新朋友，她也在适应新环境。慢慢地，我们失去了联系。我经常想起莉莉和我们在一起的那些美好时光。虽然我们不再联系了，但我会永远珍惜我们作为儿时朋友创造的回忆。我有时想知道她现在在做什么，她是否也想过我。也许有一天，如果我回到我的家乡，或者碰巧去参观她搬到的城市，我可能会试着找到她，看看我们能否重新点燃我们的友谊。

4. What did you like and dislike about school as a child?
> Words may be used:
> classmates
> class
> learn
> homework
> busy

As a child, there were definitely things I liked and disliked about school. 
小时候，学校里肯定有我喜欢和不喜欢的地方。

On the positive side, I really enjoyed interacting with my classmates. Making friends in school was one of the best parts. We would chat during breaks, play games together in the playground, and help each other with our studies. The sense of camaraderie and the fun we had during those times made school more enjoyable. I also loved certain classes, especially art and music. In art class, I could express my creativity by drawing and painting, and in music class, I got to learn new songs and play simple instruments. It was a great way to relax and have fun while still learning.
从积极的一面来看，我真的很喜欢和同学们互动。在学校交朋友是最好的部分之一。我们会在课间聊天，在操场上一起玩游戏，互相帮助学习。在那段时间里，我们之间的友情和乐趣让学校变得更加愉快。我也喜欢某些课程，尤其是艺术和音乐。在美术课上，我可以通过绘画来表达我的创造力，在音乐课上，我们可以学习新歌和演奏简单的乐器。这是一种在学习的同时放松和娱乐的好方法。

Another thing I liked was the opportunity to learn new things. Whether it was reading stories in language class, solving math problems, or learning about different countries and cultures in social studies, I found it fascinating to expand my knowledge. It gave me a sense of accomplishment when I understood a new concept or mastered a new skill.
我喜欢的另一件事是学习新事物的机会。无论是在语言课上读故事，解决数学问题，还是在社会研究中学习不同国家和文化，我都发现扩展知识很有趣。当我理解了一个新概念或掌握了一项新技能时，它给了我一种成就感。

However, there were also aspects of school that I disliked. One of the biggest things was the amount of homework. After a long day at school, having to come home and spend hours doing assignments was tiring. It made my evenings quite busy, and I often felt like I didn't have enough time to do the things I really wanted to, like playing with my toys or watching cartoons.
然而，学校也有一些方面我不喜欢。最重要的事情之一是作业量。在学校度过漫长的一天后，不得不回家花几个小时做作业，这让我很累。这让我晚上很忙，我经常觉得我没有足够的时间做我真正想做的事情，比如玩玩具或看动画片。

I also didn't like it when the classes were too strict or the teachers were unapproachable. Some teachers had a very rigid way of teaching, and it made the learning environment less enjoyable. I felt more comfortable in classes where the teacher encouraged questions and made the lessons more interactive. Additionally, when there were too many rules and regulations in school, it sometimes made me feel restricted and less able to express myself freely. 
我也不喜欢上课太严格或老师难以接近。一些老师的教学方式非常僵化，这使得学习环境不那么愉快。在老师鼓励提问并使课程更具互动性的课堂上，我感觉更舒服。此外，当学校有太多的规章制度时，有时会让我感到受到限制，无法自由表达自己。

Overall, while school had its ups and downs, I'm grateful for the experiences I had and the knowledge I gained during my childhood years. 
总的来说，虽然学校有起有落，但我很感激我在童年时期的经历和获得的知识。

5. Have you ever been asked the question 'What do you want to do when you grow up'? What was your answer?
> Words may be used:
> scientist/doctor/teacher
> family
> house
> job
> money
> 
Yes, I have been asked the question "What do you want to do when you grow up" many times during my childhood. When I was really young, my answer was often changing.
是的，在我童年的时候，我被问过很多次“你长大后想做什么”的问题。当我很小的时候，我的答案经常改变。

At one point, I wanted to be a doctor. I thought doctors were like heroes. They could help people who were sick and in pain. I imagined myself wearing a white coat, listening carefully to patients' problems, and using my knowledge to make them feel better. I also thought it would be a great job because I could make a difference in people's lives. I wanted to be able to save lives and bring hope to families. And of course, a doctor's job could also bring in a good amount of money, which I thought would be useful to take care of my future family and buy a nice house.
有一次，我想当医生。我以为医生就像英雄。他们可以帮助生病和痛苦的人。我想象自己穿着一件白大褂，仔细倾听病人的问题，并利用我的知识让他们感觉好些。我也认为这将是一份很棒的工作，因为我可以改变人们的生活。我希望能够拯救生命，给家人带来希望。当然，医生的工作也可以带来很多钱，我认为这对照顾我未来的家庭和买一栋漂亮的房子很有用。

As I got a bit older, I considered becoming a teacher. I had some really amazing teachers in school who inspired me a lot. They made learning fun and interesting. I thought if I became a teacher, I could do the same for other children. I could share my knowledge and passion for different subjects with them, and help them grow and develop. Teachers also have a relatively stable job, which was appealing to me. I liked the idea of being in an environment where I could keep learning myself while teaching others.
随着年龄的增长，我考虑成为一名教师。我在学校里有一些非常棒的老师，他们给了我很多启发。他们使学习变得有趣和有趣。我想如果我成为一名教师，我也可以为其他孩子做同样的事情。我可以与他们分享我对不同学科的知识和热情，帮助他们成长和发展。教师也有一份相对稳定的工作，这对我很有吸引力。我喜欢在这样一个环境中，我可以在教别人的同时不断学习自己。

There was also a time when I dreamed of being a scientist. I was fascinated by experiments and discoveries. I imagined working in a laboratory, wearing goggles and conducting all sorts of cool experiments. I wanted to explore the unknown and make new inventions that could benefit the world. I knew that being a scientist would require a lot of hard work and study, but I was willing to put in the effort because I was so interested in the field.
我也曾梦想成为一名科学家。我对实验和发现很着迷。我想象着在实验室里工作，戴着护目镜，进行各种很酷的实验。我想探索未知，创造造福世界的新发明。我知道成为一名科学家需要大量的努力和学习，但我愿意付出努力，因为我对这个领域非常感兴趣。

Looking back, these were all different dreams that reflected my interests and aspirations at different stages of my childhood. And while my career path might have taken a different turn as I grew up, those dreams were an important part of my journey of self-discovery. 
回想起来，这些都是不同的梦想，反映了我童年不同阶段的兴趣和抱负。虽然随着我的成长，我的职业道路可能会发生不同的转折，但这些梦想是我自我发现之旅的重要组成部分。
