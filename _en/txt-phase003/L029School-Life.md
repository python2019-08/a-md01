# 1. What’s your favorite subject and Why?
> Words may be used:
> basketball/football/swimming
> drawing
> writing
> useful
> interesting
> 
My favorite subject is **writing**, and it has become an indispensable part of my "thinking process" for several reasons that blend creativity with practicality.  
我最喜欢的科目是**写作**，它已经成为我“思维过程”中不可或缺的一部分，原因有几个，将创造力与实用性融为一体。  


First, writing is a **powerful tool for connection**. Whether crafting a story, an essay, or even a simple message, it allows me to translate abstract thoughts into tangible ideas that others can understand. For example, when I describe a basketball game I watched—how the players’ movements felt like a choreographed dance, or the crowd’s roar when the winning shot landed—I realize writing bridges the gap between personal experience and shared emotion. It’s like building a bridge between my mind and someone else’s, making moments and ideas transferable.  


首先，写作是一种**强大的连接工具**。无论是创作一个故事、一篇文章，还是一条简单的信息，它都能让我将抽象的想法转化为他人可以理解的有形想法。例如，当我描述我观看的一场篮球比赛时——球员的动作感觉就像编排好的舞蹈，或者当制胜球落地时人群的咆哮——我意识到写作弥合了个人经历和共同情感之间的差距。这就像在我的思想和别人的思想之间架起一座桥梁，让时刻和想法可以转移。  


Second, it’s endlessly **interesting** as a form of self-expression. Unlike drawing, which relies on visual skills, or swimming, which demands physical agility, writing lets me play with language itself. I love experimenting with metaphors (like comparing a sunrise to liquid gold pouring over mountains) or structuring sentences to mimic the rhythm of a heartbeat. Even writing a to-do list feels creative; arranging tasks in a way that turns mundane chores into a mini narrative makes daily life feel more intentional.  


其次，作为一种自我表达的形式，它是无休止的**有趣**。与依赖视觉技能的绘画或需要身体敏捷性的游泳不同，写作让我可以玩语言本身。我喜欢尝试隐喻（比如将日出比作山上倾泻的液态黄金）或构造句子来模仿心跳的节奏。即使是写待办事项清单也会让人觉得有创意；以一种将日常琐事变成迷你叙事的方式安排任务，让日常生活感觉更有目的性。  


What’s more, writing is **incredibly useful** in almost every aspect of life. At work, it helps me clarify complex problems—when I’m stuck on a project, jotting down ideas often reveals solutions I didn’t see before. In personal life, keeping a journal has become my way of processing emotions: writing about a challenging day is like untangling a knotted rope, each sentence loosening the knots of confusion. It’s also a tool for learning; summarizing a book or an article in my own words solidifies knowledge better than simply reading it.  


更重要的是，写作在生活的几乎每个方面都非常有用。在工作中，它帮助我澄清复杂的问题——当我被困在一个项目中时，记下想法往往会揭示出我以前没有看到的解决方案。在个人生活中，写日记已经成为我处理情绪的方式：写一个充满挑战的日子就像解开一根打结的绳子，每一句话都会解开混乱的结。它也是一种学习工具；用我自己的话总结一本书或一篇文章比简单地阅读更能巩固知识。


Lastly, writing is a **flexible companion** that adapts to any mood. When I’m energized, I might pen a lively story about a fictional soccer match where the ball seems to float like a shooting star. When I’m calm, I might write a reflective piece about the beauty of quiet moments, comparing them to the stillness before a raindrop hits a pond. This versatility means writing is never boring—it evolves with me, just as I evolve with it.  


最后，写作是一个**灵活的伴侣**，可以适应任何情绪。当我充满活力时，我可能会写一个生动的故事，讲述一场虚构的足球比赛，球似乎像流星一样漂浮。当我平静的时候，我可能会写一篇关于安静时刻之美的反思性文章，把它们比作雨滴打到池塘前的寂静。这种多样性意味着写作永远不会无聊——它和我一起发展，就像我和它一起发展一样。


In short, writing isn’t just a subject; it’s a lens through which I make sense of the world. It’s the difference between feeling overwhelmed by thoughts and feeling empowered to shape them into something meaningful. Whether I’m describing a sunset, debating an idea, or telling a joke, writing lets me leave a little piece of my perspective in the world—and that’s why it’s my favorite.

简而言之，写作不仅仅是一门学科；这是我理解世界的一个视角。这是被想法淹没的感觉和被赋予将它们塑造成有意义的东西的感觉之间的区别。无论我是在描述日落，辩论一个想法，还是讲笑话，写作都让我在世界上留下了一点自己的观点——这就是为什么它是我最喜欢的。

# 2. Talk about your best memory at school.
> Words may be used:
> friendship
> laugh
> play games
> competition
> test
> 
One of my best school memories revolves around a **science competition** that unexpectedly became a lesson in friendship and teamwork. It was during my sophomore year, when our class was preparing for a regional physics tournament, and the experience taught me that shared struggles can create the strongest bonds.  
我最好的学校记忆之一是关于一场**科学比赛**，这场比赛出乎意料地成为了友谊和团队合作的课程。那是在我大二的时候，我们班正在准备一场区域物理锦标赛，这段经历告诉我，共同的斗争可以建立最牢固的联系。  


The competition required us to build a model bridge using only popsicle sticks and glue, then test its weight-bearing capacity. My group consisted of three classmates I barely knew: Lily, who always sat in the front row taking meticulous notes; Max, who doodled in his textbook during lectures; and me, someone who often felt intimidated by science. On the first day, we argued over designs—Lily wanted a complicated arch, Max suggested a simple flat structure, and I worried we’d never agree. But as the deadline approached, our bickering turned into laughter when our first prototype collapsed under the weight of a single textbook, sending popsicle sticks flying across the lab.  


比赛要求我们只用冰棍和胶水建造一座模型桥，然后测试它的承重能力。我的小组由三个我几乎不认识的同学组成：莉莉，她总是坐在前排做细致的笔记；马克斯在上课时在课本上涂鸦；还有我，一个经常被科学吓倒的人。第一天，我们就设计进行了争论——莉莉想要一个复杂的拱门，马克斯建议一个简单的平面结构，我担心我们永远不会达成一致。但随着最后期限的临近，当我们的第一个原型在一本教科书的重量下倒塌时，我们的争吵变成了笑声，冰棍棒在实验室里飞来飞去。

What followed was a week of late-afternoon practices in the school library. We’d order pizza, take turns holding flashlights under the bridge to check for weak spots, and joke about our “engineering disasters.” One night, when Max accidentally glued his finger to a stick, Lily and I spent ten minutes trying to free him while laughing so hard we cried. Those moments of silliness made us forget we were competing; we were just friends solving a problem together.  


接下来是在学校图书馆进行了一周的下午练习。我们会点披萨，轮流在桥下拿着手电筒检查弱点，并开玩笑说我们的“工程灾难”。一天晚上，当马克斯不小心把手指粘在棍子上时，莉莉和我花了十分钟试图把他救出来，笑得我们都哭了。那些愚蠢的时刻让我们忘记了我们在竞争；我们只是一起解决问题的朋友。  


On the day of the competition, our bridge held an impressive 23 kilograms—more than any other team’s. But the best part wasn’t the trophy; it was when the judges announced our win, and Max grabbed Lily and me in a bear hug, nearly knocking over the display table. Later, we celebrated by playing an impromptu game of tag in the school courtyard, pretending to be superheroes named “Stickman,” “Glue Girl,” and “The Bridge Builder.”  

比赛当天，我们的桥牌承受了23公斤的重量，比其他任何球队都要重。但最棒的不是奖杯；就在裁判宣布我们获胜的时候，马克斯紧紧地抱住莉莉和我，差点把展示台撞倒。后来，我们在学校院子里玩了一场即兴的捉迷藏游戏来庆祝，假装是名为“Stickman”、“Glue Girl”和“the Bridge Builder”的超级英雄


Looking back, that memory stands out because it showed me how **friendship** can bloom in unexpected places. We started as strangers focused on a test, but ended up creating something stronger than any bridge—inside jokes, shared inside jokes, and a bond that lasted long after the competition. Even now, when I see a popsicle stick, I can’t help but smile, remembering how three kids who barely knew each other became a team that laughed, failed, and succeeded together. It’s a reminder that some of life’s best moments happen not when we win, but when we realize we’re not in it alone.

回想起来，那段记忆很突出，因为它向我展示了友谊是如何在意想不到的地方绽放的。我们起初是陌生人，专注于测试，但最终创造了比任何桥梁都更强大的东西——内部笑话、分享内部笑话，以及在比赛结束后持续很长时间的纽带。即使现在，当我看到一根冰棍时，我也忍不住微笑，想起三个几乎不认识的孩子是如何成为一个笑、失败、成功的团队的。这提醒我们，生活中一些最美好的时刻不是在我们获胜时发生的，而是在我们意识到自己并不孤单时发生的。

# 3. Who is your favorite teacher? Why do you like him/her?
> Words may be used:
> beautiful
> funny
> responsible
> strict
> always smile

My favorite teacher is Ms. Chen, my high school English teacher, who left an unforgettable impression on me with her unique blend of responsibility, humor, and genuine care. Even now, I still remember her classroom as a place where learning felt like a warm conversation rather than a one-sided lecture.  

我最喜欢的老师是我的高中英语老师陈女士，她以独特的责任感、幽默感和真诚的关怀给我留下了难忘的印象。即使现在，我仍然记得她的课堂是一个让学习感觉像是一场温暖的对话而不是片面的讲座的地方。  


What made Ms. Chen stand out was her ability to be both **strict** and **funny**. She had a knack for holding us accountable without making us feel pressured. For example, when we struggled with Shakespeare’s sonnets, she didn’t just assign more readings; instead, she dressed up as Juliet and acted out the play’s most dramatic scenes, making us laugh while memorizing lines. Her laughter was contagious—like the time she accidentally knocked over a pile of books during a mimicry of Hamlet’s "to be or not to be" soliloquy, then joked, "Even Shakespeare would trip over his own words!" This balance between discipline and joy made her lessons feel alive.  


让陈脱颖而出的是她既严格又风趣的能力。她有办法让我们承担责任，而不会让我们感到压力。例如，当我们努力学习莎士比亚的十四行诗时，她不仅布置了更多的阅读材料；相反，她打扮成朱丽叶，表演了剧中最戏剧化的场景，让我们在记住台词的同时开怀大笑。她的笑声很有感染力——就像她在模仿哈姆雷特的“生存还是毁灭”独白时不小心打翻了一堆书，然后开玩笑说：“就连莎士比亚也会被自己的话绊倒！”这种纪律和快乐之间的平衡让她的课感充满活力。  


She was also incredibly **responsible**, often staying after school to help students with writing. I still recall how she spent an hour with me revising a college essay, not just correcting grammar but asking questions like, "What made you feel that way?" to help me find my voice. Her red pen marks in my notebook weren’t just edits; they were little encouragements, like "Brilliant metaphor!" or "I can hear your passion here."  


她也非常负责任，经常放学后留下来帮助学生写作。我仍然记得她花了一个小时和我一起复习一篇大学论文，不仅纠正语法，还问了一些问题，比如“是什么让你有这种感觉的？”来帮助我找到自己的声音。她在我笔记本上的红笔标记不仅仅是编辑；它们并不是什么鼓励，比如“绝妙的比喻！”或“我能在这里听到你的激情。”


What I admired most was her **constant smile** and warmth. She greeted every student with a "good morning" that felt sincere, and her office door was always open for anyone needing to talk. Once, when I failed a test and felt discouraged, she pulled out a folder of her own college rejection letters, saying, "See? Even teachers have bad days. What matters is getting back up." That vulnerability made her feel less like a teacher and more like a mentor who truly cared.  


我最钦佩的是她**始终如一的微笑**和温暖。她用真诚的“早上好”问候每个学生，她的办公室门总是为任何需要交谈的人敞开的。有一次，当我考试不及格并感到气馁时，她拿出一个文件夹，里面装着她自己的大学拒绝信，说：“看吧？即使是老师也有糟糕的日子。重要的是重新振作起来。”这种脆弱让她觉得自己不太像一个老师，而更像一个真正关心我的导师。  


Ms. Chen taught me that education isn’t just about facts—it’s about inspiring others to grow. Her classroom was a place where mistakes were celebrated as learning opportunities, where jokes were as welcome as discussions, and where everyone felt seen. Even now, when I read a poem or write an article, I can hear her voice saying, "Find the story that only you can tell." She didn’t just teach English; she taught us how to find courage in our own words, and for that, she’ll always be my favorite.


陈告诉我，教育不仅仅是关于事实，更是关于激励他人成长。她的课堂是一个把错误当作学习机会来庆祝的地方，在这里，笑话和讨论一样受欢迎，每个人都觉得自己被看到了。即使现在，当我读一首诗或写一篇文章时，我也能听到她的声音说：“找到只有你能讲的故事。”她不仅仅是教英语；她教会了我们如何从自己的话中找到勇气，为此，她将永远是我的最爱。

# 4. Describe your school environment.
> Words may be used:
> buildings/classroom
> campus
> safe
> large/green
> peaceful

My school environment is a blend of modern functionality and peaceful nature, creating a space that feels both vibrant and calming. From the moment you walk through the gates, the campus unfolds like a well-designed puzzle, where each building and garden serves a purpose while contributing to a safe, green, and inspiring atmosphere.  
我的学校环境融合了现代功能和宁静的自然，创造了一个充满活力和平静的空间。从你走进大门的那一刻起，校园就像一个精心设计的拼图一样展开，每栋建筑和花园都有其用途，同时营造出安全、绿色和鼓舞人心的氛围。  


The **buildings** are a mix of contemporary architecture and classic design. The main academic building, with its tall glass windows and red-brick facade, houses bright classrooms filled with colorful desks and interactive whiteboards. My favorite classroom is on the third floor, overlooking a courtyard filled with blooming frangipani trees. Sunlight streams in during morning lessons, making the wooden floors glow, and the walls are decorated with student artworks and science project posters, giving each room a lived-in, creative feel. Nearby, the science labs are equipped with stainless steel counters and safety goggles hanging neatly on the walls, while the library feels like a cozy haven, with floor-to-ceiling bookshelves and armchairs tucked into quiet corners.  

**建筑**融合了现代建筑和经典设计。主学术楼有着高大的玻璃窗和红砖外墙，里面是明亮的教室，里面摆满了五颜六色的桌子和互动白板。我最喜欢的教室在三楼，俯瞰着一个开满了鸡蛋花的院子。阳光在上午的课程中照射进来，使木地板闪闪发光，墙壁上装饰着学生艺术品和科学项目海报，给每个房间一种居住的、创造性的感觉。在附近，科学实验室配备了不锈钢柜台和整齐地挂在墙上的护目镜，而图书馆感觉就像一个舒适的避风港，落地窗书架和扶手椅藏在安静的角落里。  


The **campus** itself is remarkably **large** and **green**, with winding paths connecting different areas. A central lawn, dotted with picnic tables and shaded by ancient oak trees, serves as a gathering spot during breaks. Students often sit here to chat, read, or play board games, while the scent of jasmine from nearby flower beds fills the air. There’s also a small vegetable garden maintained by the biology club, where rows of tomatoes and herbs grow beside a wooden sign that reads, "Grown with care." The sports field, surrounded by a running track, is always bustling with activity—students playing soccer, practicing hurdles, or simply lying on the grass watching clouds.  


**校园**本身非常**大**和**绿色**，蜿蜒的小径连接着不同的区域。中央草坪上点缀着野餐桌，被古老的橡树遮蔽，是休息时的聚会场所。学生们经常坐在这里聊天、阅读或玩棋盘游戏，而附近花坛的茉莉花香弥漫在空气中。还有一个由生物俱乐部维护的小菜园，在那里，一排排西红柿和香草生长在一块写着“小心种植”的木牌旁边。运动场周围有跑道，总是热闹非凡——学生们踢足球、练习跨栏，或者只是躺在草地上看云朵。  


What makes the environment truly special is how **peaceful** and **safe** it feels. Security guards patrol the gates, and teachers are always visible in the hallways, creating a sense of protection. During exams, the campus takes on a calm hush, with only the sound of pencils scratching paper and the occasional bird chirping outside. But even on busy days, there’s a rhythm to the chaos—lunchtime laughter echoing in the canteen, the hum of bicycles as students ride to after-school clubs, and the gentle clatter of books being stacked at the end of the day.  


让环境真正特别的是它给人的**和平**和**安全**的感觉。保安在大门上巡逻，走廊里总是能看到老师，营造出一种保护感。考试期间，校园里一片寂静，只有铅笔刮纸的声音，偶尔还有鸟儿在外面啁啾。但即使在繁忙的日子里，混乱也有节奏——午餐时间的笑声在食堂回荡，学生们骑着自行车去课外俱乐部时的嗡嗡声，以及一天结束时堆放的书籍的轻微咔嗒声。  


One of my favorite spots is the rooftop garden on the arts building. It’s a hidden gem with potted succulents, string lights, and a view of the entire campus. During stressful weeks, I’d come here to sketch or listen to music, watching the sun set behind the sports field while the campus slowly quieted down. The combination of green spaces, well-lit buildings, and friendly faces made school feel not just like a place to learn, but a second home—safe, vibrant, and full of life.


我最喜欢的景点之一是艺术楼的屋顶花园。这是一个隐藏的宝石，有盆栽多肉植物、串灯和整个校园的景色。在紧张的几周里，我会来这里画画或听音乐，看着太阳在运动场后面落下，而校园慢慢安静下来。绿地、光线充足的建筑和友好的面孔的结合让学校感觉不仅仅是一个学习的地方，更是一个安全、充满活力、充满生机的第二个家。

# 5. Do you like to take part in different kinds of activities? Why?
> Words may be used:
> new friends
> healthy
> skill
> outgoing
> active
> 
Yes, I thoroughly enjoy taking part in different kinds of activities—it’s like opening a door to new worlds, each offering unique joys and benefits that enrich both my life and mindset. Whether it’s trying a new sport, joining a workshop, or volunteering, each activity feels like a puzzle piece that helps me understand myself and others better.  
是的，我非常喜欢参加各种各样的活动——这就像打开了一扇通往新世界的大门，每一个都提供了独特的快乐和好处，丰富了我的生活和心态。无论是尝试一项新运动、参加一个工作坊还是做志愿者，每一项活动都让我感觉像是一块拼图，帮助我更好地了解自己和他人。  

First, participating in activities is a great way to make **new friends** and build connections. I remember joining a hiking club last summer; though I’d never met anyone there, we bonded instantly over shared struggles up steep trails and excitement over spotting a rare bird. By the end of the trip, we were swapping stories around a campfire, and those strangers became friends I still hike with today. Activities create natural opportunities to connect over common interests, breaking the ice much more easily than formal introductions. Even in a painting class, laughing together while accidentally splattering paint or admiring each other’s unfinished canvases fosters a sense of camaraderie that’s hard to replicate elsewhere.  

首先，参加活动是结交**新朋友**和建立联系的好方法。我记得去年夏天加入了一个徒步旅行俱乐部；尽管我在那里从未见过任何人，但我们在陡峭的小径上共同奋斗，在发现一只稀有鸟类的兴奋中立即建立了联系。旅行结束时，我们在篝火旁交换故事，那些陌生人成了我今天仍然一起徒步旅行的朋友。活动创造了自然的机会，让人们在共同的兴趣上建立联系，比正式的介绍更容易打破僵局。即使在绘画课上，在不小心泼洒颜料或欣赏彼此未完成的画布时一起大笑，也能培养出一种在其他地方很难复制的同志情谊。  


Engaging in diverse activities also keeps me **active** and **healthy**, both physically and mentally. Playing basketball with colleagues after work not only gets my heart pumping but also helps me unwind from stress. Similarly, trying a meditation workshop taught me how to calm my mind, which has been invaluable on busy days. I’ve found that switching between physical activities (like swimming) and mental ones (like writing workshops) creates a balanced routine that keeps me energized. Once, I took a pottery class as a whim, and the act of shaping clay with my hands—something so different from my daily tasks—left me feeling both relaxed and invigorated.  

参与各种各样的活动也让我在身体和精神上保持活跃和健康。下班后和同事打篮球不仅让我心跳加速，还有助于我从压力中放松。同样，尝试冥想工作坊教会了我如何冷静下来，这在繁忙的日子里是无价的。我发现，在体育活动（如游泳）和脑力活动（如写作工作坊）之间切换，可以创造一种平衡的日常活动，让我保持活力。有一次，我一时兴起上了一门陶艺课，用手塑造粘土的行为——这与我的日常任务截然不同——让我感到既放松又充满活力。  

Beyond that, each activity offers a chance to learn new **skills** that surprise me. I never thought I’d enjoy public speaking until I joined a debate club, where practicing arguments taught me how to think on my feet and communicate clearly. Even simple activities, like gardening, have taught me patience (waiting for seeds to sprout) and problem-solving (figuring out why my plants weren’t growing). These skills often spill over into other areas of life—for example, the teamwork I learned in a group dance class has made me a better collaborator at work.  


除此之外，每项活动都提供了一个学习新技能的机会，这让我感到惊讶。在加入辩论俱乐部之前，我从未想过自己会喜欢公开演讲，在那里练习辩论教会了我如何站起来思考和清晰沟通。即使是简单的活动，比如园艺，也教会了我耐心（等待种子发芽）和解决问题（弄清楚我的植物为什么不生长）。这些技能经常会渗透到生活的其他领域，例如，我在团体舞蹈课上学到的团队合作使我在工作中成为更好的合作者。

Most importantly, trying new activities helps me stay **outgoing** and open-minded. Stepping outside my comfort zone—whether it’s performing in a school play or volunteering at a charity event—pushes me to overcome shyness and embrace new experiences. I remember feeling terrified before my first improv comedy class, but laughing at my own mistakes and cheering on others taught me that vulnerability can be empowering. Now, I approach new activities with curiosity rather than fear, knowing that even if I’m not good at something right away, the journey is worth it.  
最重要的是，尝试新的活动有助于我保持外向和开放的心态。走出我的舒适区——无论是在学校演出还是在慈善活动中做志愿者——都会让我克服害羞，拥抱新的体验。我记得在上第一节即兴喜剧课之前，我感到很害怕，但嘲笑自己的错误并为他人加油让我意识到，脆弱可以赋予我力量。现在，我带着好奇心而不是恐惧来参加新的活动，知道即使我现在不擅长某件事，这段旅程也是值得的。


In the end, activities are like windows that let in different kinds of light. They introduce me to people I might never meet, teach me lessons I can’t learn in books, and remind me that life is meant to be lived actively, not passively. Whether I’m hiking a mountain, painting a landscape, or playing a board game with friends, each activity is a chance to grow, connect, and find joy in the present moment—and that’s why I love them.
最后，活动就像窗户，可以让不同种类的光线进入。他们把我介绍给我可能永远不会遇到的人，教给我书本上学不到的东西，提醒我生活应该是积极的，而不是被动的。无论我是爬山、画风景还是和朋友玩棋盘游戏，每一项活动都是成长、联系和在当下找到快乐的机会——这就是我爱他们的原因。
