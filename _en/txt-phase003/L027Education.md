# 1. Which one is the most important -- primary school,middle school, high school or college? Why?
> Words may be used:
> beginning
> stage

> teacher/friends
> view
> 
Determining the "most important" educational stage—primary school, middle school, high school, or college—depends on how we define "importance." However, each stage serves unique roles, and **high school** often stands out as a pivotal period for several compelling reasons.  
确定“最重要”的教育阶段——小学、中学、高中或大学——取决于我们如何定义“重要性”。然而，每个阶段都有其独特的作用，**高中**往往因几个令人信服的原因而成为关键时期。  


### **1. Primary School: Laying the Foundation**  
The primary school stage is the **beginning** of formal education, where children acquire essential literacy, numeracy, and social skills. Teachers here play a key role in shaping basic learning habits and curiosity. Friends at this age help form early social bonds, and the stage sets a foundation for academic confidence. However, its importance lies more in establishing routine than in defining long-term directions.  
### 1.小学：奠基**
小学阶段是正规教育的**开始**，儿童在这里获得基本的识字、算术和社交技能。这里的教师在塑造基本的学习习惯和好奇心方面发挥着关键作用。这个年龄段的朋友有助于形成早期的社会关系，这个阶段为学术自信奠定了基础。然而，它的重要性更多地在于建立常规，而不是确定长期方向。  


### **2. Middle School: Navigating Transition**  
Middle school acts as a bridge between childhood and adolescence. Students encounter more complex subjects and begin to develop personal identities. Peer influence grows, and teachers often guide students through emotional and social challenges. Yet, this stage is transitional, with many students still exploring interests rather than making definitive choices.  
### 2.中学：过渡导航**
中学是儿童和青少年之间的桥梁。学生会遇到更复杂的科目，并开始发展个人身份。同伴的影响越来越大，教师经常引导学生应对情感和社会挑战。然而，这个阶段是过渡性的，许多学生仍在探索兴趣，而不是做出明确的选择。  


### **3. College: Preparing for Adulthood**  
College is undeniably crucial for professional development, as it offers specialized knowledge and skills for career paths. Students gain independence, build professional networks, and broaden their worldviews through diverse experiences. However, its importance is contingent on the choices made **before** college—namely, the preparation that determines access to higher education.  
### 3.大学：为成年做准备**
大学无疑对职业发展至关重要，因为它为职业道路提供了专业知识和技能。学生通过多样化的经历获得独立性，建立专业网络，拓宽世界观。然而，它的重要性取决于**大学之前做出的选择，即决定接受高等教育机会的准备。  


### **4. High School: The Key to Future Trajectories**  
High school stands out as the most critical stage for several reasons:  
- **Academic and Career Orientation**: It is the first time students face specialized courses (e.g., sciences, humanities, arts) and begin to align their studies with potential careers. Standardized exams (e.g., college entrance tests) during this period directly impact access to higher education.  
- **Intellectual and Emotional Maturity**: Adolescents in high school develop critical thinking skills and form deeper values. Teachers and mentors here often influence long-term goals, while friends shape social identities and peer networks that may last a lifetime.  
- **Gateway to Adulthood**: Decisions made in high school—such as choosing majors, participating in extracurriculars, or pursuing internships—lay the groundwork for college and beyond. Unlike primary or middle school, high school performance directly affects future opportunities.  
### 4.高中：未来发展的关键**
高中是最关键的阶段，原因有几个：
-**学术和职业方向**：这是学生第一次面对专业课程（如科学、人文、艺术），并开始将他们的学习与潜在的职业相结合。在此期间，标准化考试（如大学入学考试）直接影响到接受高等教育的机会。  
-**智力和情感成熟**：高中青少年发展批判性思维能力，形成更深层次的价值观。这里的老师和导师经常影响长期目标，而朋友则塑造了可能持续一生的社会认同和同伴网络。  
-**成年之路**：高中时做出的决定——比如选择专业、参加课外活动或实习——为大学及以后的生活奠定了基础。与小学或中学不同，高中成绩直接影响未来的机会。  


### **Conclusion: High School as the Pivotal Stage**  
While every educational phase is important, high school serves as a **key** juncture where academic rigor, personal identity, and future plans converge. It bridges the foundational learning of childhood with the specialized focus of adulthood, making it a critical stage for shaping long-term success and worldview. Without a strong high school foundation, the opportunities available in college and beyond may be significantly limited.
### **结论：高中是关键阶段**
虽然每个教育阶段都很重要，但高中是学术严谨性、个人身份和未来计划交汇的关键时刻。它将童年的基础学习与成年的专业重点联系起来，使其成为塑造长期成功和世界观的关键阶段。如果没有坚实的高中基础，大学及以后的机会可能会非常有限。

### **1. Knowledge: The Foundation of Curiosity**  
Education introduced me to vast realms of knowledge—from mathematics and literature to science and history. But beyond memorizing facts, it sparked a **curiosity** to question, explore, and understand the "why" behind ideas. For example, studying literature showed me how stories reflect human experiences, while science taught me the logic of problem-solving. 教育让我接触到了广阔的知识领域——从数学和文学到科学和历史。但除了记忆事实外，它还激发了人们对质疑、探索和理解想法背后的“为什么”的好奇心。例如，学习文学向我展示了故事是如何反映人类经验的，而科学教会了我解决问题的逻辑。这些知识不仅仅是考试的工具；这是我分析挑战和寻求创新的一个视角。  


### **2. Learning Methods: The Art of Adaptation**  
Perhaps the most practical gift of education is **learning how to learn**. Whether through trial-and-error, group discussions, or self-study, I discovered that effective learning requires patience and flexibility. For instance, breaking complex tasks into smaller steps (like solving a math problem or writing an essay) taught me to tackle overwhelm. I also learned the value of feedback—teachers’ critiques and peer reviews helped me refine my work and embrace growth rather than perfection.  
### 2.学习方法：适应的艺术**
也许教育最实用的礼物就是学习如何学习。无论是通过试错法、小组讨论还是自学，我发现有效的学习都需要耐心和灵活性。例如，将复杂的任务分解为更小的步骤（比如解决数学问题或写一篇文章）教会了我如何应对压倒性的挑战。我也学到了反馈的价值——老师的批评和同行评审帮助我改进了工作，拥抱了成长而不是完美。  


### **3. Friendship: The Power of Connection**  
Classrooms and campuses were where I formed some of my deepest friendships. Working on group projects, sharing late-night study sessions, or simply chatting between classes showed me that **friendship** is about mutual support and shared growth. These relationships taught me to listen, compromise, and celebrate differences. Even conflicts with friends became lessons in empathy and communication—skills that now strengthen my relationships beyond school.  
### 3.友谊：连接的力量**
教室和校园是我建立最深厚友谊的地方。在小组项目上工作，分享深夜学习课程，或者只是在课堂之间聊天，都让我意识到**友谊**是关于相互支持和共同成长的。这些关系教会了我倾听、妥协和庆祝差异。甚至与朋友的冲突也成为了同理心和沟通的课程——这些技能现在加强了我在学校以外的关系。  


### **4. Kindness and How to Behave: Ethics in Action**  
Education wasn’t just about what I learned from books, but how I learned to **behave** as a person. Teachers modeled professionalism and compassion, while interactions with peers taught me the importance of kindness. For example, helping a classmate who struggled with a subject or standing up against unfairness showed me that integrity matters as much as intelligence. These lessons shaped my values: to be respectful, accountable, and considerate of others.  
### 4.善良与行为：行动中的伦理**
教育不仅仅是我从书本上学到了什么，而是我如何学会做人。老师们培养了专业精神和同情心，而与同龄人的互动教会了我善良的重要性。例如，帮助一位在某个科目上遇到困难或反对不公平的同学，让我意识到正直与智慧同样重要。这些教训塑造了我的价值观：尊重他人、负责和体谅他人。  


### **5. Resilience: Overcoming Challenges**  
Every educational stage brought obstacles—failing a test, struggling with a difficult subject, or balancing responsibilities. These experiences taught me that setbacks are temporary and that **perseverance** is key. For instance, when I struggled with a programming course, re-reading materials, seeking help from tutors, and practicing daily helped me master it. This resilience now helps me face career and life challenges with confidence.  
### 5.韧性：克服挑战**
每一个教育阶段都会带来障碍——考试不及格、学习困难的科目或平衡责任。这些经历告诉我，挫折是暂时的，**毅力**是关键。例如，当我在编程课程中遇到困难时，重读材料、向导师寻求帮助以及每天练习帮助我掌握了它。这种韧性现在帮助我自信地面对职业和生活挑战。  


### **Conclusion: Education as a Lifelong Journey**  
In essence, my education hasn’t just given me facts or skills; it’s molded me into someone who values **continuous learning**, cherishes human connection, and strives to act with kindness. Whether through knowledge, friendships, or ethical behavior, each lesson has become a cornerstone of who I am—and a reminder that education’s true power lies in shaping not just what we know, but how we live.
### **结论：教育是一个终身的旅程**
从本质上讲，我的教育不仅给了我事实或技能；它把我塑造成一个重视持续学习、珍视人际关系、努力行善的人。无论是通过知识、友谊还是道德行为，每一节课都成为了我是谁的基石，并提醒我们，教育的真正力量不仅在于塑造我们所知道的，还在于塑造我们的生活方式。

# 3. Do you agree that "parents are the best teachers for children"'? Why?

Whether parents are the "best teachers" for children is a nuanced question, but their role as foundational educators is undeniable. Here’s why their influence often surpasses other teachers—backed by the words and concepts you provided:  
父母是否是孩子的“最好的老师”是一个微妙的问题，但他们作为基础教育者的作用是不可否认的。这就是为什么他们的影响力往往超过其他老师的原因——这得益于你提供的词语和概念：


### **1. Parents Shape Core Values Through Words and Practice**  
Children learn first from the **words** and **actions** of their parents, who model behaviors long before formal education begins. For example, if a parent emphasizes honesty by admitting their own mistakes, the child internalizes that integrity matters. This "practice" of values is more powerful than classroom lectures because it’s embedded in daily life. Research shows that children who observe their parents volunteering or showing kindness are likelier to adopt similar habits, proving that parents act as living textbooks for ethics and empathy.  
### 1.父母通过言语和实践塑造核心价值观**
孩子们首先从父母的**言语**和**行为**中学习，父母早在正式教育开始之前就已经成为行为的榜样。例如，如果父母通过承认自己的错误来强调诚实，孩子就会内化诚信的重要性。这种价值观的“实践”比课堂讲座更强大，因为它植根于日常生活中。研究表明，观察父母自愿或表现出善意的孩子更有可能养成类似的习惯，这证明父母是道德和同理心的活教科书。  


### **2. The Unmatched Bond of Trust and Time**  
Parents spend more **time** with children than any teacher, creating a unique **trust** that fosters open learning. In this safe space, children feel comfortable asking vulnerable questions ("Why is the sky blue?") or admitting mistakes, which encourages curiosity. Unlike teachers, who manage many students, parents can tailor lessons to their child’s pace and interests—whether it’s explaining math through a favorite game or nurturing a passion for art. This one-on-one attention allows for personalized guidance that schools often can’t replicate.  
### 2.无与伦比的信任和时间纽带**
父母花在孩子身上的时间比任何老师都多，这创造了一种独特的**信任**，促进了开放式学习。在这个安全的空间里，孩子们可以放心地问一些易受伤害的问题（“为什么天是蓝色的？”）或承认错误，这会激发好奇心。与管理许多学生的教师不同，家长可以根据孩子的节奏和兴趣量身定制课程——无论是通过最喜欢的游戏解释数学，还是培养对艺术的热情。这种一对一的关注可以提供学校通常无法复制的个性化指导。  


### **3. Childhood: The Foundation That Lasts a Lifetime**  
The **childhood** years, when parents have the most influence, are critical for shaping a child’s worldview. Studies on early childhood development show that a parent’s emotional support, language exposure, and daily routines directly impact brain development and academic readiness. For instance, a parent who reads to their child from infancy not only boosts literacy skills but also creates a lifelong love of learning. These early lessons form a foundation that affects how children approach challenges, relationships, and even their own self-worth.  
### 3.童年：终生的根基**
**童年**年是父母影响最大的时期，对塑造孩子的世界观至关重要。对幼儿发展的研究表明，父母的情感支持、语言接触和日常生活直接影响大脑发育和学业准备。例如，一位从婴儿时期就给孩子读书的父母不仅可以提高识字能力，还可以培养终身的学习热情。这些早期课程构成了一个基础，影响着孩子们如何应对挑战、人际关系，甚至他们自己的自我价值。  


### **4. Parents Teach Beyond Academics: Life Skills and Identity**  
While teachers focus on academic subjects, parents impart essential **life skills**—from cooking and budgeting to managing emotions. They also help children form their identity: a parent who celebrates cultural traditions or encourages individuality helps the child understand who they are and where they belong. This guidance is especially vital during adolescence, when peer pressure and societal expectations can confuse young minds. A parent’s advice in these moments can ground a child in their values, acting as a compass through life’s complexities.  
### 4.父母教超越学术：生活技能和身份**
当教师专注于学术科目时，父母传授基本的**生活技能**——从烹饪和预算到情绪管理。他们还帮助孩子形成自己的身份：庆祝文化传统或鼓励个性的父母帮助孩子了解他们是谁以及他们属于哪里。这种指导在青春期尤为重要，因为同龄人的压力和社会期望会让年轻人感到困惑。父母在这些时刻的建议可以让孩子树立自己的价值观，成为穿越生活复杂性的指南针。  


### **5. The Limitations: Why "Best" Is Not Absolute**  
That said, the term "best" oversimplifies the role of other educators. Teachers, coaches, and mentors bring specialized knowledge, diverse perspectives, and structured learning that parents may not provide. For example, a music teacher can offer professional training a parent lacks, while a coach teaches teamwork in a way that complements a parent’s lessons. Parents are best at **foundational** education, but collaboration with other teachers enriches a child’s growth.  
### 5.局限性：为什么“最好”不是绝对的**
也就是说，“最好”一词过于简化了其他教育工作者的作用。教师、教练和导师带来了父母可能无法提供的专业知识、多样化的观点和结构化的学习。例如，音乐老师可以提供家长缺乏的专业培训，而教练则以一种补充家长课程的方式教授团队合作。家长最擅长基础教育，但与其他老师的合作可以丰富孩子的成长。  


### **Conclusion: Parents as First Teachers, Not Sole Teachers**  
Parents are indeed the **first and most influential teachers** for children, shaping their character, skills, and worldview during the formative years. Their unique blend of time, trust, and personal connection allows them to instill values and life lessons that last a lifetime. However, the title of "best" is better replaced by "essential"—for while parents lay the groundwork, a child’s learning journey thrives when supported by a village of educators, each contributing in their own vital way.
### **结论：家长是第一位老师，不是唯一的老师**
父母确实是孩子们的第一位也是最有影响力的老师，在成长期塑造他们的性格、技能和世界观。他们独特的时间、信任和个人关系的融合使他们能够灌输终生的价值观和人生课程。然而，“最好”的标题最好改为“基本”——因为当父母奠定基础时，孩子的学习之旅在一个由教育工作者组成的村庄的支持下蓬勃发展，每个人都以自己重要的方式做出贡献。

# 4. what are the problems of recent school education?
> Words may be used:
> fast
> much
> little
> score
> moral
Recent school education faces several challenges that hinder its ability to foster well-rounded development. Here are the key issues, addressed with the words provided:  
最近的学校教育面临着几个挑战，阻碍了其促进全面发展的能力。以下是用所提供的词语解决的关键问题：


### **1. Overemphasis on Scores Over Holistic Learning**  
Schools often prioritize **scores** and exam results over practical skills or critical thinking. This应试 education (examination-oriented education) mindset pushes students to memorize facts for tests rather than engaging with knowledge deeply. For example, students may cram for math exams without understanding how to apply formulas in real-life scenarios. The focus on grades creates stress and reduces curiosity, as learning becomes a means to an end rather than a joyful process.  
### 1.过度强调分数而非整体学习**
学校通常会优先考虑**分数**和考试成绩，而不是实践技能或批判性思维。 This应试 教育（应试教育）心态促使学生记住考试事实，而不是深入学习知识。例如，学生可能会在不了解如何在现实生活中应用公式的情况下为数学考试死记硬背。对成绩的关注会产生压力，降低好奇心，因为学习成为达到目的的手段，而不是一个快乐的过程。  


### **2. Excessive Workload and Fast-paced Curriculum**  
The curriculum has become increasingly **fast** and overwhelming, with students expected to cover **much** content in limited time. Textbooks are packed with information, and teachers rush through lessons to meet exam deadlines. This leaves little room for students to reflect, ask questions, or explore topics independently. For instance, a high school science course might skip hands-on experiments to finish the syllabus, depriving students of practical learning. The fast pace also leads to burnout, as students spend hours on homework each night, sacrificing sleep and hobbies.  
### 2.工作量过大和课程节奏过快**
课程变得越来越快速和压倒性，预计学生将在有限的时间内涵盖**很多**内容。教科书里塞满了信息，老师们匆匆忙忙地赶在考试截止日期前完成课程。这使得学生几乎没有独立思考、提问或探索主题的空间。例如，高中科学课程可能会跳过动手实验来完成教学大纲，从而剥夺学生的实践学习机会。快节奏也会导致倦怠，因为学生每晚要花几个小时做作业，牺牲睡眠和爱好。  


### **3. Inadequate Focus on Moral and Ethical Education**  
While academic subjects dominate, **moral** education often takes a backseat. Schools may lack structured programs to teach empathy, integrity, or social responsibility. For example, lessons on kindness or conflict resolution are rarely prioritized, leaving students to learn ethics primarily from parents or society. This gap can lead to graduates who excel academically but struggle with interpersonal relationships or ethical decision-making in their careers.  
### 3.对道德和伦理教育关注不足**
虽然学术科目占主导地位，但**道德**教育往往退居次要地位。学校可能缺乏结构化的课程来教授同理心、正直或社会责任。例如，关于善良或解决冲突的课程很少被优先考虑，让学生主要从父母或社会那里学习道德。这种差距可能会导致毕业生在学业上表现出色，但在职业生涯中难以处理人际关系或道德决策。  


### **4. Little Emphasis on Individual Needs and Skills**  
Classroom teaching tends to follow a one-size-fits-all model, offering **little** personalized attention to students’ unique strengths or learning styles. Teachers, often overwhelmed by large class sizes, can’t tailor lessons for each student. For instance, a visually oriented learner might struggle in a lecture-heavy math class, while a creative student may feel unchallenged in standardized writing assignments. This lack of customization stifles creativity and makes it hard for students to discover their true passions.  
### 4.很少强调个人需求和技能**
课堂教学往往遵循一刀切的模式，对学生的独特优势或学习风格给予**很少**的个性化关注。教师们经常被大班学生淹没，无法为每个学生量身定制课程。例如，一个以视觉为导向的学习者可能会在繁重的数学课上遇到困难，而一个有创造力的学生可能会在标准化的写作作业中感到不受挑战。这种缺乏定制的情况扼杀了创造力，使学生很难发现自己真正的激情。  


### **5. Disconnect Between Education and Real-World Skills**  
School curricula often focus on theoretical knowledge rather than preparing students for practical life challenges. There’s **little** emphasis on skills like financial management, emotional intelligence, or problem-solving in real-world contexts. For example, many high school graduates enter college without knowing how to budget or manage stress, because these topics weren’t part of their school education. This disconnect leaves students ill-equipped to navigate adulthood, despite their academic achievements.  
### 5.教育与现实世界技能之间的脱节**
学校课程往往侧重于理论知识，而不是为学生应对实际生活挑战做好准备。很少强调财务管理、情商或在现实世界中解决问题等技能。例如，许多高中毕业生进入大学时不知道如何预算或管理压力，因为这些话题不是他们学校教育的一部分。这种脱节使学生无法适应成年，尽管他们取得了学业成就。  


### **Conclusion: The Need for Balanced Education**  
Recent school education struggles with a system that prioritizes speed, scores, and rote learning over moral growth, individual needs, and practical skills. To address these issues, education must shift toward a more balanced approach—one that values character development, personalized learning, and real-world relevance alongside academic excellence.
### **结论：均衡教育的必要性**
最近的学校教育与一个优先考虑速度、分数和死记硬背而不是道德成长、个人需求和实践技能的系统作斗争。为了解决这些问题，教育必须转向更平衡的方法——一种重视性格发展、个性化学习和现实世界相关性以及学术卓越的方法。

One schoolmate who profoundly influenced me was a classmate named Lily, whose **hardworking** nature and **wise** perspective reshaped how I approached learning and life. Here’s how her example became a guiding light:  
一位对我影响深远的同学是莉莉，她勤奋的天性和智慧的视角重塑了我对待学习和生活的方式。以下是她的例子如何成为一盏指路明灯：


### **1. A Model of Diligence: Redefining Hard Work**  
Lily wasn’t just academically excellent—she taught me that **hard work** isn’t about sheer hours spent studying, but about intentional, focused effort. While others crammed the night before exams, she broke down topics into daily tasks, using flashcards and mind maps to reinforce understanding. I once asked her why she spent weekends volunteering at a community library instead of studying, and she replied, “Learning isn’t only from books; it’s from seeing how knowledge helps others.” Her routine showed me that balance between academics and real-world application makes hard work meaningful, not exhausting.  
### 1.勤奋的典范：重新定义勤奋**
莉莉不仅在学业上表现出色，她还告诉我，努力工作不是纯粹的学习时间，而是有意识、有重点的努力。当其他人在考试前一晚挤得水泄不通时，她把话题分解成日常任务，使用抽认卡和思维导图来加强理解。我曾问她为什么周末在社区图书馆做志愿者而不是学习，她回答说：“学习不仅仅来自书本；它来自于看到知识如何帮助他人。”她的日常生活告诉我，学术和现实应用之间的平衡使努力工作有意义，而不是令人精疲力竭。  


### **2. Wisdom in Listening: Changing My Thought Patterns**  
What set Lily apart was her **wise** ability to turn challenges into lessons. When she failed a math test (a rare occurrence), she didn’t dwell on the score. Instead, she asked the teacher for a detailed review, then created a错题本 (mistake notebook) to analyze each error. “Mistakes are just questions you haven’t answered yet,” she told me. This shifted my mindset from fearing failure to seeing it as a puzzle to solve. Her calm reflection taught me that intelligence isn’t about never making errors, but about using them to grow.  
### 2.倾听的智慧：改变我的思维模式**
莉莉的与众不同之处在于她将挑战转化为教训的智慧。当她数学考试不及格时（这种情况很少见），她没有过多考虑分数。相反，她要求老师进行详细的复习，然后创建了一个错题本 （错误笔记本）分析每个错误。她告诉我：“错误只是你还没有回答的问题。”这让我的心态从害怕失败转变为将其视为一个需要解决的难题。她平静的反思告诉我，智慧不是永远不犯错误，而是利用错误来成长。  


### **3. Encouragement Through Action: Leading by Example**  
Lily’s support wasn’t just words—she **encouraged** others through tangible help. When I struggled with a physics project, she spent hours explaining concepts, not by giving answers but by asking guiding questions: “What if you try this approach? How does this equation relate to the problem?” Her patience showed me that true encouragement is about empowering others to find their own solutions, not fixing problems for them. She even started a study group where everyone took turns teaching, proving that sharing knowledge strengthens it.  
### 3.以行动激励：以身作则**
莉莉的支持不仅仅是言语，她还通过切实的帮助鼓励他人。当我在一个物理项目上遇到困难时，她花了几个小时解释概念，不是通过给出答案，而是通过提出指导性问题：“如果你尝试这种方法怎么办？这个方程与问题有什么关系？”她的耐心告诉我，真正的鼓励是让别人找到自己的解决方案，而不是为他们解决问题。她甚至成立了一个研究小组，每个人轮流教学，证明分享知识可以增强它。


### **4. A Lesson in Kindness: Beyond Academic Success**  
Perhaps her greatest influence was modeling that success includes **kindness**. Despite her busy schedule, she always made time to listen to classmates’ problems—whether academic or personal. I remember one day when she skipped a club meeting to help a friend whose family was going through a crisis. “Grades fade, but kindness stays,” she said. This prioritization of empathy over achievement taught me that how we treat others defines our legacy more than any score.  
### 4.善良的一课：超越学术成功**
也许她最大的影响是塑造成功包括**善良**的形象。尽管她日程繁忙，但她总是抽出时间听同学们的问题——无论是学术问题还是个人问题。我记得有一天，她跳过了一个俱乐部会议，去帮助一个家人正经历危机的朋友。她说：“成绩会变差，但善良会留下来。”。这种将同理心置于成就之上的优先地位教会了我，我们如何对待他人比任何分数都更能定义我们的遗产。  


### **5. Lasting Impact: How Her Example Shaped Me**  
Years later, Lily’s lessons still resonate:  
- **Work smarter, not harder**: I adopted her study methods, focusing on understanding over memorization.  
- **Embrace mistakes**: My “mistake notebook” (now digital) has become a cornerstone of my learning.  
- **Lift others up**: I volunteer as a tutor, using her question-based approach to help students.  
- **Value kindness**: I prioritize empathy in both personal and professional relationships, remembering that education’s purpose is to enrich lives, not just resumes.  
### 5.持久的影响：她的榜样如何塑造了我**
多年后，莉莉的教训仍然引起共鸣：
-**更聪明地工作，而不是更努力**：我采用了她的学习方法，专注于理解而不是记忆。  
-**拥抱错误**：我的“错误笔记本”（现已数字化）已成为我学习的基石。  
-**激励他人**：我自愿担任导师，用她基于问题的方法帮助学生。

Lily wasn’t a teacher or a celebrity—just a classmate who showed that **influence** comes from living intentionally. Her blend of wisdom, hard work, and kindness proved that the most profound lessons often come from those who lead by example, reminding us that education is as much about shaping character as it is about acquiring knowledge.
### **结论：深思熟虑的同伴的力量**
莉莉不是老师，也不是名人，只是一个同学，她表明**影响力**来自有意识的生活。她智慧、勤奋和善良的结合证明，最深刻的教训往往来自那些以身作则的人，提醒我们教育既是为了获取知识，也是为了塑造性格。
