3. Can a hobby be dangerous?
> Words may be used:
> bungee jumping
> diving
> climbing
> motorcycle
> train
> -----------------

Yes, a hobby can be dangerous. There are several hobbies that carry inherent risks and can put people in potentially life - threatening situations.
Bungee jumping is a thrilling hobby, but it comes with significant risks. The elastic cord used in bungee jumping must be in perfect condition, and the equipment has to be properly attached. If there are any flaws in the cord or the attachment, a person could fall and suffer serious injuries or even death.
Diving, especially deep - sea diving, is another dangerous hobby. Divers are exposed to the risk of decompression sickness, also known as "the bends." This occurs when divers ascend too quickly, causing nitrogen bubbles to form in their blood. There's also the risk of running out of oxygen, encountering dangerous marine life, or getting trapped in underwater structures.
Climbing, whether it's rock climbing or mountain climbing, is full of perils. Climbers can fall from great heights, which can lead to severe fractures, head injuries, or worse. They also face the challenges of changing weather conditions, like sudden storms or extreme cold, which can cause hypothermia.
Motorcycle riding is a popular hobby, but it's far from safe. Motorcycles offer less protection than cars. Riders are more exposed to the elements and are at a higher risk of being involved in accidents. A minor mistake on the road can result in serious injuries, such as broken bones, road rash, or brain damage.
Even a hobby like trying to explore train tracks can be extremely dangerous. Trains are large and heavy, and they can't stop quickly. People who trespass on train tracks may not be aware of an approaching train and could be hit, leading to fatal consequences.
In conclusion, while these hobbies can be exciting and rewarding, it's crucial for enthusiasts to take proper safety precautions and be well - trained to minimize the risks.

4. Which hobbies are the least expensive?
   Which hobbies are the most expensive?
> Words may be used:
> reading
> cooking
> sailing
> collection
> time-consuming
> ---------------- 
## Least expensive hobbies
### Reading
Reading is one of the least expensive hobbies. You can borrow books from the local library for free. There are also many free e - books available online. Even if you choose to buy books, there are often second - hand bookstores where you can get them at a very low cost. It doesn't require any special equipment other than a place to sit and read, and it can be a very time - consuming and fulfilling activity.
### Cooking
Cooking can be relatively inexpensive. You can start with basic ingredients from the grocery store, which don't cost a fortune. You can find many free recipes online or in community cookbooks. You may need some basic kitchen utensils, but these are usually one - time purchases that can last a long time. You can also grow some of your own herbs at home, which further reduces the cost.

## Most expensive hobbies
### Sailing
Sailing is a very expensive hobby. Buying a sailboat can cost a significant amount of money, ranging from tens of thousands to millions of dollars depending on the size and type of the boat. In addition to the purchase price, there are ongoing costs such as maintenance, storage, insurance, and fuel (if it's a motor - assisted sailboat). You also need to invest in safety equipment and training courses, which can add up quickly.
Collection (certain types)
Some types of collections can be extremely expensive. For example, collecting rare coins, stamps, or art pieces. Rare coins and stamps can be very valuable, and building a high - quality collection may require spending large sums of money on individual items. Art collections can be even more costly, with famous paintings or sculptures selling for millions of dollars. The cost also includes proper storage and authentication services for these valuable items.


In summary, reading and cooking are among the least expensive hobbies, while sailing and certain types of collections are some of the most expensive ones.

5. Why do people need hobbies?
> Words may be used:
> work/life
> boring
> confidence
> friends
> relax
> ----------------

People need hobbies for several important reasons that greatly enhance their work - life balance and overall well - being.
Firstly, hobbies are a wonderful way to relax. In today's fast - paced world, work can be extremely stressful. After a long day at the office or dealing with various work - related pressures, engaging in a hobby allows people to unwind. For example, someone who loves painting can lose themselves in the colors and brushstrokes, forgetting about the worries of work. It helps to lower stress levels and gives the mind a much - needed break from the daily grind.
Secondly, hobbies prevent life from becoming boring. Without hobbies, people's lives might revolve solely around work and basic daily tasks, which can be monotonous. A hobby adds an element of fun and excitement. Whether it's playing a musical instrument, gardening, or doing sports, hobbies introduce new experiences and challenges. They keep life interesting and give people something to look forward to outside of work.
Hobbies also play a crucial role in building confidence. When people engage in a hobby, they have the opportunity to learn new skills and improve over time. For instance, if someone takes up photography, they start by learning the basics and gradually develop their skills to take high - quality pictures. As they see their progress, it boosts their self - confidence. This new - found confidence can then spill over into other areas of their life, including their work.
In addition, hobbies can help people make friends. Many hobbies have associated communities or groups. For example, joining a book club for a reading hobby or a running group for a fitness hobby. These groups provide a platform for like - minded people to meet and interact. Making friends through hobbies not only enriches social life but also provides a support network. People can share their experiences, exchange tips, and have a great time together.
In conclusion, hobbies are essential for a well - rounded life. They help people relax, add excitement to their daily routine, build confidence, and expand their social circle, thus improving the overall quality of work and life.
